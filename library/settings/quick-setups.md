# Quick set-ups: TRICK, SCUBA and SNORKEL presets

Save three presets once, at home, and switching the camera to the right
set-up becomes a button press. **Do this before the trip**, and test each
one (see the [practice drills](../shots/practice-drills.md)).

!!! note "Menu names"
    These steps come from Insta360's manual and a September 2025 X5 guide.
    Firmware updates sometimes rename buttons. If yours look different,
    note what they say and tell Claude, so this page can be fixed.

## How presets work on the X5
- **Open the preset list:** press the **Q button** (the small button below
  the power button). Each press moves to the next preset. You can also swipe
  down on the screen and tap **Preset**.
- **Add a preset:** first set the camera exactly how you want it (mode,
  resolution and so on). Then press **Q** until you reach the last icon, a
  **plus (+)**, and tap it. Check the settings, **rename it** (TRICK, SCUBA,
  SNORKEL), and tap the **tick** to save.
- **Edit one:** press **Q**. At the bottom of the screen, next to the
  settings line, tap the small blue **edit** button.
- **Delete one:** edit it, then tap the **bin** icon (top right).
- **Leave a preset:** just pick another mode.
- **Check the Q button is set to Presets** (it is by default): swipe down ›
  cog **Settings** › **Customize Button** › **Quick button** › **Presets**.

**Test once:** turn the camera off and on again and check your presets are
still there with the right settings.

## 1. TRICK (for Shot Lab templates and trick shots)
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Mode | **Video** (standard Video, not PureVideo, Timelapse or TimeShift) |
| Resolution | **5.7K30** |
| Everything else | Auto |

**Also:** turn **Pre-Recording** on (swipe down › **Pre-Recording**, pick 30–90
seconds). It's a separate switch, not part of the preset, and stays on until
you turn it off.

**Why:** every Shot Lab template (Spin Me Around, Street Lapse, Clone Trail)
accepts 5.7K Video. 8K, PureVideo and Timelapse-mode clips may be refused.

**Use it for:** everything on the [Trick Shot Cards](../shots/trick-shot-cards.md).

## 2. SCUBA (inside the Dive Case Pro)
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Mode | **Video** |
| Resolution | **5.7K30**. It saves battery, and one charge has to last the whole dive |
| EV | Auto |
| White balance | Auto. Fix the colour afterwards with AquaVision in the app; there's no underwater colour setting on the camera |

**The night before a dive** (at the hotel, dry hands):
1. Lens guards **off**.
2. Battery at 100%. You can't swap it inside the case.
3. Press **Q** and select **SCUBA**.
4. Turn on **Dive Case Mode**: swipe down › **Accessories** › **Dive Case** ›
   **Enable Immediately**, or **long-press the Q button**. This changes the
   stitching for water. The X5 can also detect the Dive Case Pro by itself,
   but turn it on anyway.
5. Put in a fresh anti-fog insert and seal the case. See
   [Scuba Dive](../shots/scuba-dive.md).

**Inside the case, only two buttons work:**
- **Q button:** one press starts or stops recording.
- **Power button:** long press turns it on or off. A single press changes
  mode.
- **Everything else is locked**, so set it all before you seal the case.

Afterwards, take the camera out and **swipe up** on the screen to leave
Dive Case Mode.

## 3. SNORKEL (bare camera, no case)
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Mode | **Video** |
| Resolution | **5.7K30** |
| EV | Auto |
| White balance | Auto. AquaVision in the app afterwards |

**Also:** turn on **Gesture Control** (swipe down › **Gesture Control**). An
open palm towards the camera starts or stops recording, which works when
wet fingers won't work the screen. The **shutter button** always works.

**Before you go in (every time):**
- [ ] **Lens guards off.** Insta360 says they aren't waterproof.
- [ ] **Battery door and USB door shut, with no orange showing.** Glance at
  the seals first: no sand, hair or salt.
- [ ] Wrist strap on, through a D-ring in the stick's bottom.
- [ ] Press record **before** you get in, and leave it rolling.
- [ ] Walk or slide in. **Don't jump in holding it.**

**After:** rinse it in fresh water with the doors **closed**, then let it
**soak in fresh water for 5–10 minutes**, towel it off and let it air dry.
Only open the doors once the outside is dry. No hair dryer. If the
microphone sounds muffled afterwards, that's normal and clears in about a
day.

**One honest trade-off:** Insta360 says the bare X5 underwater **may show
stitching problems in 360**, because water bends light differently at the
lenses. That's why it recommends the dive case for underwater shots. For
snorkelling, where you're in and out of the water and want good shots on
the surface, the bare camera is still the better choice. Underwater:
- keep fish and faces **in front of a lens**, not at the sides where the
  two lenses join;
- hold the camera still and let things come to you.

See [Snorkelling & surface water](snorkelling-and-water.md).
