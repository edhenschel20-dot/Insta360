# Sunset and golden hour

Treat this as a two-phase shoot: bright, high-contrast light while the sun's
up, then a switch to night settings once it's properly dark.

## Settings
| | While it's still bright | After the sun's down |
|---|---|---|
| Mode | Standard Video, Active HDR **on** | PureVideo |
| Res/FPS | 5.7K60 or 8K30 (Active HDR is safest at 5.7K60) | 4K or 5.7K30 |
| EV | −0.7 to −1.3 for a silhouette look | −0.3 to −0.7 |
| White balance | Auto (or push warmer manually if you want) | Manual 3200–4500K |
| Colour | Standard | Standard |

## Tips
- For a classic silhouette (subject as a dark shape against a bright sky),
  the negative EV is deliberate — you're exposing for the sky, not the
  foreground.
- Active HDR is built for bright, high-contrast light. Leaving it on into
  full darkness fights the wrong problem — switch to PureVideo instead.
- A separate Timelapse clip is worth doing alongside your handheld shots if
  you want the "watch the whole sky change" effect — see the
  [timelapse page](timelapse-and-timeshift.md).
