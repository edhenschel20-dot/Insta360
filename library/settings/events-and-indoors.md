# Events and indoors

Parties, dinners, family gatherings — anywhere indoors with mixed or warm
light.

## Settings
| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | PureVideo for dim rooms/evening light, standard Video for bright daytime venues |
| Res/FPS | 5.7K30–60 |
| EV | Auto normally; −0.3 if the room has bright point sources (candles, fairy lights, a spotlit cake) |
| ISO | Manual 400–800 if the camera's on a tripod/table; Auto capped ~1600 if handheld |
| White balance | Manual ~3000–4000K for warm bulbs (candlelight, tungsten), or Auto if the room mixes daylight and artificial light |
| Colour | Standard |
| Sharpness | Medium |

## Tips
- Planting the stick on a table or shelf beats handheling through a whole
  party — it's less tiring, removes shake as an issue, and works well with
  [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) for finding whoever's active in the
  frame later.
- Leaving white balance on Auto under warm bulb light often leaves an
  orange cast Auto doesn't fully correct — worth setting manually if you
  know the room's light won't change.
