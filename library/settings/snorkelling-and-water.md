# Snorkelling and surface water

For casual water use — snorkelling, beach days, kayaking, pool — where you
don't need the dive case. See [Diving](underwater-diving.md) for the case
setup.

## Settings
| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | Video, no case needed |
| Res/FPS | 5.7K30 |
| EV | Auto |
| ISO | Auto |
| White balance | Auto — fix the blue cast afterwards with AquaVision 2.0 in the app |
| Colour | Standard |
| Sharpness | Medium |

## Tips
- The X5's native waterproof rating is reportedly 15 m (check the current
  manual before relying on it near that limit) — comfortably past
  snorkelling depths. No housing needed.
- This is one water scenario where "just let it run" genuinely works —
  don't waste effort trying to aim the camera at fish or a moving subject.
  Swim with it, dunk it under, spin it around; frame it later.
- A short stick or wrist strap is enough at the surface. Save the dive case
  for real depth.
