# Photos (360 stills)

PureShot is the standard photo processing pipeline on the X5 now — you're
generally shooting with it rather than choosing to enable it.

## Settings
| | Casual / social | Virtual tour / max quality |
|---|---|---|
| Mode | Photo, PureShot | Photo, PureShot + HDR **on** |
| Resolution | 72MP | 72MP, + RAW backup |
| HDR | Off | On (blends 3 exposures) |
| Timer | 3 seconds (avoids shake) | Tripod + timer/remote |
| EV | Auto | Auto, nudge negative in bright/high-contrast scenes |
| White balance | Auto | Auto |

## Tips
- Use HDR only on a tripod or otherwise perfectly still shot — a 3-exposure
  blend needs the camera not to move between shots, or you get ghosting.
- Don't shoot RAW for casual social shots — it just creates editing work for
  files that were never going to be graded.
- Don't stack extra manual sharpness on top of PureShot — it already
  sharpens at capture.
- **HDR+RAW vs AEB:** Ben Claremont's pick for sunsets and bright beaches is
  HDR photo on + PureShot + RAW at 72 MP, with Studio merging the brackets
  automatically. AEB (Auto Exposure Bracketing, 3–7 frames, up to 4 stops
  apart for 3 frames or 1.3 stops for 7) gives more manual control but is
  capped at 18 MP — lower resolution than the HDR+RAW route.
- **Sharing 360 photos as 360** (not a flat crop): Facebook, Google Photos
  and Panorra all support genuine 360 viewing on upload — check the export
  is tagged as a 360 photo before you post, or it'll display flat.
