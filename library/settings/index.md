# Quick settings

At-a-glance table. Tap a scenario for the full page with tips and sources.
Auto is fine for anything not listed here — these are just the scenarios
worth a deliberate change.

| Scenario | Mode | Res/FPS | EV | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Night & low light](night-and-low-light.md) | PureVideo | 4K or 5.7K 30fps | −0.3 to −0.7 | Manual WB 3200–4500K |
| [Night & low light](night-and-low-light.md) (city lights) | PureVideo | 5.7K30 | −0.7 | WB 3200–3500K |
| [Bright sun & snow](bright-sun-and-snow.md) | Video | 5.7K60 or 8K30 | −0.3 to −1.0 | ND filter optional |
| [Sunset](sunset.md) | Video, Active HDR on | 5.7K60 or 8K30 | −0.7 to −1.3 for silhouette | Switch to PureVideo once dark |
| [Diving](underwater-diving.md) | Video, Dive Case Mode ON | 5.7K30 | Auto (+0.3–0.7 if murky) | AquaVision to fix colour after |
| [Snorkelling & water](snorkelling-and-water.md) | Video | 5.7K30 | Auto | No case needed under 15 m |
| [Fast motion & driving](fast-motion-and-driving.md) (car) | Video / Road Mode | 5.7K30 | Auto | PureVideo after dark |
| [Fast motion & driving](fast-motion-and-driving.md) (biking, full 360) | Video | 5.7K30 or 4K60 | Auto | Active HDR in dappled shade |
| [Events & indoors](events-and-indoors.md) | PureVideo or Video | 5.7K30–60 | Auto or −0.3 | WB 3000–4000K for warm bulbs |
| [Photos](photos.md) (social) | Photo, PureShot | 72MP | Auto | HDR off, 3 s timer |
| [Photos](photos.md) (quality/static) | Photo, PureShot + HDR | 72MP | Auto | Tripod, RAW backup |
| [Timelapse & TimeShift](timelapse-and-timeshift.md) (static scene) | Timelapse / Interval | Manual shutter | 0 | Tripod mandatory |
| [Timelapse & TimeShift](timelapse-and-timeshift.md) (you're moving) | TimeShift | Auto or up to 60x | Auto | Zero reframe work after |
| [Bullet time & slow motion](bullet-time-and-slow-motion.md) | Bullet Time | 5.7K120 | Auto | Needs good light |
| [Bullet time & slow motion](bullet-time-and-slow-motion.md) (slow-mo) | Video | 4K100/120 | Auto | Faster shutter if manual |

!!! tip
    Standard colour profile and Medium sharpness are the right default almost
    everywhere. White balance Auto is fine unless a page below says
    otherwise.
