# Trips

Each trip page is a short activity-by-activity plan — a kit list, a
storage/battery plan, and settings for what you'll actually be doing. The
detail always lives on the general pages under
[settings](../settings/index.md), [editing](../editing/index.md), and gear
(see [storage and batteries](../gear/storage-and-batteries.md) and
[memory cards](../gear/memory-cards.md)); trip pages just link to them so
nothing is duplicated or goes stale in two places.

Use a trip page as your pre-departure checklist, then fall back to the
general pages once you're there and need the full how-to.
