# Maui day by day

A sample 17-day plan showing **which shots to get on which day**, and which
step-by-step page to open that morning. It's a template: **swap the days
round to match your real bookings.** The shot lists move with the activity,
not the date.

Three rules for ordering the days:

1. **Diving and altitude:** keep the Haleakalā summit sunrise (10,000 ft), the
   helicopter flight and the flight home **at least 24 hours after any
   dive.** Check the exact wait with your dive shop.
2. **Easy day after a big day.** Road to Hana and dive days fill a card
   and flatten a battery. A beach day after gives you time to back up and
   recharge.
3. **Road to Hana early in the trip.** If the weather's bad, you have time
   to move it.

**Tricks:** the last column is optional. Do it only when the family is
lingering anyway. How-tos are on the [Trick Shot Cards](../shots/trick-shot-cards.md).

## Every evening (10 minutes)
- [ ] Download today's 3–5 best clips to the phone. Never wipe a card, and put full cards in the safe.
- [ ] Charge all batteries and the power bank.
- [ ] Wipe the lenses and rinse anything that touched salt water.
- [ ] Look at the plan for tomorrow and set the camera mode now, not in the morning.

See [Handling footage while travelling](../gear/data-while-travelling.md).

## Shot list by day type

### Road to Hana day
1. TimeShift on the windscreen for the twisty stretch (passenger starts it).
2. Planted stick at Twin Falls and Wailua Falls: falls on one lens, family on the other.
3. Black sand tiny planet at Waiʻānapanapa (EV −0.7 to −1.0). **Book a slot.**
4. Bamboo forest: camera low and pointed up, slow walk, 20 seconds standing still for the sound.
5. Rainbows: say "mark that".

### Dive days
1. Seal the case at the hotel. Gear up at the car without recording.
2. Press record at the water's edge. Clip the camera on through the waves, keeping both hands free.
3. Surface swim: half-in, half-out shot with the beach behind.
4. Camera ahead and above you, held sideways. Turn your body, not your wrist. Short stick in surge.
5. Clip it back on before the surf exit, and leave it recording.
6. That evening: AquaVision, then trim to your hand signals.

### Beach days
1. Walking clip at kid height along the waterline.
2. Overhead "drone" walk-away (stick straight up).
3. Planted tiny planet with the kids running round it.
4. Sunset silhouette.

### Town walks
1. Chest-height walking clip, lenses along the street.
2. One set piece: look-up under a tree, street lapse, or mural pivot.
3. Same pose in front of each town's sign, for an end-of-trip montage.
4. In Lahaina, film the open businesses, not the damage.

### Dinner
1. Ask the host. Then walk-in reveal and a 10-second still for the ceiling.
2. A 360 HDR photo of the room.
3. Camera in the middle of the table above the glasses, rolling until dessert.

### Waterfalls and paths
1. Chest-height walking on the path. Kid height when they're ahead.
2. Reveal walk for the last 20 metres before the falls.
3. Planted stick at the falls for 3–5 minutes.
4. Put it away in heavy rain.

## End-of-trip film
Collect one 10–15 second piece from each day as you go (the "must-get" column).
On the flight home, string them in order, add [Beat Sync](../editing/beat-sync-to-music.md)
to one song, and add the "same pose, many towns" montage at the end. See
[Fast Holiday Reel](../editing/fast-holiday-reel.md).
