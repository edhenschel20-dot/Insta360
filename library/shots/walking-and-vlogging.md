# Walking & Vlogging

You're the subject: talking to camera, wandering somewhere, or just narrating what's happening. Let it roll — the edit sorts out the framing later.

## Setup (once)
- Invisible selfie stick, chest-to-head height, arm out 60–90 cm in front and slightly to the side so your face isn't sitting on the stitch line.
- Pick which lens is "front" and stay facing it for the session — don't swing the stick around to check different directions.
- Turn on Horizon Lock so the world stays level even if the stick tilts.

## While you're enjoying it
- Nothing to do. If something worth finding later happens, say "mark" or tap the flag — that's it.

## Edit it fast
- If you shot in InstaFrame, you may already have a ready flat video alongside the 360 file — check that first before reframing anything.
- Otherwise, [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) locked on your face is the fastest one-pass option, or steer it live with [Quick Edit](../editing/quick-edit-steer-by-phone.md).

## Effects that work here
- [Rotating Reveal](../effects/rotating-reveal.md) — swing round to reveal what's behind you.
- [360 Look-Around (Slow Pan)](../effects/look-around-pan.md) — a slow scan of the surroundings.
- [Invisible Selfie Stick Shot](../effects/invisible-selfie-stick.md) — the base technique underneath all of this.

## Settings
See [Settings index](../settings/index.md) for the full picture; walking vlogging doesn't have its own dedicated settings page, so pick between [Bright sun & snow](../settings/bright-sun-and-snow.md), [Sunset](../settings/sunset.md) or [Night & low light](../settings/night-and-low-light.md) depending on the light you're in.

## Walking with kids
Hold the camera at their eye height rather than over your head — see
[Kid-Height Invisible Cinematographer](../effects/kid-height-invisible-cinematographer.md).
