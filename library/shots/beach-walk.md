# Beach walk

A walk along the sand with the family: morning at Kaanapali or Wailea, a
golden-hour stroll, or the black sand at Waiʻānapanapa on the Road to Hana.
The plan: **stick at kid height, let it roll, and plant it for one or two
"set pieces"** when everyone stops anyway.

## Before you leave (2 minutes)
- [ ] **Wipe both lenses.** Sunscreen fingerprints are the number one beach
  problem, so wipe them after you've put sunscreen on, not before.
- [ ] Battery half-full or more. A walk is an hour at most.
- [ ] Bring the stick with its mini tripod screwed on, and a ground stake if
  you have one (for sand).

## Settings (set once)
| | |
|---|---|
| Daytime | **Video 5.7K30**, EV **−0.3** (bright sand and water fool the meter) |
| Golden hour / sunset | Video 5.7K30, EV **−0.7**, or PureVideo once the sun is down |
| Black sand | EV **−0.7 to −1.0** to keep the sand black and the foam white. See [Black & Red Sand Beach](../effects/black-red-sand-beach.md) |
| White balance | Auto |

See [Bright sun](../settings/bright-sun-and-snow.md) and [Sunset](../settings/sunset.md).

## Where to stand and how to hold it
- **Walking:** hold the stick out to the side and slightly ahead, **at about
  the kids' head height**, with the lenses facing along the beach (sea on one
  side, land on the other). That keeps the shoreline off the stitch seam.
  See [Kid-Height Invisible Cinematographer](../effects/kid-height-invisible-cinematographer.md).
- **Walk on the wet, firm sand near the waterline.** The waves come into
  shot and the footprints show behind you.
- **Keep salt spray off:** hold the stick on the land side when waves are
  breaking.

## While you're enjoying it
- Press record at the start of the walk. Say **"Mark that"** when the kids
  find a shell or a crab, or a turtle hauls out.
- **Turtles on the sand:** stay at least 3 m (10 ft) back. The 360 lets you
  reframe close later, so you don't need to walk up.

## Two set pieces (one minute each, when you stop anyway)
1. **The overhead "drone" walk-away:** extend the stick fully straight up
   over your head while the family walks ahead along the waterline. It looks
   like a drone following you. See [Fake Drone Shot](../effects/fake-drone-shot.md).
2. **Footprints tiny planet:** plant the stick in the sand (stake or tripod)
   at the waterline and let the kids run a lap round it, or walk away and
   come back. See [Walking Tiny Planet](../effects/walking-tiny-planet.md)
   and [Legless Tiny Planet](../effects/legless-tiny-planet-ground-stake.md).

## Optional extras
- **Sunset silhouette:** at sunset, everyone stands between the camera and
  the sun. EV −1.0. See [Sunset Silhouette](../effects/sunset-silhouette.md).
- **Wave splash:** hold the camera low at the waterline as a small wave
  washes past. Rinse the lens with fresh water afterwards. See
  [Splash Transition](../effects/splash-transition.md).
- **Tide pools:** half-dip the camera into a rock pool. See
  [Tide Pool Dip](../effects/tide-pool-dip.md) (snorkel-case or housing advice is there).
- **Base camp:** while you're lying on the towels, plant it and let it
  run. See [Beach Base Camp Cam](../effects/beach-base-camp-cam.md).

## Edit it fast
1. Use a **fixed view pointed along the beach** for the walking stretch.
   It's quick and looks steady. See [Split & Fixed Views](../editing/split-and-fixed-views.md).
2. Use **Deep Track on a child** for any stretch where they run ahead. See
   [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md).
3. Use **Tiny planet** for the set piece. See [Tiny Planet](../editing/tiny-planet.md).
4. AI Edit is fine here, as long as you trim any long stretches it
   spends on you holding the stick.

## Tips
- Early morning is calmer, with fewer people and softer light. Late afternoon
  gives the golden glow.
- Don't wipe a salty lens dry. Rinse it with fresh water from a water bottle
  first, then dry it.
- Don't leave the camera baking in the sun in a bag. The X5 can overheat in
  direct Maui sun while recording, so give it shade between clips.
