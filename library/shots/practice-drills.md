# Practice drills (do these at home before the trip)

A trick you've done once in the yard is one you'll remember on the beach.
Each drill takes about 10 minutes and has a **"you've got it if…"** test.
Do them over a couple of evenings. The kids make great test subjects.

Write down anything that looks different from this guide (a button with
another name, a template that refuses a clip) and send it to Claude, so the
guide can be fixed before you go.

## Drill 1: set up the TRICK preset (5 min)
1. On the camera: Video mode, **5.7K30**, **Pre-Recording on** (choose 30–90
   seconds).
2. Swipe down › **Preset** › save it as a new preset. Call it TRICK if the
   camera lets you name it.
3. Also check your everyday preset is still there for normal filming.

**You've got it if:** you can switch to TRICK and back with one swipe, in
under 5 seconds, without looking at this page.

## Drill 2: calibrate the gyro (2 min)
Every Shot Lab tutorial says to do this if you see odd rotation or shaking.
Do it once now, and again after any drop. Find **gyro calibration** in the
camera's settings (or the app's camera settings), then set the camera on a
flat, still surface and follow the prompt.

**You've got it if:** it says calibration is complete. Note where you found
the setting, because the menu path isn't in this guide yet.

## Drill 3: the signature spin, three places (15 min)
1. Label the 114 cm stick: *TRICK preset · full length · slow clockwise spin
   · 30 s · still 5 s each end.*
2. Do the spin in three different spots: the front yard, the back yard, the
   end of the street.
3. That evening: Edit › Shot Lab › **Spin Me Around** › pick all three ›
   export.
4. **Test the Android question:** record one extra spin in **8K** and one in
   **PureVideo**, and see whether Spin Me Around will accept them. (The
   Shot Lab table suggests not on Android.)

**You've got it if:** the three spots cut together cleanly, and you know
whether 8K and PureVideo clips work in the template.

## Drill 4: Street Lapse at a corner (10 min)
1. Stand the 2-in-1 on a pavement with some traffic or people.
2. Clip 1: stand still beside it for 20 s, wearing a light-coloured top.
3. Clip 2: walk off and let it record 2 min. **Don't touch the camera
   between clips.**
4. Edit › Shot Lab › **Street Lapse**.

**You've got it if:** you're frozen and sharp while everything else rushes
past.

## Drill 5: Clone Trail with the kids (10 min)
1. 2-in-1 standing in the yard.
2. One kid walks or jogs round it, **about 2 m away, steady speed**, doing
   a new pose every few steps, for 30–60 s.
3. Edit › Shot Lab › **Clone Trail**. Keep the app open while it
   recognises them.

**You've got it if:** you get a clean trail of copies. If not, try a wider
circle or a steadier pace.

## Drill 6: crowd flicker and vanishing person (10 min)
1. **Flicker:** SmallRig mini tripod on a wall. Timelapse mode, 1 s
   interval, 5 min, somewhere people or cars pass.
2. **Vanish:** any normal walking clip. Speed it up 8–16× and use Trim to cut
   out a second as someone passes.

**You've got it if:** people flicker in the first clip, and someone
disappears in the second. See
[Things Appear & Disappear](../effects/things-appear-and-disappear.md).

## Drill 7: Spatial Capture of one room (15 min, plus 1–2 h waiting)
1. Clear the living room of people and pets.
2. 114 cm stick overhead, slow back-and-forth passes no more than 2 m apart,
   then chest height round the sofa. **Under 2 minutes.**
3. Album › **Spatial Capture** › **Create** › pick it › **Generate**.
4. When it's ready, explore it and try making a fly-through video.

This uses one of your 4 free goes this month. You get 4 more in December
for the trip.

**You've got it if:** you can move round your living room on the phone, and
you've seen how anything that moved came out. See
[Spatial Capture](../effects/spatial-capture-3d-scene.md).

## Drill 8: gear checks (15 min)
- [ ] **Cart mount:** strap the 114 cm stick to a post or fence the way you
  will on the cart, then shake it hard. See [Golf Cart Mount](../gear/golf-cart-mount.md).
- [ ] **2-in-1 in the wind:** fully extended in the yard on a breezy day.
  Find out how far you can extend it before it wobbles or tips.
- [ ] **Dive weight:** screw the weight block, case and 114 cm stick
  together. Check it's tight. Screw a D-ring into the weight.
- [ ] **Lens swap:** do one practice swap indoors, then film a test clip.
- [ ] **Battery swap:** time yourself. It should take under a minute.
