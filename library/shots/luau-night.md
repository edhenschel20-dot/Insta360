# Luau night

A seated night show with torches, fire knives and a lit stage — filmed from
your table without ever leaving your family to go "get the shot." **Set it
up once, let it roll, watch the show.**

## Before you leave (5 minutes)
- [ ] **Charge the battery** — this is a full evening, seated, with no easy
  swap once the show starts.
- [ ] **Check card space** for a couple of hours at 5.7K.
- [ ] **Wipe both lenses.** Torches and stage lights plus a smudge give big
  glowing halos.
- [ ] Bring the mini tripod or use the stick's own stand. Check ahead
  whether the venue allows selfie sticks at your seats — some don't; see
  the Optional extras below for a no-stick backup.

## Settings (set once, before it starts)
| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | **PureVideo** |
| Resolution | **5.7K 30fps** (or 8K 30fps) |
| EV | **−0.7 to −1** to start |
| White balance | Auto, or manual ~3000–4000K once the stage lighting settles |
| Colour | Standard |

Why negative EV: torches and fire knives are small bright spots on a dark
stage, and negative EV stops them blowing out into white blobs. See
[Night & low light](../settings/night-and-low-light.md) and
[Events & indoors](../settings/events-and-indoors.md).

## Where to stand and how to hold it
- **Table edge, lenses facing the stage, not side-on.** That keeps the
  performers off the stitch seam.
- **Best option: the stick planted on the mini tripod at the table edge**,
  raised to about chest-to-head height so it doesn't block anyone behind
  you.
- **Handheld option:** held at chest height, braced against the table.
  Don't wave it around — a still camera against moving torches gives sharp
  performers and nice light streaks; a moving camera in the dark gives
  blur.

## While you're enjoying it
- **Press record before the show starts, and let it roll.**
- Say **"Mark that"** at the fire-knife section, or when your kids light up
  at something.
- That's it. Watch the show.

## Optional extras (only if you feel like it)
- **Tiny planet photo** of the lawn or stage area at dusk, before the show
  starts, while there's still some light in the sky.
- **No-stick backup:** if sticks aren't allowed at your seats, the
  [Foldable Utility Handle](../effects/foldable-utility-handle.md) or a
  short handheld grip works fine at chest height — you lose a little
  reach, not the shot.

## Edit it fast (tomorrow)
1. **Skip AI Edit and Auto Frame.** They'll swing round to faces in the
   crowd instead of the stage.
2. **Fastest:** one fixed view pointed at the stage, then export. See
   [Split & Fixed Views](../editing/split-and-fixed-views.md).
3. **Want the kids' reactions too?** Use
   [Multi-View](../editing/multi-view.md) for the stage and the table side
   by side, or split the clip where they react and point that piece at
   them.
4. **Follow the fire knife** with [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md).

## Tips
- Full dark is fine here (unlike a parade) — the stage lighting is doing
  the work, and the flame stays interesting all evening.
- This is the Lighted Parade plan applied to a stage: same "set it once,
  let it roll" idea, same night settings.
