# Dinner in a beautiful room

A special dinner somewhere where the building is half the point: a
high-ceilinged hotel dining room, an open-sided lanai restaurant, or a
grand lobby at sunset. Two things to capture: **the room itself** (one
short shot on arrival) and **the table** (planted, rolling, forgotten).

## Before you leave (2 minutes)
- [ ] **Wipe both lenses.** Candles and lamps plus a smudge give glowing halos.
- [ ] Battery at least half full.
- [ ] Bring the **foldable handle or the short stick**, not the long one, and
  the mini tripod. A fancy room is the wrong place for a 3 m stick.
- [ ] **Book a table by the window or the lanai edge if there's a sunset
  view.** It does more for the footage than any setting.

## Settings
| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | **PureVideo** (the evening light in the room is the whole look) |
| Resolution | 5.7K30 (8K30 if the camera is planted and you want extra sharpness) |
| EV | **−0.3** (candles, chandeliers and lit glasses are bright points) |
| White balance | **Manual ~3500K** for warm lamps or candles. Auto if the room is open to the sunset |
| Room shot only | A 360 **photo in HDR** gives the most detail on the ceiling and windows. See [HDR photo recipe](../effects/hdr-aeb-photo-recipe.md) |

Full reference: [Events & indoors](../settings/events-and-indoors.md).

## Shot 1: the room (on arrival, about 1 minute)
1. **Walk-in reveal:** start recording outside the entrance with the camera
   just behind your shoulder, then walk in at a normal pace. In the edit,
   the view swings up over you onto the room. See
   [Up-and-Over Reveal](../effects/up-and-over-reveal.md).
2. **Look up:** stop in the middle of the room (or by the host stand) and
   hold the camera still at head height for 10 seconds. Later you can tilt
   the view up to the ceiling. 360 handles big ceilings better than any
   phone.
3. **One 360 photo** of the room from the same spot. It gives a lovely tiny
   planet or a "drag-around" photo to send to family.

Do this before you're seated, while there's a natural pause. **Ask the
host if filming is OK.** Most places are fine with a quick, quiet shot, but
some ask for no cameras in the dining room.

## Shot 2: the table (planted, about 1 hour)
- **Put the camera in the middle of the table** on the mini tripod or
  standing on the short stick, **raised just above the glasses and the
  candle**, so nothing tall blocks one lens.
- **Lenses facing along the table** (one lens towards each end) so nobody's
  face sits on the stitch seam.
- Press record and **forget it**. Do it while drinks are ordered and stop it
  after dessert. Say **"Mark that"** for the toast, the birthday candle or the
  kids' faces when the dessert arrives.
- If there's a window or sunset behind the table, the 360 catches both the
  family and the view at once.

## Optional extras
- **Toast in slow motion:** for the clink, switch the Q button to a slow-mo
  preset for that one moment. Only do this if the room is well lit. Slow-mo
  in dim light gets noisy.
- **Curtain or door fade:** if there are big doors or curtains to the lanai,
  push the camera through them for a natural transition. See
  [Curtain Reveal](../effects/curtain-reveal-natural-fade.md) and
  [Swim-Through Doorway / Arch](../effects/swim-through-doorway-arch-transition.md).
- **Sunset on the way out:** if you're leaving at sunset, do a
  [Sunset Silhouette](../effects/sunset-silhouette.md) on the terrace.

## Edit it fast
1. **Room:** keyframe a slow tilt from the floor up to the ceiling or
   chandelier. That's two keyframes with easing. See
   [Keyframes with Easing](../editing/keyframes-with-easing.md).
2. **Table:** [Multi-View](../editing/multi-view.md) with both ends of the table
   side by side is the fastest "everyone's in it" result. Or use a fixed view
   on the kids' end with short splits to whoever's talking.
3. AI Edit does fine at a dinner, because the people are the subject here.
   Just trim any long stretches of you.

## Tips
- Don't put the camera next to the candle. Moving flame on the seam looks
  odd, and it gets warm.
- No flash, no lights, and keep it quiet. The X5 doesn't need extra light in
  PureVideo.
- Waiters move fast around tables. Keep the stick short and inside your own
  table space.
