# Kapalua Plantation Course (hole by hole)

The round you most want to film. Only the **Plantation Course** is open; the
Bay Course is closed for now, so this is the one you'll play. It's the
famous one anyway: wide, hilly fairways that run down towards the ocean,
with Molokai across the channel.

The plan: **the camera rides above the cart roof all round**, and you add
just a few planted shots at the holes worth the extra minute. Everything
else is the same as the general [Golf Round](golf-round.md) plan.

## Before you leave (the night before)
- [ ] **Charge both batteries to 100%**, and bring a power bank and the
  battery charger. One battery lasts roughly 2¼ hours at 5.7K30, so you'll
  need the second one for the back nine. Swap at the turn and put the first
  one on charge.
- [ ] **Card space:** start with plenty free. Change to a fresh card at the
  turn if the first one is past half full.
- [ ] Pack the [cart mount](../gear/golf-cart-mount.md) kit (clamp, rubber
  pad, two straps, safety cord on a D-ring) **and** the **2-in-1 stick and
  tripod** for the tee shots. See [My Kit](../gear/my-kit.md).
- [ ] Something to weigh the tripod down: it's windy up there.

## The two set-ups you'll use
**On the cart (all round):** the **114 cm stick** strapped to the front roof post,
camera 30–50 cm (1–1.5 ft) above the roof, recording. This gives a "drone
following the cart" view of every hole without you doing anything. See
[Golf Cart Mount](../gear/golf-cart-mount.md).

**Planted beside the tee (at a few holes):** leave the 114 cm stick on the
cart. Unscrew the camera, screw it onto the **2-in-1 stick and tripod**,
open the legs and extend it upright. **Nobody holds it while
they swing.** Put it **a few metres (10–12 ft) to the side of the tee box,
slightly behind the tee markers**, well clear of the swing and the line the
ball takes off on. The 2-in-1 is about 1 m long, so the camera sits at
about head height, which is a good view of the
swing and the hole, and the stick doesn't show. (For a true "drone" height
you'd need a longer stick; see [Fake Drone Shot](../effects/fake-drone-shot.md).) Leave it recording while **everyone tees off in
turn**, then pick it up when the last ball is gone and screw the camera back onto
the cart stick.

!!! tip "Wind"
    The 2-in-1's legs are small for a stick that long, so it blows over easily. Hang your bag or a
    water bottle from the tripod, or only extend the stick part way. Or
    have whoever isn't hitting hold it upright, standing well back.

## Hole by hole
Only five stops need anything from you. On every other hole, just play.

| Hole | What it's like | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Long downhill par 4, ocean ahead | **Tee-box reveal:** planted beside the tee. In the edit, start on the golfer and turn slowly to the view down the hole. Wipe the lenses here first. |
| **2** | Par 3 | Planted beside the tee: the whole ball flight and the green in one clip. |
| **8** | Par 3, a favourite for photos | Planted beside the tee, the same as on 2. |
| **13** | Mountains behind, ocean ahead | Planted beside the tee for everyone's tee shots, for the flyover look. |
| **18** | The famous long downhill par 5 to the ocean, with Molokai across the water | The big one. See below. |

Par 3s are the easiest to plant at, because everyone's waiting at the tee
anyway. Add hole 11 (another par 3) if you're enjoying it.

### Hole 18: three shots
1. **Planted beside the tee.** Wipe the lenses, extend the stick fully if
   the wind allows, and record everyone's tee shots, with the fairway falling
   away towards the ocean.
2. **The cart ride down the hole.** Put the camera back on the cart and
   **check it's recording** before you set off. This is the clip you'll use
   most: the cart rolling down the 18th towards the water.
3. **The finish on the green.** Take it off the cart again and plant it
   beside the green, out of everyone's putting line. Record the last putts,
   the handshake and the hugs. That's the ending of your golf film.

## While you're enjoying it
- Say **"mark that"** after any good shot, a funny moment or a great view.
- **At the turn:** swap the battery, check the mount, re-tighten the straps
  and wipe the lenses.
- **Keep up with play.** If a group is waiting behind you, skip the planted
  shot. The cart camera is already recording.

## Edit it fast
1. Trim the cart ride down 18 first. That's your opener or your ending.
2. For the tee shots, use a [fixed view](../editing/split-and-fixed-views.md)
   on the golfer, then a slow turn to follow the ball down the hole.
3. Cart footage: a forward fixed view down the fairway. There are no faces
   above the roof, so the app's AI has nothing to swing round to.
4. Suggested order: hole 1 reveal, a cart montage, the par 3s, 13, then the
   18th tee, the ride down 18 and the green.

## Tips
- **Ask at the bag drop** about the cart mount and a camera on the tee.
- Check the course's current status and tee-time rules when you book.
  Hours and closures change.
- Afternoon light on the back nine is lovely, but it's windier.
