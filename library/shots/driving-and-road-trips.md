# Driving & Road Trips

Dashcam-style footage, scenic drives, the whole family in the car. The car does all the movement — you don't need to do anything once it's mounted.

## Setup (once)
- Suction or dash mount on the windshield or dash, lens axis along the direction of travel — one lens forward through the windscreen, one lens back into the cabin.
- Keep sessions to natural trip segments (a scenic stretch, not necessarily the entire multi-hour drive) so files stay manageable — this is about file size, not about stopping and restarting for editing convenience.

## While you're enjoying it
- Nothing to do — just ride. Say "mark" at a good view, a landmark, or something funny happening in the cabin.

## Edit it fast
- Default to a fixed forward view through the windscreen (see [Split & Fixed Views](../editing/split-and-fixed-views.md)), with an occasional glance back to the cabin as a secondary point of interest — roads don't need tracking.
- For long boring motorway stretches, treat them as hyperlapse material rather than reframing them by hand.

## Effects that work here
- [Hyperlapse / TimeShift](../effects/hyperlapse-and-timeshift.md) — long motorway stretches.
- [Split Screen / MultiView](../effects/split-screen-multiview.md) — the road ahead and the cabin at once.
