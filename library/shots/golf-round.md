# Golf round

A whole round, filmed with almost no fiddling: a range session the day
before to learn the camera positions, then the camera rolling in the bag
or planted at the tee for the round itself. **[general advice]** — still no
2025–26 tutorial exists about golf on a 360 camera; this plan is built from
non-360 swing-analysis coaching plus X5 golf examples with no narration.

## See it
No verified 360 golf tutorial exists. The Jewkes Clan's X5 rolling in the
golf bag is an example of the "let it roll" idea, not a how-to:
[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fwu1DGVxLfw).

## Before you leave (5 minutes)
- [ ] **Charge the battery** for a full round (allow 4+ hours).
- [ ] **Check card space** for a whole round at 5.7K.
- [ ] **Wipe both lenses.**
- [ ] Bring the mini tripod or short stick for range/tee positioning.

## Where to stand and how to hold it
- **The day before, on the range:** try the two coaching positions —
  **face-on** (camera at hand height, at right angles to the target line)
  and **down-the-line** (behind your hands, lens at hand height, pointing
  down the target line through your hands). A camera position that moves
  even a little changes the apparent swing plane, so plant it in the same
  spot each time you check it.
- **On the course:** plant the stick or a small tripod a few metres to the
  side of the tee, out of the swing path and sightlines, roughly
  waist-to-chest height. Never hold up play or wander into another group's
  line while setting up.
- **Between shots:** let the camera roll in the golf bag as you walk the
  fairway — the round films itself, and you reframe the swings out of it
  later.

## While you're enjoying it
- **Play normally.** Say **"Mark that"** before a swing you want to find
  easily later — a good drive, a tricky putt.
- Practice-range swing checks only; never mid-round.

## Optional extras (only if you feel like it)
- A flyover-style overhead look at the hole from the tee, using the
  overhead invisible-stick technique — see
  [Fake Drone Shot](../effects/fake-drone-shot.md).
- Wear light clothing if you want a slow-motion swing check — an all-dark
  outfit turns into a silhouette at high frame rates.

## Edit it fast
1. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) on the golfer covers most swings
   without hand keyframes.
2. For the swing itself, slow it down in the edit rather than planning
   slow motion live — see
   [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](../effects/slow-motion-tracked-run.md).
3. [Split & Fixed Views](../editing/split-and-fixed-views.md) works well
   for golf: a fixed view on the swing, a short second piece for the
   reaction or the view down the fairway.

## Tips
- Course etiquette first: a camera on a tripod near the tee, out of the
  way of the tee box and swing path, is the safest default.
- Sixteen inches of camera offset is enough to change the apparent swing
  plane in coaching video — don't expect a single 360 clip to substitute
  for a dedicated swing-analysis camera, but it's a good record of the
  round either way.
