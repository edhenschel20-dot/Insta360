# Summit sunrise

A sunrise-above-the-clouds timelapse from a cold, dark summit, without
frosted lenses or a snapped tripod. **[general advice]** — built from
winter-photography practice and a sunset-timelapse settings recipe, since
no summit-specific 360 tutorial exists.

## See it
No verified 360 tutorial exists for a summit sunrise timelapse
specifically. The settings recipe below is adapted from a sunset timelapse
guide: [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKNm8xc_50M).

## Before you leave (the night before)
- [ ] **Charge the battery fully**, and keep the spare in an inside jacket
  pocket — cold roughly halves battery life.
- [ ] **Gloves** — you'll be adjusting a cold camera in the dark for up to
  40 minutes before sunrise.
- [ ] **Wipe both lenses** before you start up.
- [ ] Bring a hand warmer to rubber-band to the camera body (under the
  mini-tripod head, not over a lens).
- [ ] Arrive earlier than you think you need to.

## Settings (set once, before you climb)
| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | **Timelapse** |
| Resolution | **11K** |
| Interval | **5 seconds** (2 seconds if you only have ~30 minutes) |
| Colour | Vivid |
| EV | **−0.3** |
| White balance | **Manual, 6500K** (Auto jumps around as the light changes) |
| Stabilisation | Low-light stabilisation off |

See [Timelapse & TimeShift settings](../settings/timelapse-and-timeshift.md)
and [Night Interval Timelapse](../effects/night-interval-timelapse-72mp.md)
for the decision between timelapse modes.

## Where to stand and how to hold it
- Plastic tripod and clamp parts can snap in the cold — extend and fold
  them gently, don't yank.
- Plant the tripod somewhere stable with a clear view east; start the
  timelapse **30 minutes before sunrise** and leave it. The camera builds
  the video itself.
- If frost forms on the lens, breathe on it gently and wipe with a cloth —
  never scrape it.

## While you're enjoying it
- **Nothing to do once it's running.** Watch the sunrise with your family
  instead of checking the camera.
- Say **"Mark that"** if something notable happens near the camera, though
  a timelapse mostly needs no marking.

## Optional extras (only if you feel like it)
- A slow manual pan from the clouds to your family at the end of the
  sequence — see [Keyframes with Easing](../editing/keyframes-with-easing.md).

## Edit it fast
1. Speed the clip to under 10 seconds.
2. One slow pan from the clouds to the family, using
   [Keyframes with Easing](../editing/keyframes-with-easing.md).
3. No tracking needed — this is a tripod-only shot from start to finish.

## Tips
- The camera does the hard part; your job is staying warm and not
  disturbing the tripod once it's running.
- Manual white balance matters more here than almost anywhere else in this
  guide — Auto visibly hunts as a sunrise changes colour.
