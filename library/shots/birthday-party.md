# Birthday party

Presents, candles, and the group all in one clip, without anyone holding a
phone. **[general advice]** — no 360-specific birthday tutorial exists;
this plan is the Family Events approach plus a couple of editing moves.

## See it
No verified 360 tutorial exists for a birthday party specifically. Raw
party uploads exist but have no how-to behind them, so this plan leans on
[Family Events](family-events.md) and general practice instead of a single
source video.

## Before you leave (5 minutes)
- [ ] **Charge the battery** for the whole event.
- [ ] **Wipe both lenses.**
- [ ] Bring the mini tripod or a shelf/table spot to plant the stick.

## Settings (set once, before the candles)
| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | PureVideo for dim rooms, standard Video for bright daytime |
| Resolution | 5.7K 30–60fps |
| EV | Auto normally; −0.3 if there's a spotlit cake or bright point sources |
| White balance | Manual ~3000–4000K for warm bulbs, or Auto if the room mixes daylight and artificial light |

See [Events & indoors settings](../settings/events-and-indoors.md).

## Where to stand and how to hold it
- **Plant the stick on a table, shelf, or its own mini tripod** rather
  than holding it — the X5's field of view means you don't need to be
  close to anyone.
- Let it run stationary through the whole party. A static camera turns the
  clip into "reframe wherever the interesting thing happens," which
  tracking handles well.

## While you're enjoying it
- **Press record before the candles or presents start, and let it roll.**
- Say **"Mark that"** at candles, toasts, or a good moment.
- Be part of it — you're always in the 360 capture, and that's fixed in
  the edit, not by staying out of shot.

## Optional extras (only if you feel like it)
- If the kids are up for it, six quick jumps around the garden make a
  ready-made [Split Jump](../effects/split-jump-giant-jump.md) sequence
  once you're back at the app.

## Edit it fast
1. **Present-opening, two angles:** split the clip at the unwrapping
   moment, point one segment at the person opening the gift and the next
   at the giver's reaction — see
   [Present-Opening Two-Angle](../effects/present-opening-two-angle.md), or
   [Split Screen / MultiView](../effects/split-screen-multiview.md) to do
   both at once instead of choosing.
2. [Time Freeze (Animated Photo)](../effects/time-freeze-animated-photo.md)
   at the candle-blowing or toast moment, as a flying-camera still.
3. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) on whoever's active — opening
   presents, blowing out candles — pausing and resuming it as the "story"
   moves to someone else.
4. Check [Auto Frame / AI Edit](../editing/auto-frame-and-ai-edit.md) as a
   first pass if you like, but don't trust its first cut blindly — it's
   known to prefer people holding the camera over the actual action.

## Tips
- This is one of the few situations where a static, planted camera
  genuinely beats a moving one.
- Say "mark" liberally — a birthday has several moments worth finding
  again, not just one.
