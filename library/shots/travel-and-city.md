# Travel & City

Markets, streets, architecture, plazas — walking through a place worth remembering.

## Setup (once)
- Handheld at chest/head height for slow walk-throughs, or the extended overhead stick for a landmark "hero shot."
- Pick a direction and walk it steadily; pause for a few seconds at anything worth a proper look rather than swinging the camera to it.

## While you're enjoying it
- Just walk and look around normally. Say "mark" wherever you'd want to find the moment again — a great doorway, a view, a street performer.

## Edit it fast
- Because you paused at points of interest, this is a clip type where a few manual keyframes are genuinely fast — one keyframe per pause, letting the app interpolate the walk between them. See [Keyframes with Easing](../editing/keyframes-with-easing.md).
- For long days of walking, use [Finding Moments in Long Clips](../editing/finding-moments-in-long-clips.md) rather than scrubbing everything by hand.

## Effects that work here
- [Look-Up Shot](../effects/look-up-shot.md) — towers and domes converging overhead as you walk under them.
- [Object Reveal / Push-Through](../effects/object-reveal-push-through.md) — walking through a gate or archway.
- [Tiny Planet & Inverted](../effects/tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) — plazas, staircases and symmetric architecture make great planets.
- [Ghost Town](../effects/ghost-town.md) — an empty version of a landmark, from a static timelapse.

## When you want a hero shot
- [Rise Shot (Crane)](../effects/rise-shot-crane.md) at a viewpoint, or [Fake Drone Shot](../effects/fake-drone-shot.md) over a plaza — both are deliberate, short, planned shots rather than something to try mid-walk.
