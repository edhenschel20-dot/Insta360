# Christmas morning

The camera is already rolling by the tree before the kids come down.
Nobody films — everybody opens presents. **Set it up the night before, let
it roll for the whole hour.**

## Before you leave (the night before)
- [ ] **Charge the battery fully** and have it ready — this is roughly an
  hour rolling, first thing in the morning.
- [ ] **Check card space** for a full hour at 5.7K30.
- [ ] **Wipe both lenses.**
- [ ] Set up the stick and mini tripod beside the tree before you go to
  bed, so nothing needs doing in front of sleepy kids.

## Settings (set the night before)
| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | **PureVideo** (indoor lights) |
| Resolution | **5.7K 30fps** |
| EV | **−0.3** |
| White balance | Auto |

See [Events & indoors settings](../settings/events-and-indoors.md).

## Where to stand and how to hold it
- Stick and mini tripod beside the tree, at sitting height, lenses toward
  the sofa and the pile of presents.
- Use [Hands-Off Triggers](../effects/hands-off-triggers.md) — Timed
  Capture, or Pre-recording plus a peace sign — so the camera starts itself
  and nobody has to press a button while holding a toddler.

## While you're enjoying it
- **Nothing to do.** The camera started itself; just open presents.
- Say **"Mark that"** at a big reveal if you're near the camera and want to
  flag it, though with the whole hour rolling this is optional.

## Optional extras (only if you feel like it)
- The evening before, a [Holiday Lights Walk](../effects/holiday-lights-walk-purevideo.md)
  round the neighbourhood or a lights display pairs naturally with this
  plan — same PureVideo night settings as
  [Christmas Lights Roof-Mount Drive-Past](../effects/christmas-lights-roof-mount-drive-past.md),
  but on foot rather than from the car.

## Edit it fast
1. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) each child in turn — pause and
   resume it as the "story" moves from one to another.
2. [Multi-View](../editing/multi-view.md) for the opener and the giver's
   face at once.
3. [Time Freeze (Animated Photo)](../effects/time-freeze-animated-photo.md)
   on the big reveal.

## Tips
- The whole point is that nobody has to remember to film — it's already
  running. Resist the urge to pick it up and point it.
- If the tree lights are the brightest thing in the room, −0.3 keeps them
  from blowing out without making everything else too dark.
