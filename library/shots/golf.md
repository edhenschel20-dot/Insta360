# Golf

Not covered directly in this project's research — the notes below are general 360/action-cam advice applied to golf, marked **[general advice]** where they aren't backed by a specific source. Treat them as a reasonable starting point, not a tested recipe.

!!! note "Course etiquette first"
    Whatever you do, don't hold up play or wander into another group's line while setting up a shot. A camera on a tripod near the tee, out of the way of the tee box and swing path, is the safest default. [general advice]

## Setup (once) [general advice]
- Plant the stick or a small tripod a few metres to the side of the tee, out of the swing path and sightlines, camera roughly waist-to-chest height.
- For a flyover-style look at the hole, an overhead invisible-stick shot from the tee (see [Fake Drone Shot](../effects/fake-drone-shot.md)) gives a course-overview feel without an actual drone.
- On the green or fairway, the same static "plant it and walk away" approach used for [Family Events](family-events.md) works well for a group of players.

## While you're enjoying it [general advice]
- Play normally. Say "mark" before a swing you want to find easily later (a good drive, a tricky putt).

## Edit it fast [general advice]
- [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) on the golfer covers most swings without hand keyframes.
- For the swing itself, slow it down in the edit rather than trying to plan slow motion live — see [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](../effects/slow-motion-tracked-run.md).

## Effects that work here
- [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](../effects/slow-motion-tracked-run.md) — swing slow-mo, decided in the edit.
- [Bullet Time](../effects/bullet-time.md) — only in good light (120fps needs it), and only when you have a clear moment to yourself away from other groups. [general advice]
- [Fake Drone Shot](../effects/fake-drone-shot.md) — a flyover-style overhead look at the hole from the tee.

## Settings
[Bright sun & snow settings](../settings/bright-sun-and-snow.md) for most daytime rounds; [Bullet time & slow motion settings](../settings/bullet-time-and-slow-motion.md) for the swing.
