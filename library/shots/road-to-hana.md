# Road to Hana (full day)

The famous winding road along Maui's north-east coast: about 600 curves,
dozens of one-lane bridges, waterfalls, black sand, and the bamboo forest
at the far end. It's a long day, and the footage can easily turn into 10
hours of the inside of a car. The plan: **a TimeShift running for the drive,
plant-and-go at four or five stops you choose ahead, and don't film the rest.**

This is the Maui version of [Coastal drive with stops](coastal-drive-with-stops.md).
That page has the general method. This one is the day plan.

## The night before (10 minutes)
- [ ] **Book Waiʻānapanapa** (black sand beach) if you want to stop. Non-residents
  need a timed reservation, booked up to 30 days ahead, with no same-day
  bookings. Pick a slot that fits the drive (see the timeline below).
- [ ] **Charge both batteries plus the power bank.** Bring the car charger
  cable. You can top up the camera in the car between stops.
- [ ] **Empty a card.** A full Road to Hana day can easily be 150–250 GB.
- [ ] **Download offline maps.** There's no signal for most of the road.
  The GyroGuide or Shaka Guide audio apps are popular for knowing what's
  coming up.
- [ ] **Suction mount** for the windscreen. See
  [Accessories for shots](../gear/accessories-for-shots.md).

## The camera in the car
- **Mount it on the windscreen in the middle, low**, with one lens forward
  and one back into the car. That gives the road ahead and the family in one
  clip. **The passenger presses record, never the driver.**
- **TimeShift for the twisty stretches** (Huelo to Keʻanae is the most
  dramatic). Run it for 20–30 minutes at a time, not the whole day.
- **Between stretches, turn it off.** The hours of jungle-wall in between
  aren't worth the card space.
- Alternative: the passenger holds the stick **out of the window on the
  ocean side for 30 seconds** on a straight with no oncoming traffic, for the
  "drone flying beside the car" shot. Only on a quiet, straight section, and
  pull it in before every bend and bridge. See
  [Passenger Window Panorama](../effects/passenger-window-panorama.md).

## A sample timeline (leave early, around 7am)
Mile markers restart at 0 where Highway 360 begins, just past Pāʻia.

| Time | Stop | What to shoot |
|---|---|---|
| 7:00 | **Pāʻia** (coffee, last proper shop) | Quick [Town Walk](town-walk.md) clip, or skip it and do it on the way back |
| 7:30 | **Hoʻokipa Lookout** (just before the road starts) | Surfers below, and turtles on the beach in the afternoon. Planted stick at the rail, 2 minutes |
| 7:45 | **Twin Falls** (mile marker 2, paid parking, short walk) | First waterfall. Planted stick at the falls, then the walk back. See [Waterfalls & Trails](waterfalls-and-trails.md) |
| 8:30–10:30 | **The twisty section** (Huelo, Waikamoi, Garden of Eden, which is paid) | **TimeShift running.** Stop once at a viewpoint with a pull-off, such as the Keʻanae lookout |
| 10:30 | **Keʻanae Peninsula** | Black lava and big surf. Plant the stick on the rocks (well back from the water), with the waves on one lens and the family on the other. Banana bread from the stand |
| 11:30 | **Upper Waikani Falls (Three Bears)**, seen from the bridge | Quick. Stick held out over the bridge rail, 30 seconds. Don't park on the bridge |
| 12:00 | **Waiʻānapanapa** (your reserved slot) | Black sand, sea caves and a blowhole. [Beach Walk](beach-walk.md) with the black-sand settings. Tiny planet on the black sand |
| 13:30 | **Hāna town** (lunch) | Optional short clip |
| 14:00 | **Wailua Falls** (past Hāna, right by the road) | Planted stick by the road wall, 2 minutes. No hike needed |
| 14:30–17:00 | **Kīpahulu / Pīpīwai Trail** (Haleakalā NP, $30 per car, card only) | Bamboo forest look-up and Waimoku Falls. See [Waterfalls & Trails](waterfalls-and-trails.md) |
| 17:00 | **Drive back the way you came** | Optional TimeShift at golden hour. Otherwise, camera off and charging |

That's a big day. **If the kids are flagging, pick only the bold stops** and
skip the rest. Many families stay a night in Hāna rather than doing it there
and back in one go.

## While you're enjoying it
- At each stop, **plant first, then enjoy.** Stand the stick, press record,
  and go and look at the waterfall with everyone.
- Say **"Mark that"** for rainbows (very likely on this coast) and the first
  sight of each falls. See [Rainbows After a Shower](../effects/rainbows-after-a-shower.md).

## Edit it fast (one 2–3 minute film of the day)
1. **TimeShift clips need no reframing.** Trim them to 10–20 second chunks
   to use between stops.
2. **Each stop:** one 10–15 second piece. Fixed view or one slow keyframed
   reveal.
3. **Order:** drive, stop, drive, stop. The TimeShifts work as the
   transitions.
4. Add [Beat Sync](../editing/beat-sync-to-music.md) to a track, and you're done.
   Skip AI Edit for the stops (it'll pick faces over waterfalls).

## Road manners (the locals live here)
- **Pull fully off the road** to stop, and only where parking is allowed. No
  stopping on bridges or blind bends.
- **Let locals pass.** Pull over when someone's behind you.
- **Respect "kapu" (keep out) and private-property signs.** Many viral
  waterfalls are on private land.
- Flash floods: don't cross a flooded stream, and don't swim after heavy
  rain upstream.
