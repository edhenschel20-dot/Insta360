# Family Events

Parties, dinners, kids playing, presents, candles — the moments you actually want the camera rolling for and never want to have missed by fiddling with it.

## Setup (once)
- Plant the stick on a table, shelf or its own mini tripod rather than holding it — the X5's field of view means you don't need to be close to anyone.
- Let it run stationary. This is one of the few situations where a static camera genuinely beats a moving one: it turns the whole clip into "reframe wherever the interesting thing happens," which tracking handles well.

## While you're enjoying it
- Be part of it — you're always in the 360 capture and that's fine, it gets fixed in the edit, not by staying out of shot. Say "mark" at candles, toasts, or a good moment.

## Edit it fast
- [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) on whoever is active (opening presents, blowing out candles) is the fastest way to follow the story without hand keyframes. Pause and resume it if the "story" moves to someone else.
- For a first pass, [Auto Frame / AI Edit](../editing/auto-frame-and-ai-edit.md) can work, but check it — it's known to prefer people (often you, holding the camera) over the actual action, so don't trust the first cut blindly.

## Effects that work here
- [Split Screen / MultiView](../effects/split-screen-multiview.md) — front of the table and back at once, no tracking needed.
- [Tiny Planet & Inverted](../effects/tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) — a fun still from the table centrepiece angle.
- [Time Freeze (Animated Photo)](../effects/time-freeze-animated-photo.md) — a candle-blowing or toast moment as a flying-camera still.
