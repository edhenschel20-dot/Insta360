## Top 10 people shots to try first

Best result for the least effort, pulled from the two people-shot pages above.

1. **Low angle for full body, eye-level-or-above for faces** — say "chin forward and down." See [People Photos with a Phone](people-photos-with-a-phone.md#the-low-angle-and-why-it-can-backfire).
2. **Take three variations** — the asked-for shot, a low/0.5x shot, and a candid "after" shot. See [People Photos with a Phone](people-photos-with-a-phone.md#take-three-variations-every-time).
3. **Backlight, tap the face** — the single biggest jump in "does this look professional." See [People Photos with a Phone](people-photos-with-a-phone.md#backlight-tap-the-face).
4. **Shoot through something** — foreground blur with no Portrait Mode fakery. See [People Photos with a Phone](people-photos-with-a-phone.md#shoot-through-something).
5. **One posing prompt** — "walk toward me, now look at me." See [People Photos with a Phone](people-photos-with-a-phone.md#posing-prompts-instead-of-smile).
6. **Camera on the ground, everyone leans in** — one capture, two portraits (ring of faces + tiny planet). See [People Shots with the X5](people-shots-with-the-x5.md#camera-on-the-ground-everyone-leans-in).
7. **Tiny planet self-portrait** — the "how did you get that?" shot. See [People Shots with the X5](people-shots-with-the-x5.md#the-self-portrait-how-did-you-get-that-shot).
8. **The giant** — the 360 version of the low angle, further down than a phone can go. See [People Shots with the X5](people-shots-with-the-x5.md#the-giant-shot).
9. **3 m overhead "drone" shot** — most people can't tell it from a real drone. See [People Shots with the X5](people-shots-with-the-x5.md#the-overhead-invisible-stick-drone-portrait).
10. **Hand-held bullet time** — spin on the spot, or orbit a friend, no throwing required. See [People Shots with the X5](people-shots-with-the-x5.md#bullet-time-without-throwing).
