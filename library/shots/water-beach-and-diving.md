# Water, Beach & Diving

Kayaking, pool, beach, boat, snorkelling and diving. The X5 is natively waterproof for casual use (check the current depth rating in the manual before going deep) — no housing needed for most of this, and letting it roll is genuinely useful underwater: things swim past behind you that you only notice later.

## Setup (once)
- Short stick or chest mount for splash resilience; a longer stick works fine above the surface.
- Use the Invisible Dive Case if you're going deeper or faster than the camera's native rating.
- Pick a steady paddling/floating pace rather than whipping the stick around to "catch" a splash — let 360 capture it and choose the moment in the edit instead.

## While you're enjoying it
- Enjoy the water. Say "mark" (or use a physical marker if your voice won't register underwater) when something worth finding later happens.

## Edit it fast
- A fixed forward view or [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) on a fellow swimmer/diver covers most of this footage with very little reframing.
- For long dives or snorkels, [Finding Moments in Long Clips](../editing/finding-moments-in-long-clips.md) is the way to find the good bit without scrubbing the whole thing.

## Effects that work here
- [Portal Water Jump / Dive Match-Cut](../effects/portal-water-jump.md) — a deliberate, planned transition if you want one.
- [Bullet Time](../effects/bullet-time.md) — from a boat or dock, swinging over the water.
