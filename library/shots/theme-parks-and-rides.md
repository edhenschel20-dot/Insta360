# Theme Parks & Rides

Queues, rides, kids' reactions, characters, fireworks over the castle at closing time. Let the camera roll through all of it rather than stopping and starting for each moment.

## Setup (once)
- On rides with kids: mount or hold the stick so it's steady, and just **hold still at the rail** rather than swinging the camera around to follow the ride yourself — the 360 capture already sees everything, so your job is to keep it steady, not to aim it.
- Chest mount or short stick is easier to keep secure than a fully extended one on a moving ride.
- Pick one lens as "front" facing the kids before the ride starts.

## While you're enjoying it
- Just hold on and enjoy the ride with them. Say "mark" at a big moment (the drop, the scream, the photo-op) if you want to find it fast later.

## Edit it fast
- For ride footage: reach for [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) or a fixed view first — this is exactly the kind of footage where [Split & Fixed Views](../editing/split-and-fixed-views.md) beats manual keyframing, since you already know who the subject is (the kids) and roughly where they'll be.
- For a long day of queueing and walking between rides, use [Finding Moments in Long Clips](../editing/finding-moments-in-long-clips.md) rather than scrubbing the whole day by hand.

## Effects that work here
- [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](../effects/slow-motion-tracked-run.md) — for kids running to the next ride.
- [Freeze Go](../effects/freeze-go.md) — freezes the peak of a big scream or jump.
- [Split Screen / MultiView](../effects/split-screen-multiview.md) — reaction shot and the ride at once.

## Settings
[Fast motion & driving settings](../settings/fast-motion-and-driving.md) for the rides themselves; [Events & indoors](../settings/events-and-indoors.md) for indoor/dark rides; [Night & low light](../settings/night-and-low-light.md) for evening fireworks over the park.

## Where sticks aren't allowed
Some venues ban selfie sticks. See the
[Foldable Utility Handle](../effects/foldable-utility-handle.md) — a
folding case-handle that covers the lenses when closed and stands the
camera on a table when open, confirmed to pass a bag check at one park.
Hold it at arm's length for faces, since there's no distance from the
lens the way a stick gives you.
