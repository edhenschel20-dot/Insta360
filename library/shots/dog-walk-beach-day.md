# Dog walk / beach day

What the dog sees on the walk, the low chase shot of it running at the
camera, and a beach afternoon planted at the towels so nobody has to hold
the camera. **Mount it once for the dog, plant it once for the beach.**

## Before you leave (5 minutes)
- [ ] **Charge the battery** — a walk plus a beach afternoon is a long
  session.
- [ ] **Wipe both lenses** — sand and sea spray are both lens killers;
  wipe with a wet cloth and rinse, never dry-wipe.
- [ ] Fit the Pet Harness at home first, with treats, so the dog isn't
  meeting it for the first time on the walk.
- [ ] Bring the stick with its mini tripod for the beach base-camp shot.

## Settings (set once)
| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | Standard Video |
| Resolution | 5.7K 30–60fps |
| EV | **−0.3 to −0.7** on dark sand, to keep the sand dark and the surf white instead of a grey wash |
| White balance | Auto |

See [Bright sun & snow settings](../settings/bright-sun-and-snow.md).

## Where to stand and how to hold it
- **Dog walk:** X5 on the Pet Harness's anti-tilt base, screen-side
  backwards so the stitch seam runs along the dog's spine, straps snug.
  Expect some sulking the first time — that's normal.
- **Run-at-the-camera shot:** camera low on the ground or a mini tripod,
  someone calls the dog so it runs straight at the lens.
- **Beach base-camp:** stick planted in the mini tripod at the towels,
  raised to about head height so heads aren't cut off, lenses toward the
  sea and the towels rather than along the beach.

## While you're enjoying it
- **Press record at the start of the walk, or once you're set up at the
  towels, and let it roll.**
- Say **"Mark that"** at a good moment — the dog finding something, a
  sandcastle, someone running into the water.
- Enjoy the beach; the base-camp cam doesn't need a camera person.

## Optional extras (only if you feel like it)
- A [Tide-Pool Dip](../effects/tide-pool-dip.md) if there are rock pools
  nearby: lower the camera in on the short stick, waterline roughly on
  the housing seam, for creatures below and faces peering in above.
- A dry-sand tiny planet, with the beach as the "planet" and white surf
  round the rim.

## Edit it fast
1. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) on the dog for the walk, or on a
   child for the beach.
2. [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](../effects/slow-motion-tracked-run.md) for
   the run-at-the-camera shot.
3. A fixed forward view is enough for the beach base-camp footage; only
   reframe where something specific happens.

## Tips
- Switching mounts through a day (harness, then handheld, then planted)
  keeps a long outing from looking like one shot repeated all day.
- This is a "let it roll" situation for the same reason as everywhere
  else in this guide: the good moments at a beach happen when nobody's
  aiming the camera.
