# Trick shot cards: "we're here, what's the trick?"

Open this page when the family is **lingering anyway**: waiting for shave
ice, at a lookout, before the boat boards. Find the place, pick **one** trick
and do it in under a minute. Then put the camera back to normal and enjoy
the day. Tricks are optional extras, never the plan.

!!! tip "Put this page on your home screen"
    In Chrome: ⋮ menu › **Add to Home screen**. Before the Road to Hana and
    the summit, open it and tap ⋮ › **Download** (the ↓ arrow), so it works
    with no signal.

## Three habits that make every trick work
1. **The TRICK preset.** One saved camera preset: **Video, 5.7K30,
   Pre-Recording on**. Every Shot Lab template accepts it. Press the
   **Q button** to switch to it, and switch back afterwards. Set it up with
   [Quick set-ups](../settings/quick-setups.md).
2. **One signature move for the whole trip: the slow spin.** Stick **fully
   extended**, held upright with the bottom at your belt and your arms
   locked. Spin slowly **clockwise**. Hold still for **5 seconds before and
   after**, and **30 seconds** in all. Same move, same height, same
   direction, every place. That builds the
   [Same Pose, Many Places](../effects/same-pose-many-places.md) montage
   all trip.
3. **Two-part tricks: do part two before you leave.** Say **"mark that, part
   one"** to the camera. Don't get back in the car until part two is done.

**Stick label** (label tape on the 114 cm stick): *TRICK preset · full
length · slow clockwise spin · 30 s · still 5 s each end.*

## If it goes wrong
- **The template won't take the clip?** It was probably 8K, PureVideo or
  Timelapse mode. Use the TRICK preset next time.
- **The spin cut looks jumpy?** A different stick length, height or
  direction. Check your stick label.
- **The clones overlap or vanish?** The camera moved, or people stood too
  close. Plant it, and keep people about 2 m out.
- **Weird rotation in a template?** Calibrate the gyro, if your camera
  offers it (see the [practice drills](practice-drills.md)).
