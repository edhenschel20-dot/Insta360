# Fast holiday reel recipe

Turning a day's worth of clips into a reel on the phone, with the fewest
steps — Ben Claremont's travel workflow, adapted to letting the camera roll.

## Steps
1. Aim for roughly three shot ideas per location, rather than trying to
   cover everything.
2. Download footage in batches (e.g. once a day), rather than letting it
   all pile up until the end of the trip.
3. Skim and keep only the winners — don't try to use every clip you shot.
4. Trim each clip down to its best few seconds before reframing.
5. Speed clips up where the footage calls for it (see
   [Hyperlapse / TimeShift](../effects/hyperlapse-and-timeshift.md)).
6. **No more than three keyframes per clip** — if a clip needs more than
   that to look right, it's probably two clips.
7. Export at 4K for a reel that's quick to render and share.
8. Fast/slow choice: shoot 60fps when you know you'll reframe hard or want
   slow motion later; use 16/32/64× hyperlapse speeds when building from
   normal video; save TimeShift mode itself for genuinely long stretches
   (10+ minutes) rather than short ones.

## Disagreement to flag
Ben's workflow advises keeping individual clips short (5–30 seconds) for
speed of editing. **That conflicts with letting it roll** — if you let the
camera roll and edits afterwards (see this project's `CLAUDE.md`), because
long rolls catch things you only notice later. Rather than shooting
shorter, use [Finding moments in long clips](finding-moments-in-long-clips.md)
(voice marks, AI Highlights) to get the same "quick to find the good bit"
benefit without giving up the long roll.

## A starter reel sequence
No creator publishes a single named "trending 360 reel format" beyond
individual shot ideas, but a sensible sequence to cut 2–3 effects together
into one reel is:
1. A location reveal — see [Tiny Planet & Inverted](../effects/tiny-planet-and-inverted.md)
   (the "Location Reveal" variant: start tight, lift the stick, land on a
   tiny planet at the top, slowed to 0.5×).
2. A group movement shot with the kids — e.g.
   [Walking Tiny Planet](../effects/walking-tiny-planet.md), or a Hamster
   Wheel–style inverted spin if that page exists in your library.
3. A vehicle or boat "drone" shot — see
   [Car Exterior Stick & Drive-By](../effects/car-exterior-stick-drive-by.md)
   or [Boat Selfie / Off-the-Stern](../effects/boat-selfie-off-the-stern.md).
4. A spin transition into the next place — see
   [Spin and Barrel Roll](../effects/spin-and-barrel-roll.md).
5. A low, grounded finishing shot to close the reel.

Full worked 10–12 shot examples to watch rather than read: Best360's
travel vlogs (app version, `YyVv2tKdO1U`; Studio version, `JRNfhT4cP1w`,
2026-04-10) and MountMedia's 12-shot reel (`DZcy1So1Et4`, 2026-03-28).

## Tips
- Consistency across a day's clips (same rough speed choices, same colour
  recipe — see [Colour in the phone app](colour-in-the-app.md)) reads as
  more polished than any individual fancy shot.
