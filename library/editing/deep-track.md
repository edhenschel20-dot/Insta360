# Deep Track

Draw your own box around a subject and the app keeps it centred. This is
the only reframing method where **you** choose the subject rather than the
AI guessing — the fix for "the AI keeps following me, the stick holder,
instead of the actual thing I wanted."

## Steps
1. Open the clip in the Editor or Quick Edit.
2. Find Deep Track (menu names vary by app version). It usually suggests a
   box on its own — ignore the suggestion if it's wrong.
3. **Draw or move the box yourself** onto whoever or whatever you actually
   want followed. Two tracker types: Universal (objects — helmets, cups,
   phones) and Individual (whole people/pets).
4. Let it process, then preview and adjust if needed.
5. **To switch targets mid-clip:** pause tracking, re-pick the box on the
   new subject, and resume. This is the official way to hand off from one
   subject to another partway through a clip (e.g. when the "story" moves
   from one person to another at an event).

## Tips
- Deep Track can't be combined with keyframes or Movement templates on the
  same clip, and isn't available for Bullet Time, Timelapse, or
  single-lens clips.
- Works best when the subject is a reasonable size in frame and not too far
  from the camera.
- This is the go-to method for family events and anything static-camera
  with people moving through frame — plant the stick, let the moment
  happen, then Deep Track whoever's active afterwards.
- Don't fight a bad auto-suggestion with more keyframes — just re-draw the
  box or fall back to [split and fixed views](split-and-fixed-views.md).
