# Quick Edit: steer by phone

Play the clip and steer the view live — by swiping, a virtual joystick, or
physically moving the phone (it uses the phone's own gyro). One pass, no
keyframes, and it saves instantly.

## Steps
1. Open the clip in Quick Edit / Record mode (menu names vary by app
   version — sometimes called "Snap Wizard").
2. Play the clip. As it plays, swipe, use the on-screen joystick, or
   physically tilt/turn the phone to steer where the view points.
3. There are also **spin** and **barrel roll** buttons you can tap live
   while it plays, for a quick spin effect without keyframing one.
4. Let it play through once — that one pass is your reframe. Export.

## Tips
- Reportedly works well even sitting still and just spinning in an office
  chair while holding the phone — a decent "reframe controller" for free.
- Good for walk-throughs, look-arounds, and anything where you just want a
  natural "look around the scene" feel rather than a precise cinematic
  move.
- Also called **"viewfinder mode"** in some tutorials — same feature: hold
  record on the clip, physically turn your body and tilt the phone to
  trace the camera move you want (used for bicycle tiny-planet and
  hyperlapse-reveal shots, for example).
- If you mess up partway through, just play it again — it's a live pass,
  not a set of edits you have to undo one at a time.
- For a precise, repeatable move (a reveal timed exactly to a beat), use
  [keyframes with easing](keyframes-with-easing.md) instead — this method
  trades precision for speed.
