# Find your way round the app

The Insta360 app was redesigned (version 2.0 onward). The X2-era app had
AI / Quick / Pro buttons along the top of the player. The current one
sorts things into **bottom tabs** and a **player with an editing bar**.
This page is a map. For the "how do I get the result" detail, follow the
links.

> **Checked 2026-10-30.** Names below come from Insta360's official online
> manual. The newest Android build found
> was **2.28.0 (July 2026)**, but the manual does not say which version
> each page was written for. Button names can shift between versions, so
> anything marked **[unverified]** needs a look on your phone.

!!! tip "Practise before the trip"
    Run through jobs (a) to (e) once at home with a short test clip.
    Anything that looks different on your phone, note it down so this page
    can be corrected before you go.

## What moved since the X2 days

| Job | X2-era app | Current app (v2.x) |
|---|---|---|
| See your clips | Album | **Album** tab (bottom). Sub-tabs: **Camera**, **Downloaded**, and **Cloud** (Cloud only shows if you pay for it). |
| Get clips off the camera | Select, then download | Album, then the select icon (top right), then **Download** (into the app) or **Export** (into the phone's gallery). Same idea, new icons. |
| Open a 360 clip to look round | Player | **Player page**: tap a clip in Album. Left-hand quick tools: Trim, Keyframe, Snapshot, Highlights, Favourite, Tag, Delete. They fade after about 3 seconds; tap the arrow at the bottom to keep them on. |
| Reframe a 360 clip | Pro / Quick edit buttons | Bottom of the Player: **Auto Edit**, **Editor**, **Record**. "Record" is the old "Quick" (user-reported, **[unverified]**). Or the **Edit** tab, then **Create a Video**, then **360 Reframe**. |
| View choices | Drag round | Row of view buttons: **Custom** (drag), **Selfie**, **Forward**, **AI Frame**, and **InstaFrame** (X5 only, follows a subject). |
| Keyframes | Pro editing | Player: **Keyframe** icon on the left. Or Edit, Create a Video, 360 Reframe, then add a keyframe. Newer extras: **Auto Keyframe** (v2.10.4+) and **Keyframe Curves** (v2.17.0+). |
| Deep Track | Tracking | Player: **Editor**, then **Deep Track**, then **Start tracking**. Or **Record**, then **Tracking**. Also in Create a Video, 360 Reframe, then **Tracking**. |
| AI edit / Auto Frame | AI button | **Auto Edit** at the bottom of the Player, or **Auto Edit** on the Edit tab. **AI Frame** is a view option. |
| Templates / Shot Lab / FlashCut | Templates inside editor | **Edit** tab: Create a Video, Auto Edit, FlashCut and **Shot Lab** (AI templates, themes, effects). Movement templates: Player, Keyframe, **Movement**; or 360 Reframe. |
| Export | Export button | Player: **Export** (top right). Album: long-press a clip, **Export to Phone**, **Save settings**. See below. |
| Saved edits | Projects | **Edit** tab, then **Drafts**. |
| Settings / firmware | Camera settings page | **[unverified]** Likely the **Me** tab for app settings (the manual mentions "Performance Mode" on the Me page). Camera firmware: usually offered when the camera is connected. Check on the phone. |

## The five jobs

### (a) Connect and download only the clips you pick
1. Turn the camera on. Open the Insta360 app. Connect to the X5 (Bluetooth
   and location permissions need to be allowed; if it won't connect, turn off
   "WLAN+" or auto-switch Wi-Fi in Android settings).
2. Tap **Album** at the bottom. Open the **Camera** sub-tab to see what is on
   the card.
3. Tap the **select icon (top right)** (a tick/check icon) and tick only the
   clips you want. There is no limit on how many.
4. Choose at the bottom:
   - **Download** saves the original into the app (best for editing).
   - **Export** (to Phone) saves a finished video to your gallery.
5. **Keep the app open and the phone unlocked** while it runs. On Android,
   allow the app to run in the background (Settings, Apps or Battery) or it
   may stall.

Note: batch **Export** does not support 360 video or 8K. Use **Download** for
your 360 clips.

### (b) Trim a long clip and find the moment
1. **Album**, **Downloaded** sub-tab, tap the clip. It opens in the Player.
2. Scrub the timeline. If you said "mark that" while shooting, see
   [Finding Moments in Long Clips](finding-moments-in-long-clips.md). The
   **Highlights** icon (left tools) shows AI-found moments. On Android it
   needs a Qualcomm phone chip. If it is greyed out, that is why.
3. To cut: tap **Trim** (left tools), or **Editor**, then **Trim**. Drag the
   ends, then **Confirm Clip**. Jump Cut is in the same tool.
4. Then **Edit and Export** (bottom right) takes you to Quick Editing.

### (c) Reframe: a fixed view, then a couple of keyframes
Fixed views first: see [Split and Fixed Views](split-and-fixed-views.md).

1. Open the clip in the Player and tap **Editor**, then **Trim**, then
   **Edit and Export** (bottom right). This opens Quick Editing. [From the manual.]
2. Pick a view: **Custom** (drag the picture to where you want it),
   **Forward**, **Selfie** or **AI Frame**. Choosing a view this way applies to the
   **whole** clip.
3. For two or three keyframes: go back to the Player and tap the **Keyframe**
   icon (left). Move the playhead, swipe the picture to aim it, and a keyframe is
   set. Move on and repeat. Two keyframes is enough for a smooth turn.
4. Optional: with two or more keyframes, tap **Keyframe Curve** and pick
   **Ease In/Out** for a gentle move. Hard Cut jumps instead of turning.
5. **Auto Keyframe** (in **Editor**, v2.10.4+) makes a first pass for you. Delete
   the ones that swing round to the family.

Facts: keyframes can't be copied and pasted in the app (Studio on a PC can).
[Keyframe screen buttons may not look exactly like this: **[unverified]**
on the 2026 build. The manual's keyframe page does not show screenshots.]

More: [Keyframes with Easing](keyframes-with-easing.md).

### (d) Deep Track a subject
See [Deep Track](deep-track.md) for the why. The tap path:

1. Album, tap the clip, **Editor**, **Deep Track**, **Start tracking**.
2. A **green box** appears on a guessed target. Move or resize it onto the
   thing you want (pinch to zoom). Individual tracker is for people and pets;
   Universal is for objects.
3. Let it finish, then check it.
4. To switch subject part way: use **pause** (Quick Editing only; the manual is
   inconsistent on whether this is Android in v2.21.0+, so **[unverified]**).

Limits: 360 only; not with keyframes or Movement templates on the same clip.

### (e) Export at good quality and share
- **Edited 360 clip (reframed, keyframed, tracked): in Quick Editing tap
  Export (top right), then Export Full** (whole thing) or **Export Clip**
  (pieces). This gives a flat video.
- **Unedited 360 file:** Player, **Export** (top right), choose "360" or "flat".
- **Settings:** Save settings page lets you set resolution. 360 export offers
  1080p or 4K only. Defaults for batch are 1080p, 30 fps, 25 Mbps.
- **Low-RAM phones:** if 4K is blocked or crashes (Android with 6 GB RAM or less),
  turn on **Performance Mode** at the bottom of the **Me** page.
- Keep the app open and the phone unlocked while exporting.
- Share: after export the app offers share buttons, or share from the gallery.
  [Share-sheet layout **[unverified]**.]

### (f) Where do the files live on Android?
- **Downloaded originals** (the app's own copy) are at:
  `Internal storage > Android > data > com.arashivision.insta360akiko > files > Insta360OneR > galleryOriginal`
  Newer Android versions often block the Files app from opening `Android/data`.
  If so, search for "insv", "VID" or "DCIM", or use a PC in MTP mode.
- **Exported videos** go to the phone's normal gallery.
- **Importing `.insv` copied to the phone by USB-C card reader:**
  **[unverified, and likely unreliable]**. Insta360's manual (GO 3S) uses an
  "IMPORT" folder for a different camera. For the X5, users report the app
  cannot see a normal USB card reader's folders on Android, because of
  Android security. The Insta360 **Quick Reader** adapter is said to make the
  card look like phone storage. I found no official X5 page saying "copy
  `.insv` into Files and open it". The safe route: download over the camera
  connection (job a), or copy to a PC and use Studio. A card-reader copy
  is still a useful **backup**, even if the app can't open it.

## If you get lost
- **Back to the clip:** Album tab, then tap the clip. You are in the Player
  again. Bottom bar: Auto Edit, Editor, Record.
- **Back to saved work:** **Edit** tab, then **Drafts**. Tap one to resume,
  or use the three dots to Rename, Duplicate or Delete.
- **Only Advanced Editing makes a draft** (when you exit it). Quick Editing
  just keeps its data and may ask whether to save a draft on exit. If you
  leave without saving, expect to redo it.
- **Undo:** The manual says Quick Editing has **no undo/redo**. Re-adjust the
  view, or delete the keyframe/tracking and redo it. Deleting a keyframe is
  the quick fix for a bad swing.
- **Tools disappeared:** tap the arrow at the bottom of the Player to pin their
  names; double-tap the picture to toggle Clean Screen.
- **Greyed-out Highlights / Auto Edit:** often a phone chip or RAM limit, not
  something you did.
- **Edits don't carry through Album export:** a plain export from Album keeps
  only your **trim**; reframe/keyframe changes in Quick/Advanced editing aren't
  included. Export from inside the editor instead.
