# Colour in the phone app

Where the colour tools hide in the current app, and a simple recipe that
doesn't need Studio or a desktop NLE.

## Steps
1. **Find the tools (2026 app path):** Keyframe editor > expand the clip >
   **Edit > Adjust** (menu names vary by app version). There's also a
   one-tap **Filters** option, and a single **Color Plus** slider for a
   quick, broad push.
2. **Everyday recipe:** highlights down a touch, shadows up a touch,
   saturation up slightly, temperature nudged to taste. Keep every change
   small — this is a polish pass, not a full grade.
3. **Night clips:** shadows up, highlights down, saturation up — the
   opposite balance from a bright daytime clip, because night footage
   starts crushed and desaturated.
4. **If you want more control:** Ben Claremont's free five-LUT pack
   (includes a Night look and an HDR look) is built for Studio or DaVinci
   Resolve, not the phone app — the phone app's LUT/colour tools are more
   limited, so stick to Filters/Adjust there and move to Studio only if you
   want the LUT pack specifically.

## Tips
- Small, repeatable adjustments beat chasing a "perfect" grade on a phone
  screen — colours often look different again once you view the export on
  a bigger screen.
- Apply the same rough recipe across a whole day's clips for consistency,
  rather than grading each one from scratch.
