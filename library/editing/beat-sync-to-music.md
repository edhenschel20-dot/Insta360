# Beat-sync a reel to music

There's no dedicated one-tap "auto beat-sync" feature in the app (checked
2026-09-26: only the manual workflow below and the music picker in Auto Edit
templates exist). This is the manual way to land a cut or transition exactly
on the drop of a trending sound.

## Steps
1. Screen-record the sound where you found it (Instagram/TikTok) so you have
   a local copy of the audio.
2. In the Insta360 app, open the music icon on the edit timeline and use
   **Extract music** to pull the audio out of that screen recording (2022-era
   UI — confirm the option is still named this in your current app version).
3. Trim the extracted track to the section you want, using the waveform to
   find the drop or beat.
4. Trim your video clip so the cut (or the start of a transition effect)
   lands exactly on that point in the waveform.
5. Pick a transition effect from the library that suits the moment — see
   the [effects library](../effects/index.md) for the transitions category.

## Tips
- If you're posting to Instagram or TikTok, use that platform's own music
  library rather than an extracted copy where possible — safer for
  copyright than re-uploading someone else's track.
- This is a manual, fiddly process — worth it for a short highlight reel you
  care about, not for every clip.
- Auto Edit templates (see [Auto Edit 2.0 & Moments Pro](../effects/auto-edit-2-and-moments-pro.md))
  pick their own music and pacing automatically — a faster, lower-control
  alternative if you don't need the cut on an exact beat.
