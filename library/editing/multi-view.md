# Multi-view

Shows two to four angles from the same moment at once, instead of forcing
you to choose one. Good when both the action and the reaction matter.

## Steps
1. Open the clip and find the reframing menu (menu names vary by app
   version — look for Multiview or Split Screen).
2. Choose a layout (2 to 4 panes).
3. Drag each pane's selection box onto the angle you want (e.g. the parade
   float in one pane, a reaction in another).
4. Adjust each pane's zoom individually if needed. Some layouts support a
   blend edge or picture-in-picture instead of a hard split.
5. Preview and export — this applies once, for the whole clip.

## Tips
- Good for events and two-person conversations where you don't want to
  pick a speaker or choose between the action and the reaction.
- Applied once, it covers the whole clip — no per-moment keyframing needed.
- If one pane ends up more interesting than the others, you can always
  re-export that portion as a single [fixed view](split-and-fixed-views.md)
  afterwards.
