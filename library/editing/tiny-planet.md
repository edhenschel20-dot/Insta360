# Tiny planet

A one-tap look that wraps the whole 360 view into a little sphere, with you
(or the scene) sitting on top of it. Good for group shots and static
scenes — not a general reframing method.

## Steps
1. Best shot with the stick planted vertically on the ground, camera at
   roughly 1–1.5 m, with a group standing in a loose circle around it or a
   landscape surrounding it (photo or a few seconds of video works).
2. Open the clip or photo in the app.
3. Find the FOV control and drag it to maximum, tilting straight down for a
   tiny planet (or straight up for the inverted/sky-ball version) — or pick
   a **Tiny Planet** effect/template directly if the app offers one (menu
   names vary by app version).
4. Export — it's a one-tap effect, no keyframing needed.

## Tips
- Works whether the camera is static with things moving around it, or
  moving through a changing environment — keep the stick vertical either
  way.
- A good "establishing shot" to pair before a walking b-roll clip of the
  same spot, or as a group-photo alternative at a static location.
- For a transition effect (flipping from tiny planet into a normal view),
  set one keyframe in tiny planet and a second 1–2 seconds later in normal
  view — see [keyframes with easing](keyframes-with-easing.md).
