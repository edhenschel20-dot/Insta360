# Use the guide with no signal

The whole guide can live on your phone, so it works on the Road to Hana, at
the summit, on a boat, or in airplane mode.

## Do this once, at home, on Wi-Fi
1. Open the guide in **Chrome** on your phone.
2. Tap **⋮** (top right) › **Add to Home screen** › **Install**. You now
   have an "X5 Guide" icon that opens like an app.
3. Open it from that icon, come back to this page, and tap the button:

<button id="offline-save" class="md-button md-button--primary">Save the whole guide for offline</button>

<p id="offline-status">Checking…</p>

It takes a minute or two. When it says **✅ Saved**, you're done.

## After that
- **It keeps itself up to date.** Whenever you open it with signal, it
  re-saves the guide in the background, once a day at most.
- **Before a no-signal day** (Road to Hana, the summit, a boat trip), open
  this page and check the date next to ✅. Tap the button again if it's old.
- **Test it:** turn on airplane mode, open the X5 Guide icon and tap round a
  few pages. Search works too.

## What doesn't work offline
- **Embedded YouTube videos.** The written steps are all there.
- **Links to other websites** (sources, shop pages).

## If it doesn't work
- Use **Chrome**. Some other Android browsers don't support saving a site.
- If the button does nothing, close the app fully, open it again, and tap
  it once more.
- **Backup plan:** on any page, tap ⋮ › **Download** (the ↓ arrow) to save
  that one page. Do it for the [Trick Shot Cards](shots/trick-shot-cards.md),
  [Maui day by day](trips/maui-day-by-day.md) and
  [Quick set-ups](settings/quick-setups.md).
