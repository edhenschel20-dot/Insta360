# Memory cards

## Steps
1. **Buy V30 or better** (microSDXC, exFAT, sustained 30 MB/s write) —
   that's the X5's stated minimum. Faster (V60/V90) doesn't hurt but isn't
   required.
2. **Avoid fakes.** Cheap "high-capacity" cards from marketplace listings
   are a known source of counterfeits that report the wrong size or fail
   under sustained write. Buy from a card manufacturer directly, a major
   retailer, or the Insta360 store, not the cheapest marketplace listing
   you can find.
3. **1TB cards** are supported and worth it for a multi-day trip — roughly
   12 hours of headroom at 5.7K (based on the worst-case 80 GB/hr figure —
   see [storage and batteries](storage-and-batteries.md)), comfortably
   past what any single battery charge will use in a day.
4. **Bring fresh cards per trip** rather than reusing an old, heavily
   rewritten card for something important — card wear is real, and a
   corrupted card mid-trip is worse than the cost of a new one.
5. **Back up without a laptop:** phone + a USB-C card reader + a portable
   SSD. Copy the card to the SSD each evening (or every couple of days),
   confirm the copy, then you can safely reuse or wipe the card if needed.
   Wired transfer (USB-C, or the optional Quick Reader accessory) is
   reportedly "dramatically" faster than WiFi for large files — worth doing
   backups this way rather than over WiFi.

## Tips
- Card space is rarely the tight constraint on a single continuous take —
  battery runs out first. Don't over-buy cards at the expense of a spare
  battery.
- Format cards in the camera (not just deleting files) when starting a new
  trip, to avoid filesystem issues.
