# Accessories that unlock shots

Which accessory unlocks which shots — a phone-friendly shopping guide, not a
full review site. Rough costs are given only where the research actually
found a number; most accessory prices weren't found this round, so check
Insta360's store for current pricing before buying.

## Must-have
These cover most everyday shooting.

- **Extended Edition 3 m Selfie Stick.** Unlocks
  [Fake Drone Shot](../effects/fake-drone-shot.md),
  [Boat Selfie / Off-the-Stern](../effects/boat-selfie-off-the-stern.md), tall
  night timelapses, and fireworks (see
  [Parades & Fireworks](../shots/parades-and-fireworks.md)). Ben Claremont
  rates it only 7/10 (it bends, and the viewpoint isn't hugely different
  from the standard stick) — but creators who shoot boats and fireworks
  keep reaching for it anyway.
- **Electric Suction Cup Mount** (self-pumping, warns when pressure drops)
  **or the cheaper Triple Suction Cup Car Mount.** Unlocks
  [Car Exterior Stick & Drive-By](../effects/car-exterior-stick-drive-by.md)
  shots and roof/window/mirror mounting generally. Mount so the arm folds
  *against* acceleration, not braking — sway under braking is the main
  failure mode with cheaper cups.
- **Invisible Dive Case** (standard, plastic dome) **or Dive Case Pro**
  (optically-correct glass dome, roughly **$20 more**, noticeably clearer
  per one review). Needed for anything past snorkelling depth — see
  [Underwater diving settings](../settings/underwater-diving.md). Dive Case
  Mode locks most other settings once it's on, so set a Q-button preset
  (e.g. 5.7K60 for slow-mo) *before* you go under — you can't change
  settings inside the case mid-dive.
- **ND filters (ND16/32/64).** Unlocks the "shutter = half the interval"
  night timelapse look and the speed-blur trick for fast motion — see
  [Night & low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md) and
  [Fast motion & driving settings](../settings/fast-motion-and-driving.md).
- **A spare battery or power bank, plus the all-weather USB-C door.** Not a
  "shot" accessory, but unlocks longer sessions and charging in the rain.
  See [Storage and batteries](storage-and-batteries.md).

## Nice-to-have
Worth it if the specific shot matters to you.

- **Bullet Time Cord** (retractable, screws into the base). Unlocks
  [Bullet Time](../effects/bullet-time.md) and
  [Bullet Time Mix](../effects/bullet-time-mix.md) at group events. MrBlack
  calls it "old school but just as good"; Ben rates the newer Bullet Time
  stick 2.0 only 4/10, so the cord isn't obviously worse.
- **GPS Preview Remote / Action Remote.** Start/stop recording from a boat
  rail or bike bars without touching the camera, and supplies GPS for a
  stats overlay (speed, heart rate) on rides and runs.
- **Ground stake (any ¼″ tent-peg-style spike).** Turns any stick into a
  planted mount on turf, sand or garden — no tripod legs in the shot.
- **Quick Release plate.** Faster mount swaps between activities — useful
  if you're switching from stick to suction cup to tripod in one day.
- **Foldable / FreeFrame Selfie Stick (remote kit).** See
  [Foldable Selfie Stick & Remote Kit](../effects/foldable-selfie-stick-remote-kit.md)
  and [InstaFrame & Virtual Gimbal](../effects/instaframe-and-virtual-gimbal.md)
  — built for InstaFrame 2.0's single-lens tracking, which is a niche mode
  per this project's rules. Mention it, don't lead with it.

## Specialist
Only worth buying for a specific activity.

- **Dive Buddy** (buoyancy handle with adjustable weights + a 3-leg seabed
  stand, ¼″ thread). Lets the camera sit on the seabed hands-free while you
  swim around it — not for currents, it will drift away.
- **Water Sports Rope Mount.** Clamps onto a wakeboard tow rope or kite
  line so the rider stays centred in frame — tubing and banana-boat rides
  too.
- **Pole Mount (ski/hiking pole clamp, ¼″ thread).** Clamps onto a ski pole
  or golf club shaft for a "from the club's point of view" angle — practice
  swings only, not a shot with a ball in play. See [Golf](../shots/golf.md).
- **Bike Tail Mount Kit, Headset Cap Mount, Bike Computer Mount, and
  Third-Person Handlebar Mount.** Between them these cover
  [Front-Mount "Who Is Filming Me"](../effects/front-mount-pov.md),
  [Third-Person Follow / Top-Down](../effects/third-person-follow-view.md),
  and wheel-centred hyperlapses (see
  [Hyperlapse / TimeShift](../effects/hyperlapse-and-timeshift.md)). The
  handlebar mount is rated for 25–32 mm bars, under 60 km/h, roads only.
- **Back Bar Mount.** A back-mounted third-person shot — MountMedia calls
  it "not comfortable, but my coolest shots." Related:
  [Third-Person Follow / Top-Down](../effects/third-person-follow-view.md).
- **Ski Pole Mount, Unicorn Helmet Mount, Adhesive Mount Kit, Chest Strap.**
  Insta360's own mount map for skiing: helmet mount for first-person (must
  see your own skis/boots to read as POV), pole mount for third-person,
  adhesive mount for first-person, chest strap for hands-free first-person —
  see [Chest-Mount 360 POV](../effects/chest-mount-pov.md) for the chest
  option specifically.
- **Horizontal (vertical-horizontal) Mount.** Ben rates it C-tier — niche,
  for low-profile helmet or vehicle mounting only.
- **Utility Frame.** Wind-noise reduction, cold shoes for an external
  mic/light, and a lanyard point for extra security — no specific shot,
  just a quality-of-life cage.
- **Mic Air ×2.** Pairs two wireless mics to one X5 — useful when you and a
  child (or riding partner) are apart, e.g. skiing or biking separately.
