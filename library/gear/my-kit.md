# My kit: what I own and when to grab it

Everything in Ed's camera bag, what each item is for, and which shots it
suits. For things worth buying, see [What to buy next](#what-to-buy-next) at
the bottom. For the general shopping guide, see
[Accessories That Unlock Shots](accessories-for-shots.md).

## Which one do I grab?
| Situation | Grab this |
|---|---|
| Walking around, sightseeing | **114 cm stick**, with the **wrist strap** on a D-ring at the bottom |
| Camera stands by itself (tee shot, beach base camp, waterfall, group shot, timelapse, clone effects) | **2-in-1 stick and tripod** |
| Dinner table (needs to clear the glasses) | **2-in-1**, collapsed or pulled out a little, legs open |
| Very low (sand, tide pools, dashboard, a wall top) | Camera straight onto the **SmallRig mini tripod** |
| Sticks not allowed, crowds, helicopter | **Short handle**, tethered to your wrist |
| Golf cart, boat rail, lookout railing, jet ski | **Neewer clamp and arm** (114 cm stick or cage on top) |
| A steel surface such as a car body (parked or slow only) | **Neewer magnetic base**, plus a safety cord |
| Hands busy: paddling, holding a kid's hand, snorkelling | **Hand and wrist mount** (camera in the cage) |
| A rental board, kayak or boat with a GoPro mount | **Cage** (folding prongs), or the **GoPro adapters** |
| Scuba | **Dive Case Pro** with the **weight block**, a **D-ring** and a lanyard |

**Two rules that apply to everything:**
1. **Nothing between the camera and a stick.** The stick only disappears
   because it hides in the blind spot right under the camera. A cage or
   adapter there sticks out of that spot and shows in the footage.
2. **Cord on anything near water, edges or moving vehicles.** A D-ring screw
   in a ¼-inch socket gives you a strong loop to clip a cord to.

## Sticks and stands

### 114 cm Invisible Selfie Stick (came with the X5 bundle)
- **Use for:** walking shots, the cart mount, holding it overhead (see
  [Fake Drone Shot](../effects/fake-drone-shot.md)), and the underwater
  "drone" (see [Underwater Third-Person Drone](../effects/underwater-third-person-drone-bts.md)).
- **Bottom socket (¼ inch):** takes a D-ring for a strap or cord, or the
  SmallRig mini tripod to make it stand up.
- It's slim and light, so it whips less on bumps than a long stick.
- **Limit:** at 1.14 m it isn't a true "drone height" stick. See
  [What to buy next](#what-to-buy-next).

### 2-in-1 Invisible Selfie Stick and Tripod (about 105–109 cm)
- **Use for:** any time the camera stands by itself. Examples are the tee
  shots at [Kapalua](../shots/kapalua-plantation.md),
  [Beach Base Camp](../effects/beach-base-camp-cam.md), the planted shot at
  the falls, [Plant-and-Go](../effects/plant-and-go-stick-tripod.md),
  [Clone Trail](../effects/clone-trail.md),
  [Same Pose, Many Places](../effects/same-pose-many-places.md), sunset
  timelapses and family group shots.
- **Collapsed, it's also a short handle and a table stand.** At dinner it
  raises the camera above the glasses (see
  [Dinner in a Beautiful Room](../shots/dinner-in-a-beautiful-room.md)).
- The legs are built into the handle. The button on the bottom opens them.
  There's no socket at the bottom.
- **Wind:** the legs are small for a stick this long. Extend it part way or
  hang a bag from it. On sand, press the feet in.
- The legs show slightly at the bottom of the 360 view. In the edit, keep
  the view level or looking up.

### SmallRig mini tripod
- **Straight on the camera:** low ground shots (see
  [Low-Angle Ground Skim](../effects/low-angle-ground-skim.md) and
  [Ground-Level Tiny Planet](../effects/ground-level-inverted-planet-photo.md)),
  the top of a wall or a café table for a timelapse, a dashboard or a boat
  deck.
- **On the bottom of the 114 cm stick:** a second stand-up stick.
- Set it on flat ground, out of the wind. It's top-heavy.

### Short handle (red ends, keyring)
- **Use for:** places where a stick isn't allowed or doesn't suit, such as
  restaurants, shows, crowds, the luau, and the helicopter (ask the
  operator, and keep it tethered).
- Also for low skims over sand and tide pools, and following kids at their
  height.
- Its bottom socket takes the mini tripod or a D-ring. The keyring takes
  the wrist strap.
- Your hand will be bigger in the shot than on a stick, so save it for
  those moments.

## Mounts

### Neewer CA175 clamp kit (clamp, arm, ball heads, magnetic base)
- **Clamp and arm:** for the golf cart (already fitted, at an angle), boat
  and lookout railings, jet ski handlebars, a balcony rail for a sunset
  timelapse, and a lamp post at a parade (see
  [Parade Periscope](../effects/parade-periscope-lamp-post-clamp.md)). See
  [Golf Cart Mount](golf-cart-mount.md).
- **Magnetic base:** steel only. A car body works; a golf cart, boat or
  railing usually won't. For car shots (see
  [Car Exterior Stick & Drive-By](../effects/car-exterior-stick-drive-by.md)),
  only use it parked or at low speed, **always with a safety cord**.
- Lock it with the star-knob nut so the camera can't twist loose.

### Hand and wrist mount (GoPro-style)
- **Use for:** keeping your hands free. Examples are paddling, holding a
  kid's hand, and snorkelling with your arm out ("Superman").
- The camera fits by the **cage's folding prongs** or the **GoPro to ¼-inch
  plate**.
- Your arm will fill the bottom of the 360 view, so point the edit forward
  or up. It's for short fun moments.
- Velcro loosens when it's wet, so pull it tight and rinse it afterwards.

### Clip mount (GoPro-style)
- **Use for:** a backpack strap, a belt, the beach bag or a stroller handle.
  On a shoulder strap you'll be big in the shot. It's better as a base-camp
  camera clipped to the bag or the cooler.
- Clips can pop off, so add a cord near water.

### Stick-on mounts (two round discs) — leave them at home
- They're adhesive, and you don't want glue on rental cars, golf carts or
  boats.

## Protection

### K&F Concept cage
- **Use for:** clamps and GoPro-style mounts (flip the prongs out, no
  adapter needed), rocky places, and tying a cord through the loop hole.
- **Take it off** for the selfie sticks (it shows, and it's heavier up top)
  and for the dive case (the camera must be bare).
- **Rule:** cage for mounts, bare camera for the stick.

### Spare X5 lenses
- Swap a lens only for a **real scratch or chip** that shows in footage. A
  smudge just needs a cloth.
- Swap **indoors, with dry hands**. Never do it on the beach.
- **Practise one swap at home** before the trip. Afterwards, film a short
  test clip and check the join between the lenses looks clean.

## Water

### Invisible Dive Case Pro (for X5)
- **The case:** glass domes, for scuba. Seal it at the hotel. See
  [Scuba Dive](../shots/scuba-dive.md).
- **Anti-fog inserts** (a pack comes with the case): put a fresh one in
  each dive day, where the blue label says.
- **Weight block** (the black knurled bar, on the 3-prong adapter and thumb
  screw): this is what lets the camera sit on the sand for the
  [Seabed Drop and Swim-Around](../effects/seabed-drop-and-swim-around.md),
  and it steadies hand-held shots in surge.
- **The weight's bottom socket:** screw in a **D-ring** and clip on the
  lanyard to your BCD for the surf entry. Or screw on the 114 cm stick for
  the underwater "drone" (test it at home).
- **Neoprene bag:** for storage and travel.
- **Rinse** the case, weight, adapters, D-ring and stick in fresh water
  after every dive. Salt seizes threads.

## Batteries and charging
You have **two X5 batteries**, plus power banks and a battery charger that
runs off a power bank.

- **The plan:** one battery in the camera, one in the charger. When the
  camera's battery runs low, swap them and put the flat one straight on
  charge.
- **How long one lasts:** roughly 2¼ hours at 5.7K30, less in the heat and
  less at 8K. See [Storage & batteries](storage-and-batteries.md).
- **Long days:** a 4-hour golf round or a full Road to Hana day needs both
  batteries. Swap at a natural break (the turn at golf, a lunch stop on the
  road) and charge the flat one from the power bank as you go.
- **Dive days:** you can't swap inside the dive case, so start each dive
  with a full battery. Swap in the car between dives only with dry hands.
- **Every night:** charge both batteries and the power banks.
- **Flying:** spare batteries and power banks must go in your carry-on, not
  in checked bags.

## Small parts (keep in one pouch)
| Part | What it does | Bring to Maui? |
|---|---|---|
| D-ring screws (×3) | A strong loop in any ¼-inch socket for a strap or safety cord | **Yes, all three** (stick, dive weight, spare) |
| Paracord wrist strap | Stops the stick leaving your hand | **Yes** |
| Star-knob nut | Locks a camera or adapter on the clamp's ball head | Yes |
| GoPro → ¼-inch adapter (knurled cylinder) | Fits the camera onto a GoPro mount | Yes |
| GoPro → ¼-inch flat plate | The same, low-profile | Yes |
| ¼-inch screw → GoPro prongs (×2) | Turns a ¼-inch socket into a GoPro mount | Optional |
| Double-ended screws (×2) and the headless threaded rod | Join two sockets | Optional (easy to lose) |

## Maui packing list
**Bring:**
- both sticks and the mini tripod;
- the short handle;
- the Neewer clamp kit (clamp, arm and magnetic base);
- the cage;
- the hand mount and clip mount;
- the dive case kit (case, weight, anti-fog inserts, bag);
- the spare lenses;
- both batteries, the battery charger and the power banks (in your carry-on);
- the small-parts pouch.

**Leave at home:** the stick-on discs.
