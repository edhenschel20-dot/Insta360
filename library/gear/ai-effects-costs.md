# AI Warp vs AI Effects: what's free, what costs

Two separate generative features exist in the app today, with two
different (and differently documented) free allowances. Knowing which one
you're using is the single most useful thing here.

## AI Warp (Shot Lab)
- Pick a clip (4–15 seconds), apply a preset style or a text prompt, or
  paint over an area.
- **3 free generations per day**, resetting at 8:00 AM Beijing time (check
  the in-app countdown for your local time). After that, each generation
  costs **20 diamonds** (Insta360's in-app currency).
- **Preview button:** you can preview the effect before committing —
  use it before spending a generation, every time.
- **Refunds:** a failed generation is automatically refunded — confirmed in
  the official manual. A generation that renders fine but you just don't
  like isn't refunded (not stated either way, but that's the general
  pattern with AI-generation products).

## "AI Effects" (newer, separate section in the Edit tab)
- A different, more experimental set of generative effects.
- Reportedly limited to **just three uses total** (not per day) at launch —
  more tightly rationed than AI Warp, and its current limit isn't published
  anywhere found. Check the in-app counter to see your actual remaining
  count for whichever feature you're using.

## Why not to buy Insta360+ for this
Insta360+ is a separate subscription (cloud backup, cloud editing, and a
different auto-highlight tool called "Moments"). Its included credits
appear to be for Moments specifically — **there's no confirmed link between
Insta360+ credits and AI Warp/AI Effects generations.** Don't buy a
monthly plan hoping it unlocks more AI Warp generations; the evidence
points to it not applying.

## The cheapest plan
1. **This week, free, 10 minutes:** open the AI Warp/AI Effects screen and
   note the exact remaining-count indicator, and open "Top Up Diamonds"
   once to see the real price list for your region.
2. **Between now and any trip, free:** use the free daily allowance,
   spread across multiple days rather than all at once. Use Preview before
   committing each one.
3. **On the day you want more:** top up diamonds on demand — pay-as-you-go,
   no subscription needed.
4. If you separately want cloud backup of a trip's footage (a different
   want from AI effects), the first-time 30-day free trial of Insta360+ can
   be started before a trip and cancelled before it renews.

## Diamonds
Diamonds are a separate virtual currency from Insta360+ credits; 20
diamonds buys one AI Warp generation beyond the free 3/day. The exact USD
price of a diamond top-up pack isn't published anywhere — check the in-app
"Top Up Diamonds" screen for the real price before budgeting anything.
