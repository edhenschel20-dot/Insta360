# Golf cart mount (stick above the roof)

The camera rides on the Invisible Selfie Stick, just above the cart roof,
for a whole round: a "drone following the cart" view of every hole. The
rules for a smart course like Kapalua: **nothing that marks the cart,
nothing that needs cutting off, on in a minute and off in a minute.** So
there are no zip ties, hose clamps or tape on the cart. Rubber, straps and
knobs only.

The magnetic ball base won't work, because golf cart frames are aluminium
and plastic, so there's nothing for a magnet to stick to.

**What you need:** your clamp and arm (already owned), a piece of rubber for
inside the clamp, two rubber straps, and a safety cord.

1. **Rubber inside the clamp.** Cut a strip from an old bike inner tube,
   about 3 × 10 cm, and lay it inside the clamp's jaws before you tighten
   it. It just sits there: nothing is stuck to the cart, it stops scratches,
   and it grips far better than bare paint.
2. **Clamp low on the front roof post**, about knee to hip height.
3. **Fold the arm up** so the stick stands right beside the post, not out
   to the side.
4. **Two rubber straps** round the stick and the post: one just under the
   roof, one halfway down. They take seconds to put on and off, they're
   stretchy and grippy, and they don't mark paint.
5. **Safety cord** from the stick to the post, short enough that it can't
   reach a wheel. If anything slips, the camera hangs instead of hitting the
   cart path.
6. **Camera 30–50 cm (1–1.5 ft) above the roof.** Any higher, and it whips
   over bumps.

A **double clamp** (also sold as a "selfie stick support clamp") grips the
roof post with one jaw and the selfie stick's body with the other, using
two knobs. It's the fastest on and off of all the options. **Check the
clamp's size range before buying.** Some only open to about 4 cm, which may
be too small for a cart's roof post, so pick one that opens wider. Add one
rubber strap up top and the safety cord, as in Fix 1.

## Fix 3: a shorter arm

If you'd rather keep your current clamp exactly as it is, a **short
double-socket arm** brings the stick much closer to the post. RAM's short
B-size arm is 1.75 in (44 mm) from socket to socket. It only fits if your
balls are **1 inch (25 mm)**, so measure one first. It helps, but Fix 1 or
Fix 2 does more, and you'd still want the strap and the cord.

check it after a few holes.

## At the course
- **Ask at the bag drop:** "OK if I strap a camera to the roof post? Nothing
  sticky, comes straight off." Most courses are fine with it.
- **Test it at home first:** strap it to a lamp post or fence post and shake
  it hard.
- **Check it on the first tee and at the turn**, and re-tighten anything loose.
- **Above the roof, the roof hides you all.** You get the course, not faces.
  That's ideal for the scenery, and it means the app's AI has no faces to
  swing round to. For a reaction shot, take it off for one hole and use the
  normal [Golf Round](../shots/golf-round.md) set-ups.
- Settings: standard Video, 5.7K30, Auto. Full plan on the
  [Golf Round](../shots/golf-round.md) page.
