## The three habits
1. **Let it roll** and enjoy the moment. You'll fix the view later.
2. **Say "Mark that"** when something great happens.
3. **Skip the AI auto-edit for events.** Use [Split + fixed views](editing/split-and-fixed-views.md) or [Deep Track](editing/deep-track.md) instead.
