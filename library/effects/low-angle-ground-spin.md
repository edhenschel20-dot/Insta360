# Low-Angle Ground Spin
A slow, bullet-time-style orbit shot from knee height — no cord, no arm held overhead. You turn on the spot instead of swinging the camera around you.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Hold the stick low, close to the ground.
2. Turn slowly on the spot in a full circle — a tee box or the first green, with the course behind you, works well.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe: linear movement, zoom around 130, tilt up about 10°.
2. Add three keyframes panning clockwise, timed to keep yourself centred in frame as you turn.

## Settings
- Any resolution already shot at.

## Tips
- This is a lower, slower cousin of the standard [Bullet Time](bullet-time.md) spin-on-the-spot variant mentioned there — this version is specifically about the low angle and a golf-course setting.
- Golf etiquette: do this before or after your shot, not while others are waiting to play — it only takes a few seconds but pick a moment that isn't holding anyone up.
