# Night Interval Timelapse (72 MP Stills)
Instead of Timelapse video mode, this shoots a sequence of full 72 MP RAW stills and assembles them afterwards — more dynamic range and detail than any in-camera night video mode.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Photo mode > **Interval**, with PureShot and RAW on, 72 MP.
2. ISO 100, white balance around 6000K. Shutter: auto if you start at sunset, or a fixed ~10 seconds once it's properly dark.
3. Interval 5–10 seconds, tripod, run for 20–60 minutes.

## Edit it (phone app / Studio)
1. This one's heavier than the usual phone-only flow: Studio exports the still sequence, and assembling it into a moving clip is a desktop job (creators use Premiere or similar).
2. Only worth it for a scene you've planned in advance — not a spontaneous grab.

## Settings
- Photo > Interval, PureShot + RAW, 72 MP, ISO 100, WB ~6000K, 5–10 s interval, tripod.
- Full reference: [Timelapse & TimeShift settings](../settings/timelapse-and-timeshift.md) and [Night & low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md).

## Tips
- Gaba's rule of thumb for which night mode to reach for: **PureVideo** = fast, no thinking · **manual video** = predictable light · **Interval stills** = max quality when you can plan the shot · **Starlapse** = things your eyes can't see (star trails). See [Starlapse](starlapse.md) for that fourth option.
- Save this one for a planned scene — a viewpoint over a bay at blue hour, not a family evening you're rolling through.
