# Motion Timelapse

A timelapse where the camera also pans — clouds racing past while the view slowly sweeps across the sky or a crowd.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Timelapse mode, 11K.
2. Interval: 5 seconds for clouds, 2 seconds for crowds.
3. Run it for at least 10–15 minutes — this is one clip that benefits from planting the camera and walking away rather than holding it.

## Edit it (phone app)
Keyframe at the start and end, panning in the direction the clouds (or crowd) are moving.

## Settings
Full reference: [Timelapse & TimeShift settings](../settings/timelapse-and-timeshift.md).

## Tips
- Different from [Hyperlapse/TimeShift](hyperlapse-and-timeshift.md): this is a static Timelapse mode capture with a pan added in the edit, not an in-camera moving hyperlapse.
