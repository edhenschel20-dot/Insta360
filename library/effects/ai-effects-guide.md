# AI Effects Guide

A hub page for getting good results out of the app's generative AI features — AI Warp and AI Effects — before you burn through the free generations you get each day. Read this before you try to make your son "turn into Ghost Rider" or a wave crash over someone.

**Difficulty:** Medium (results are hit-or-miss; budget more than one try) · **Works on X5:** Yes

## What footage it needs
The app is repainting a real clip, not generating one from scratch, so the source footage matters more than it looks like it would:
- **A clear subject in the middle of frame**, filling a good chunk of it — not a tiny figure in a wide landscape.
- **A short clip, 4–15 seconds.** Trim to the moment you actually want transformed before you generate.
- **Steady footage.** Handheld shake or fast camera movement makes it harder for the AI to track what to repaint; a tripod or a steady stick helps a lot.
- **A clean, simple background** tends to give more predictable results than a busy, cluttered one.

**For "transformation" style effects specifically** — like a person running toward the camera and turning into something else (Ed's idea: his son running toward him and becoming "Ghost Rider," flaming skull and chains) — the footage that seems to work best is:
- The subject running or walking **straight toward the camera**, filling more of the frame as they approach.
- Camera held still (tripod, or braced) so the AI only has to track the person, not compensate for camera motion too.
- A clear runway with nothing else moving through the shot.

This same setup — steady camera, subject approaching head-on, clean background — is also the starting point for a "wave crashing over someone" effect via a custom prompt (see below), just swap the subject for someone standing where the wave will "hit."

## Always Preview first
Before you spend a generation, tap **Preview** (bottom-left of the AI Warp screen). It shows you a version of the result before you commit to the full "Make AI video" step. Do this every single time — it's free, and it's the only way to catch a bad result before it costs you a generation.

**A failed generation is automatically refunded.** If the AI Warp task fails outright, the diamonds (or free-generation count) go back — confirmed directly from Insta360's own manual. There's no confirmed refund for a result that completes fine but you just don't like it, so Preview is genuinely your only free look before that point.

## The free limits
- **AI Warp** (Shot Lab): **3 free generations per day**, resetting at 8:00 AM Beijing time (roughly late afternoon/evening the previous day in the US — check the in-app countdown for your exact local reset). After that, each further generation costs 20 diamonds; the real £/$ price of a diamond isn't published anywhere online, so check the in-app "Top Up Diamonds" screen once to see it.
- **AI Effects** (the separate, newer Edit-tab section): reported as limited to **"three total uses"** at its launch — three ever, not per day — and no official source confirms whether that's changed since.
- Screenshot which exact feature name appears when you tap an effect (AI Warp vs AI Effects) — that single check tells you which allowance you're actually spending against, more reliably than anything published online.
- Full cost breakdown, including diamonds and subscriptions: [AI Warp & AI Effects](ai-warp-and-ai-effects.md).

## Writing a custom AI Warp prompt with a painted area
Beyond the built-in presets (Cyberpunk, Sci-Fi, Space, Anime), AI Warp has a **custom prompt / painted-area** mode — this is the route to something like "Ghost Rider" or a crashing wave, since neither is a named preset:

1. Shot Lab > **AI Warp** > pick your clip.
2. Choose the **custom prompt** or **painted-area ("Custom AI effect")** option rather than a preset style.
3. **Paint over the area** you want transformed — e.g. paint over the person for a character transformation, or paint the space in front of them for something like a wave.
4. Type a short, specific text prompt describing what should appear. Keep it concrete and visual rather than abstract.
5. Tap **Preview** before generating.

**Example prompt ideas to try:**
- *"Flaming skull, chains, Ghost Rider style, fire trailing behind"* — painted over a running person, for a transformation effect.
- *"Huge wave crashing over, ocean spray, dramatic"* — painted over the space around/in front of a standing person.
- *"Fire rider, motorbike made of flames"* — for a moving-subject transformation.
- *"Crashing wave, foam and spray, powerful"* — as a standalone environmental effect over open space.
- *"Turning to stone, cracked grey rock texture"* — a slower-reveal transformation idea, works well with a subject that pauses partway through the clip.

Results are hit-or-miss, so treat each attempt as an experiment: if the first prompt doesn't land, tweak the wording (more specific nouns tend to help) and paint a slightly different area before trying again.

## Tips
- Spread your free generations across multiple days rather than burning all 3 in one sitting while you're still learning what works.
- Don't buy an Insta360+ subscription hoping it unlocks more generations — its credits are for a different feature ("Moments"), not AI Warp or AI Effects.
- Keep a note of which prompts worked (or didn't) — since the free allowance is so tight, it's worth not re-learning the same lesson twice.
