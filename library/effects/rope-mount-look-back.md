# Rope-Mount Look-Back
The camera clamps onto the tow rope itself, so the rider being pulled behind the boat stays centred in frame without anyone holding a camera.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Likely (mount is from the X3 era; ¼" thread is standard, should fit the X5)

## Shoot it
1. Fit the Insta360 Water Sports Rope Mount onto a wakeboard tow rope, or a kite line for kite sports.
2. Let it roll for the whole pull — the rider stays roughly centred by the geometry of the rope, no reframing needed live.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe wide to keep the rider comfortably inside the frame even as the rope flexes.

## Adapt it
- **Tubing or a banana boat:** the same mount works clamped to the tow line behind a rental boat.

## Settings
- 5.7K30. Full reference: [Fast motion & driving settings](../settings/fast-motion-and-driving.md).

## Tips
- Good one to leave running for a whole tubing session rather than setting up per-run — nobody needs to hold anything.
