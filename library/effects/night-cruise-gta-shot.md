# Night Cruise GTA Shot
The daytime [Bumper / GTA Chase Shot](bumper-gta-chase-shot.md), done at night: a third-person, video-game-style view of the car gliding under city lights.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Suction-cup mount on the boot or bonnet — the creator here upgraded to a 4-inch cup for extra stability at night, since you're relying on it more than you can see.
2. Drive as for the daytime version; night adds no extra rigging, just different camera settings (below).

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Same reframe as the daytime shot: Direction Lock on, keyframe the car centred, speed to taste.

## Adapt it
- **Hotel-to-dinner drive:** an easy way to get this shot without a dedicated outing.
- **Golf cart at dusk:** works the same at cart speed.

## Settings
- **PureVideo** for the low light. Full reference: [Night & low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md).

## Tips
- See [Bumper / GTA Chase Shot](bumper-gta-chase-shot.md) for the full mounting and Direction Lock/speed recipe — this page only covers what changes at night.
