# Me Mode & FreeFrame

Two Single-Lens capture modes: **Me Mode** points the active lens at the stick holder specifically (a plain selfie, not 360), and **FreeFrame** records single-lens footage you can crop to 16:9 or 9:16 later.

Per this guide's usual approach, Single-Lens modes are **niche only** — they throw away the 360 capture that's the whole point of the X5. Documented here for completeness, not as something to reach for by default.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Mode > **Single-Lens** > **Me Mode** or **FreeFrame**.
2. Me Mode: hold the stick as normal, the active lens faces you.
3. FreeFrame: shoot single-lens footage now, decide the crop orientation later.

## Edit it (phone app)
- Me Mode needs no reframing — it's already a plain selfie shot.
- FreeFrame: choose 16:9 (landscape) or 9:16 (vertical/social) when you export.

## Settings
- No special resolution requirement beyond what Single-Lens mode already offers.

## Tips
- Only reach for these when you specifically don't want the 360 capture at all — e.g. a quick one-off selfie clip you know you'll only ever want in one aspect ratio.
- For everything else — even a simple selfie moment — shooting full 360 and reframing afterwards keeps your options open, which is the whole reason to own this camera.
