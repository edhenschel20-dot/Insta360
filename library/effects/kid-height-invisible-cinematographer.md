# Kid-Height Invisible Cinematographer
Walking with small kids, hold the camera at their eye level instead of up over your own head. High up, you get you and the pavement; low down, the scene — the trees, the parade, the jungle path — shows up behind them properly, and the low angle makes them look bigger and more "in the shot" than they really are.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Hold the stick at hip height beside you rather than raised overhead — imagine an invisible cinematographer walking at the kids' own height, not looking down on them.
2. Angle one lens toward the child and walk half a step behind them.
3. On a kerb or at a parade, the same idea applies sitting down — get low rather than staying at your own standing height.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) the child through the walk.
2. Widen the FOV a touch (distortion around 0.1) for more of the super-wide, "epic" look that a low angle already gives you.

## Settings
- Any standard resolution — this is a holding-height habit, not a camera mode.
