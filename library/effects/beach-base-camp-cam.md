# Beach Base-Camp Cam
Plant the camera at the towels and let it roll the whole beach afternoon — sandcastles, the run to the water, the return — with nobody assigned to hold it.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Stand a Throwpod (or stick plus mini tripod) at the towels — no camera person needed. The family comes and goes around it.
2. Raise it to about 1.1m so heads aren't cut off by the horizon line.
3. Point the lenses toward the sea and the towels, not along the length of the beach.
4. This is a 60–90 minute roll: start with a full battery and the big card.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Find the good bits with Highlights or AI Director rather than scrubbing the whole clip — it's genuinely worth checking what it caught, since it can surface things nobody noticed at the time.
2. Say "mark" during the session at anything worth finding faster later — see [Hands-Off Triggers](hands-off-triggers.md).

## Tips
- Pair this with a stick that always carries its mini tripod on the bottom (see [Invisible Selfie Stick Shot](invisible-selfie-stick.md)) so it takes ten seconds to plant, not a proper setup.
- This is as much an everyday-life habit as a beach one — the same plant-and-roll idea works at a park or in the backyard.
