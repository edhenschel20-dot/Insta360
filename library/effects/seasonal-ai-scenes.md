# Seasonal AI Scenes

Insta360 periodically adds limited-time seasonal entries to Sky Swap and AI Warp — holiday skies and styles that come and go rather than staying in the app permanently.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## See it
_No verified example video yet. Add one with the "Add a video" form._

## What footage it needs
- For a seasonal **Sky Swap**: the same as regular Sky Swap — open space, plenty of visible sky, 15+ seconds of footage.
- For a seasonal **AI Warp** style: a 4–15 second clip, same as any other AI Warp generation.

## Cost
- **Free Sky Swap doesn't use your AI Warp generation allowance** — it's a separate, non-generative feature.
- A seasonal **AI Warp** style pack, by contrast, is still AI Warp — it counts against the same 3-free-per-day allowance as any other AI Warp style. See [AI Warp & AI Effects](ai-warp-and-ai-effects.md) for the full cost breakdown.
- Always tap **Preview** first; a failed generation is refunded.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Shot Lab > **Sky Swap** or **AI Warp** — seasonal entries appear alongside the regular options for a limited time (typically around a holiday).
2. Pick the seasonal entry, Preview, then generate if it's an AI Warp style.

## Tips
- These are time-limited — if you spot one you like, use it while it's there rather than assuming it'll still be around next time.
- Check Shot Lab around major holidays (Halloween, Christmas, etc.) for what's currently available.
