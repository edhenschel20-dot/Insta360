# Bullet Time

The world spins around you in slow motion, Matrix-style.

**Difficulty:** Easy–Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. This is a deliberate, short clip on purpose — one of the few effects where a quick, planned take beats rolling footage.
2. Use Bullet Time mode (5.7K120).
3. Swing the camera overhead on the Bullet Time handle or cord at roughly one rotation per second — no faster. Try to keep one lens up and one down as it swings.
4. Also works: hold the stick out horizontally and spin on the spot yourself, or swing the camera around a friend who stays still (they become the orbited subject).
5. Good light only — low light forces a slower shutter at 120fps, which shows as blur.

## Edit it (phone app)
The camera auto-centres you as you export — open the clip, trim the lead-in/lead-out, done.
