# Aircraft / Gondola Window Shot
Mount the camera centred in a cabin window — a gondola, cable car, or small aircraft — and add a slow zoom-out as the view opens up outside.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Likely (in-cabin X5 demos exist for gondola and small aircraft)

## Shoot it
1. Clamp or suction-mount the camera inside the cabin, centred in the window frame.
2. **Never mount it outside an open helicopter door** — ask the operator what's allowed, and keep anything you rig tethered.
3. On a doors-off tour specifically, this is entirely down to the operator's rules — ask before assuming you can mount anything.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keep the frame centred on the window/view.
2. Add a zoom-out keyframe as the view opens up (taking off, climbing, or the gondola clearing the treeline).

## Settings
- [Bright sun & snow settings](../settings/bright-sun-and-snow.md) generally applies at altitude in daylight.

## Tips
- See [Aerial & Helicopter](../shots/aerial-and-helicopter.md) for the rest of the setup/safety notes for a flight day.
- The zoom-out is what turns a static window shot into something that feels like a reveal — a locked-off frame with no zoom reads flat by comparison.
