# Slow-Mo Particle Shot

Water spray, sand, snow or bubbles passing the camera on all sides at a quarter speed — the 360 lens catches particles you'd normally only see from one angle.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Shoot 4K120 (or 5.7K60 if you want more resolution and can accept less slow-mo).
2. Get right into the spray, sand, snow or bubbles — close enough that particles pass on multiple sides of the lens, not just in front.
3. Let the camera roll normally; there's no need to stop and start for this.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Find the moment in the timeline, then use the lightning-bolt speed tool.
2. Drag the speed handle down to around ¼ speed over the particle moment.
3. Reframe afterwards if you want to follow a specific person through the spray.

## Settings
- 4K120 fps gives the smoothest slow motion; 5.7K60 is the fallback if you want more detail and can live with 2x instead of 4x.
- Full reference: [Bullet time & slow motion settings](../settings/bullet-time-and-slow-motion.md).

## Tips
- Works well for kids running through sprinklers, wave splashback, or a sandy kick-up at the beach.
- Because it's full 360, you don't need to aim — just get in among the particles and pick the angle later.
