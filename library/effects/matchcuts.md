# MatchCuts

An automatic match cut: do the same gesture (a clap, a click, a hand swipe across the lens) in two different clips, and the app finds and cuts on that moment for you. Rated A-tier by Ben Claremont.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Check app version — the manual lists this as Android-only and for flat (non-360) video

## Shoot it
1. Pick a clear, repeatable gesture: a clap, a finger click, a high-five, or swiping a hand across the lens.
2. Do the same gesture at the start or end of each clip you want to join, in two different locations or moments.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Edit > Shot Lab > **MatchCuts**.
2. Pick your two clips — the app looks for the matching gesture and cuts on it automatically.
3. If the auto-cut misses, you can always fall back to cutting manually right before the gesture in each clip.

## Settings
- Shoot with sound on — the app appears to use the audio spike (clap/click) as part of its detection.

## Tips
- The manual notes this feature is Android-only and works on flat (non-360) video — if you're on iPhone or want to keep the full 360 sphere, do it manually instead: export both clips flat, cut just before the gesture in each, and join them in any video editor.
- Good for kids' games or repeated actions (jumping, high-fives) across different spots on a day out.
