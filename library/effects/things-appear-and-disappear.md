# Things appear and disappear (which trick is it?)

Those fast-motion clips where people pop in and out, or you stand frozen
while the world rushes past, are made in one of four ways. None of them
needs masking. Pick the look you saw:

| The look | The trick | How hard |
|---|---|---|
| **You stand still, the street rushes past you** | Shot Lab **Street Lapse** | Easy |
| **Crowds flicker in and out** of a busy spot | A **Timelapse-mode** clip from a planted camera | Easy |
| **Copies of you or the kids** appear round the camera | Shot Lab **Clone Trail / Clone Loop / Shadow Clone** | Easy–Medium |
| **A person or car vanishes** mid-walk | A **jump cut** in a sped-up clip | Easy |

All four need **a camera that doesn't move**, or a steady walk for the last
one. Use the **2-in-1 stick and tripod**. See [My Kit](../gear/my-kit.md).

## 1. You frozen, the street rushing past (Street Lapse)
Two clips from the same unmoved spot: about 20 seconds with you standing
still, then 1–3 minutes without you. **Video mode, not Timelapse.** Full
steps: [Street Lapse & Flash Dash](street-lapse-and-flash-dash.md).

## 2. Crowds flickering in and out (Timelapse)
1. Stand the camera somewhere busy: a town corner, the luau entrance as
   people arrive, a harbour while the boats load.
2. Switch to **Timelapse** mode, with an interval of 0.5–2 seconds, and leave
   it for 5–10 minutes.
3. Anyone walking through is only in a frame or two, so they flicker in and
   out. Anyone standing still stays put. Stand still yourself, and you're
   the one solid thing in a blur of people.

See [Hyperlapse & TimeShift](hyperlapse-and-timeshift.md).

## 3. Copies of you appearing (clone templates)
- **Clone Trail:** walk or jog round the camera about 2 m away, at a steady
  speed. See [Clone Trail](clone-trail.md).
- **Shadow Clone:** stand in 3–4 clearly separate spots for 10–15 seconds
  each, without overlapping. **Clone Loop:** walk one full circle round the
  camera at a steady pace. See
  [Clone Loop & Shadow Clone](clone-loop-and-shadow-clone.md).
- Kids love these. Give each one a "spot" and a silly pose.

## 4. Someone vanishes mid-walk (jump cut)
1. Film a steady walk, or a planted camera with people moving through it.
   Normal Video.
2. In the edit, speed it up 8–16×, then use **Trim** to cut out a second or
   two right as someone passes. They're there, then they're gone.

See [Find your way round the app](../editing/find-your-way-round-the-app.md)
for where Trim lives.

## Why it usually goes wrong
- **The camera got nudged** between clips, or mid-clip. Plant it and don't
  touch it.
- **Too short.** Street Lapse needs 1–3 minutes without you, and a
  timelapse needs 5–10 minutes.
- **Wrong mode:** Street Lapse won't take Timelapse-mode clips. Use Video.
- **Dark clothes on a dark background** (Street Lapse). Wear something light.
- **Leaving the app** while it's recognising people. Do these edits in the
  evening, with the phone on charge.

## A hand that peels someone off (or throws something on)
That's an in-app AI effect, not a timelapse trick. See
[Peel off / stick on](ai-effects-guide.md#peel-off-stick-on-a-hand-removes-or-adds-something)
in the AI Effects Guide.
