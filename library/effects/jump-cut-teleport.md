# Jump-Cut Teleport & Outfit Swap
Jump up in one spot and land in a completely different location — or run out of frame in one outfit and back in wearing another. Same trick as the "teleport" trend: a locked-off camera and a clean split.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Camera on a tripod, or the stick held completely steady — it must not move between takes.
2. Jump version: jump, and stop the take at the top of the jump (or just as you leave frame).
3. Outfit-swap version: run out of frame, change, run back in — repeat for each outfit.
4. This is an X2-era demo, but the technique is unchanged on the X5; a desktop version of the same idea is also documented on the Insta360 forum.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Narrow the FOV down to something closer to flat video — the teleport illusion works better without the full 360 sphere distracting from the join.
2. Split each clip either at the top of the jump, or the moment you leave frame.
3. Delete the in-between frames and butt the clips together directly — no transition effect, just a hard cut.

## Settings
- Any resolution — a locked-off, unmoving camera position is what actually matters here.

## Tips
- Works great as a fun one with kids: teleporting between rooms, or "changing" from swimwear to clothes between the pool and the car, cut cleanly.
- The camera must not move at all between setups — even a small nudge on the tripod will show as a jump in the background.
