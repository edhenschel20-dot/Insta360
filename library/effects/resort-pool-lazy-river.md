# Resort Pool & Lazy River Float-Along
The camera drifts round a lazy river on the tube with you, or covers the whole pool from the sun-lounger while it rolls.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

**[general advice]** — the example clips are raw X5 footage with no tutorial behind them; the technique here is built from the existing water pages.

## Shoot it
1. **Lazy river:** X5 on the short stick, resting on the tube in front of you, or on a floaty handle held loosely. Let it roll the full loop — the world drifts past on all sides and the kids float in and out of shot without you doing anything.
2. **Pool:** stick in the mini tripod at the lounger, lenses toward the pool, let it roll — see [Beach Base-Camp Cam](beach-base-camp-cam.md) for the same idea applied to a towel spot.
3. **Water parks:** sticks are usually banned on slides. Use a chest strap or a folding grip case instead if the venue won't allow a stick.

## Tips
- The lazy river version genuinely needs no camera operator — that's the whole point, so don't hold it tightly or try to aim it.
- Check the venue's stick policy before you get in the water with one.
