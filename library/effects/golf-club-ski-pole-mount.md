# Golf-Club / Ski-Pole Mount Shot
The camera rides on the club shaft or ski pole itself, stick sticking out sideways, so a practice swing (or a turn on the slope) is filmed from the club's or pole's own point of view.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Likely (the Pole Mount itself is a generic clamp; using it on a golf club is a creator suggestion, not an official Insta360 use)

## Shoot it
1. Clamp the Insta360 Pole Mount (a ski/hiking pole clamp with a ¼" thread) onto the club shaft, then screw a short stick into it.
2. Practice swings only, on the range or an empty part of the tee — not with a ball in play mid-round.
3. Keep it well clear of your own or anyone else's stance and swing path.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe to the lens looking back along the shaft at you and the ball.
2. Slow the clip through impact (0.5x or so) if you want a technique-check look — see [Form Check Slow-Mo](form-check-slow-mo.md) for a more complete multi-angle version of this.

## Settings
- Standard Video, 5.7K30 is plenty for a static range shot.

## Tips
- Golf etiquette: this is a range/warm-up shot, not something to rig up mid-round — don't hold up the group behind you, and keep the mount out of anyone else's line.
- MrBlack's demo used a caged X3, not a bare X5; check whatever case you use still clears the clamp before swinging anything with force nearby.
- For skiing, the same clamp goes straight onto a ski pole — treat that as a "for later" note.
