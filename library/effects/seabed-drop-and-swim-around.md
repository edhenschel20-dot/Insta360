# Seabed Drop & Swim-Around
Drop the camera on the seabed and swim around it — it sits like a parked drone, hands-free, while fish and divers circle past.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. In the dive case, set the X5 down on sand or a stable patch of seabed — either resting flat, or on a small tripod built into the case.
2. Or use the **Dive Buddy** handle: it has adjustable weights for neutral buoyancy and a ¼" screw fitting for its own 3-leg stand, so it sits level without you holding it.
3. Swim around it, or leave it running and swim off — fish are often drawn to the camera's flares and will approach on their own.
4. **Not for currents** — the camera will drift away if the water is moving.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe onto whatever passes closest, or let it run and pick the best moment afterwards — the long, hands-free take is the point.

## Settings
- Dive Case Mode on. Full reference: [Underwater diving settings](../settings/underwater-diving.md).

## Tips
- This lets you keep both hands free and keep enjoying the dive rather than holding the camera the whole time.
- Remember the dive-case battery can't be swapped mid-dive — this is a good one to save for early in a tank when charge is highest.
