# 360 Photo Animate & AI Layout

Two photo-editing additions from app version 2.29.0 (July 2026):
- **Animate:** turns one 360 photo into a moving clip by applying a one-tap camera move within the still image.
- **AI Layout:** suggests framing/crop options for a 360 still automatically.

This "Animate" feature is one half of the photo-to-photo spin transition — see [Spin Transition](spin-transition.md) for how it's combined with a cut into another animated still.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Take a 360 photo as normal — no special setup needed.
2. Photos with a clear subject in the middle, or a group arranged in a circle, tend to animate more convincingly than a busy or cluttered scene.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Open a 360 photo > tap **Animate** > choose a camera movement from the preset options.
2. For **AI Layout**, look for automatic framing suggestions when viewing or exporting a 360 still (menu names vary by app version).

## Settings
- No special capture settings — this works on any 360 photo you've already taken.

## Tips
- Combine with a transition (see [Spin Transition](spin-transition.md)) to cut this animated still into another animated still or clip for the "photo spins into the next photo" look.
- If the animated result looks odd, try a photo with a clearer single subject rather than a busy group or landscape.
