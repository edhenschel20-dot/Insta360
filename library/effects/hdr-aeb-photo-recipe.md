# HDR / AEB Photo Recipe
Two ways to get more dynamic range out of a 360 still, for sunsets and bright beach scenes where a single exposure blows out the sky or crushes the shadows.

**Difficulty:** Medium (choosing between the two modes needs a moment's thought; shooting itself is one tap) · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
Pick one:
1. **HDR photo + PureShot + RAW, 72 MP** — Ben's usual pick. Studio merges the exposure brackets automatically afterwards, so it's minimal fuss at shoot time.
2. **AEB (Auto Exposure Bracketing)** — 3 to 7 frames, up to 4 stops apart at 3 frames or 1.3 stops apart at 7. More manual control over the blend, but capped at 18 MP rather than 72 MP.

## Edit it (phone app / Studio)
1. HDR+RAW: open in Studio, it merges the brackets on its own — nothing to do by hand.
2. AEB: merge the bracketed frames in Studio, adjusting the blend if any highlights or shadows look off.
3. For night stills specifically, watch for ghosting around anyone moving during a longer exposure.

## Settings
Full reference: [Photo settings](../settings/photos.md).

## Tips
- Default to HDR+RAW at 72 MP for most sunset and bright-beach shots — it's the simpler workflow and the higher resolution.
- Reach for AEB only when you want more manual control over the blend than the automatic HDR merge gives you.
