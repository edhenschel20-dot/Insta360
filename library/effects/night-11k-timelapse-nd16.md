# Night 11K Timelapse with ND16
A night timelapse where traffic and pedestrians blur smoothly with no flicker — the trick is locking the shutter rather than leaving it on auto.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Timelapse mode, 11K, 2 second interval (the fastest interval available at 11K).
2. Set the shutter manually to **1 second — half the interval** — and fit an ND16 filter even though it's night. Then fine-tune brightness with ISO, not shutter.
3. The fixed shutter also stops LCD signs and streetlights flickering between frames, which a fast auto-shutter can't avoid.
4. Mount on the 3 m stick standing in a tripod or Throwpod for an ultra-tall vantage point over the street.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe freely — 11K gives enough resolution for a big zoom or pan without it looking soft.

## Settings
- 11K, 2 s interval, manual shutter 1 s, ND16, ISO to taste. Full reference: [Timelapse & TimeShift settings](../settings/timelapse-and-timeshift.md) and [Night & low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md).

## Tips
- "Shutter = half the interval" is the rule to remember for any night timelapse — it's what keeps motion blur smooth instead of stuttery.
- See also [City Light-Trail Drive-Lapse](city-light-trail-drive-lapse.md), which uses the same shutter/interval relationship from inside a car.
