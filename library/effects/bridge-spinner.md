# Bridge Spinner
From a bridge or lookout rail, the camera rises from below the family up past them while spinning the stick. In the edit, keeping them centred turns the raw footage into a corkscrewing "drone" move.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Use a long stick. One person stands on the bridge or at the rail.
2. Move the X5 from below the person up past them while spinning the stick, in one continuous motion.
3. Family version: Ed on the pier or a footbridge, kids at the rail, stick coming up from the water side — this also works as an ending into [Over-the-Edge Selfie](over-the-edge-selfie-slow-rotation.md).

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe so the person stays centred through the whole rise-and-spin — [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) works well here since the framing needs to track them continuously through the motion.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or higher — no special mode needed.

## Tips
- This is a cousin of [Stick Freestyle / Windmill Orbit](stick-freestyle-windmill-orbit.md), but with a deliberate start-below/end-above path, which is what makes it read as a drone corkscrew rather than just a wobbly wave of the stick.
- Works well at the end of a scenic walk — a waterfall footbridge, a pier, any rail with a view behind the person.
