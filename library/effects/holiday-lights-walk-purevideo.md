# Holiday Lights Walk (PureVideo)
Walking a neighbourhood, a botanic-garden lights trail, or a Halloween-decorated street after dark, with the kids' faces lit by the displays as you pass them.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. **PureVideo 5.7K30, EV −0.7** — see [Night & low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md).
2. Hold the stick at kid height, not over your head — see [Kid-Height Invisible Cinematographer](kid-height-invisible-cinematographer.md).
3. Walk slowly, and stop at each big display for about 10 seconds so a fixed view works cleanly on that section.
4. Works the same way for a Halloween decorations walk as it does for Christmas lights.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Fixed forward view for the walking sections.
2. [Split Screen / MultiView](split-screen-multiview.md) with the kids' faces at the best display.
3. A [Motion Timelapse](motion-timelapse.md) of a longer trail if you walked a lot of ground.

## Settings
- PureVideo 5.7K30, EV −0.7.

## Tips
- This is the on-foot version of [Illuminations Roof-Mount Drive-Past](christmas-lights-roof-mount-drive-past.md), which is the car version of the same idea — use whichever fits how you're seeing the display.
- Night settings from this page also carry over to a torch-lit fire show or luau — see the [Night & low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md) page.
