# Stop Motion

Turns a steady walk into a stop-motion-style animation of you moving forward, static, or backward.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Check app version (menu names vary by app version)

## Shoot it
1. 5.7K30, stick at full length.
2. Walk at an even pace for at least 2 minutes so the AI has enough footage to learn your pose.

## Edit it (phone app)
Edit > Shot Lab > **Stop Motion**, long-press yourself in the preview, then pick one of the three motion styles offered.

## Settings
- 5.7K30, stick fully extended, even walking pace throughout.

## Tips
- Consistent pace matters more than speed — the AI needs a repeatable pose to work from.
