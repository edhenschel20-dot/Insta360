# CineLapse

An automatic hyperlapse template: the app picks out the "interesting" parts of a walk or drive, speeds them up, and can transition into a second clip on its own.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Any straight-ish walk, drive or bike ride works — no special setup needed, just let the camera roll as usual.
2. Longer clips give the AI more to choose from.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Edit > Shot Lab > **CineLapse** > Use this theme > pick your clip.
2. The app analyses it, applies speed changes automatically, and can cut into a second clip you provide.

## Settings
- No special camera settings — works from normal footage.

## Tips
- This is the "let the AI drive" version. If it moves around too much or picks the wrong moments, the manual [Hyperlapse / TimeShift](hyperlapse-and-timeshift.md) page gives you full control over speed and keyframes instead.
- Good as a first pass on travel footage — quick to try, easy to redo manually if you don't like the result.
