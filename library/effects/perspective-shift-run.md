# Perspective-Shift Run & Follow Perspective
One single take — a run, a walk, a ride — is cut into segments, and each segment is given a different pan angle in the edit. It reads as several cameras (front, your face, left, right) even though only one clip was ever shot. A second version, Follow Perspective, walks or rides behind a subject and reconfigures direction, FOV and speed like a one-take movie.

**Difficulty:** Medium (needs a bit of planning about where the cuts fall) · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Nothing special at shoot time — run, walk, or follow the kids normally with the stick up, and let it roll.
2. A longer, steady take gives you more to cut up afterwards.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. **Perspective-Shift Run:** split the clip into 3–4 short segments. Give each one a different pan — forward, on your own face, hard left, hard right — timed to the same pace, so it reads as cutting between different camera angles.
2. **Follow Perspective:** walk or ride behind the subject (a child running ahead, say) and reconfigure the reframe — direction, FOV, and clip speed — as if directing a one-take film, rather than a single fixed follow shot.
3. For a longer continuous take used the same way, see [Finding moments in long clips](../editing/finding-moments-in-long-clips.md) ("The Long Take").

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K30 — no special mode needed.

## Tips
- Works well on a golf-course walk between shots, or chasing the kids around a garden — anywhere you'd otherwise just have one long boring walking clip.
- Golf etiquette: keep this to the walk between shots, not during someone's swing, and don't let editing plans slow down your pace of play.
