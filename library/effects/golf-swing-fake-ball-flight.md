# Golf Swing + Fake Ball Flight
Two shots cut together: the real swing, then a fairway walk sped up into a fake "ball flight" that lands where the drive would have gone.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## See it

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_ZB5rn8N_4&t=502s) · Gimbal Guru

## Shoot it
1. **Shot 1 — the swing:** extended stick, positioned safely to the side (see [Golf](../shots/golf.md) for the etiquette/setup notes), capture the actual swing.
2. **Shot 2 — the "flight":** walk the fairway afterward, moving the camera in a low-high-low arc as you go, roughly following the line the ball would have taken.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Speed up shot 2 into a hyperlapse.
2. Add a roll keyframe near the end, timed to where you want the "ball" to land.
3. Cut from the swing straight into the hyperlapse.

## Settings

## Tips
- The simpler version of the swing itself is a mouth-mount POV — see [Mouth-Mount / Bite POV](mouth-mount-bite-pov.md) — if you just want the real swing without the fake-flight add-on.
