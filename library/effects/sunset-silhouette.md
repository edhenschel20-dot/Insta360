# Sunset Silhouette (Manual Exposure)
You and the kids as black shapes against the sunset sky — the manual-exposure version of the effect, for video or a tiny-planet photo.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Swipe from the right, tap M, and set shutter/ISO for the sky rather than for the people — that's what makes the subject go black. Easiest right at sunrise or sunset.
2. Photo version: manual mode, fast shutter, lowest ISO, expose for the horizon, then use the 5-second self-timer so you can get into frame.
3. Everyone stands on a raised line — a beach berm, a low wall — between the camera and the sun for the family version, camera kept low.
4. Manual mode also freezes motion — a jump, a bird crossing the frame — that auto exposure would otherwise blur at dusk light levels.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Export a [Tiny Planet](tiny-planet-composition.md) that's mostly black sky/ground with your silhouette on the rim.
2. For video, no special reframe is needed beyond keeping the horizon roughly level.

## Settings
Full reference: [Sunset and golden hour](../settings/sunset.md) has the auto-EV shortcut (−0.7 to −1.3); this page is the manual version of the same idea for when auto doesn't nail it.

## Tips
- Manual is worth the extra step specifically when auto keeps trying to brighten the people back up — that's the sign to switch.
- A jump at the exact moment of exposure is a nice beat to add, since manual mode won't blur it the way auto might in low light.
