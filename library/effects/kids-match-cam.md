# Kids' Match Cam (Pitch-Side Placement & the Overheating Fix)
One clamped camera covers the whole football pitch, basketball court, or baseball diamond for an entire game — no tripod to trip over, and a fix for the camera cooking in a hot gym on a long tournament day.

**Difficulty:** Medium (worth setting up the cooling fix before a hot indoor season) · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. **Basketball / indoor court:** clamp on the scoring table at centre court — never a tripod, since people trip over it and stray balls hit it.
2. **Football / outdoor pitch:** mid-pitch on the touchline, at the halfway line, is the equivalent spot.
3. Games run 35–40 minutes: carry two 128 GB+ cards and a quick card reader, and swap batteries between games rather than mid-game.
4. **Overheating fix for a hot gym:** a small USB fan (around 40 mm) in a simple sleeve clipped near the camera stops it overheating in a warm indoor space during a long tournament day.
5. Reduce distortion in-app so both goals/ends of the pitch stay in frame on a wide-field sport.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Start with one fixed, wide keyframe covering the whole pitch or court.
2. Use the record-button style single-take export for one fast break or a specific play, or run it through the editor for a full highlights cut.
3. For two ends of a pitch or two courts at once, [Split Screen / MultiView](split-screen-multiview.md) covers both without re-clamping.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K30 so there's enough resolution to zoom in on a specific play afterwards.
- Reduce distortion for wide-field sports (football-sized pitches).

## Tips
- This is the same "plant it and forget it" idea as [Court-Side "Kitchen Cam"](courtside-kitchen-cam.md) (pickleball), scaled up to a full pitch and a whole season of games.
- The cooling-fan fix matters most for back-to-back indoor games in summer — worth setting up before the first hot tournament, not after the camera's already overheated once.
