# Rise Shot (Crane)

The camera lifts from ground level up past you into the sky — a jib/crane move for the cost of raising a stick.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. This is a "when you want a hero shot" extra: pick a moment, extend the stick fully.
2. Start with the camera near the ground, then raise it smoothly overhead in one motion. Having something on the left/right of frame (trees, a doorway, people) adds depth.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe at the start (low) and the end (overhead), let the app interpolate the lift.
2. Optional: change FOV between the two keyframes (Mega to Dewarp, or the reverse) for extra depth on the move.

## Settings
- 8K or 5.7K30, whatever you're already shooting.

## Tips
- Works as an opener or closer for a longer edit — it reads as a title-card move.
- Pairs well with [Rotating Reveal](rotating-reveal.md) if you turn to face something as you rise.
