# Mic the Kid (Wireless Mic + Deep Track)
A lapel mic taped to a child's chest captures their own voice — the excitement, the commentary, the first "I did it" — instead of just wind and ambient noise from the camera's own mic. Works for skiing, a sports practice, a playground session, or any activity where the kid does the talking.

**Difficulty:** Medium (one extra thing to fit and pair before you start) · **Works on X5:** Yes (pairs with one DJI mic, or two Mic Pros)

## Shoot it
1. Tape a lapel mic to the child's chest — a Mic Pro, or a Bluetooth DJI mic (the X5 pairs with one DJI mic, or two Mic Pros for two kids).
2. Mount the X5 on the stick or a pole/mount for the activity — a ski pole mount, a stick at the child's face height, whatever fits what you're doing.
3. Shoot 5K24 or your usual high-bitrate mode, wind reduction on, full 360 as always.
4. **Don't stop recording all day.** The mic doesn't always reconnect cleanly after a stop, and it's easy to forget to restart — let it roll through the whole session instead of starting and stopping between moments.
5. Stick placement matters for audio too as well as picture: keep it around the child's face height rather than held high over your head, or you lose both the framing and the sense that the mic is close to them.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) on the child through the clip — do this on the way home or on the lift/car ride, not later, so you don't lose the moment.
2. Kids often go quiet while concentrating — the best audio is usually on the walk or ride back afterwards, so don't cut the clip too early.
3. Send the finished clip to the other parent (or the family group) before the next round starts, while it's still fresh.

## Settings
- 5K24, high bitrate, wind reduction on.
- Mic pairing: one DJI mic, or up to two Mic Pro units, via the app's audio settings (menu names vary by app version).

## Tips
- This turns any activity — skiing, a match, a playground visit — into something with the child's own voice on it, not just yours narrating from behind the camera.
- Works well alongside [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](slow-motion-tracked-run.md) or [Kid-Height Invisible Cinematographer](kid-height-invisible-cinematographer.md) for the picture half of the same clip.
