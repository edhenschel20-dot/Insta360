# 360 Look-Around (Slow Pan)

A slow, deliberate pan around the environment while you keep moving — good for "here's where we are" moments.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Just walk normally, stick raised. Nothing to do at shoot time — this comes entirely from footage you already have rolling.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Two keyframes with different pan angles, with a long gap of several seconds between them so the move reads as slow, not a whip.
2. Use an ease-in/ease-out curve on the segment between them.

## Settings
- Whatever resolution you're already shooting — no special mode.

## Tips
- This is one of the best uses of long, rolling footage: pick any calm stretch and pan across it later, rather than trying to plan a pan while it's happening.
