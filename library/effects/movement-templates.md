# Movement Templates (One-Tap Camera Moves)
The Keyframe editor has a library of 40+ preset camera moves, grouped into categories (Tiny Planet, Protagonist, Advanced, Highlights) — tap one onto a section of your clip instead of hand-placing keyframes.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes (menu names vary by app version)

## Shoot it
1. No special shooting needed — these apply to footage you already have. Just let the camera roll as normal.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Open the clip's Keyframe editor and go to the Movement tab.
2. Browse the categories: **Tiny Planet**, **Protagonist**, **Advanced**, **Highlights**.
3. Tap a template to apply it to the currently highlighted section — drag the section's edge to lengthen or shorten how long the move plays out.
4. Some confirmed names worth trying: **360 Left**, **Drag Down**, **Interstellar** (a barrel-roll tiny-planet move under Tiny Planet), **Pulse**, **Zoom In/Out**, **Roll**, **Pan**, **Push**, **People Swap**, **Freeze Go** (see [Freeze Go](freeze-go.md) for that one specifically).
5. The full list isn't published anywhere and changes between app versions — scroll through what's currently offered rather than planning around a fixed list.

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at.

## Tips
- Try a template first before hand-placing keyframes — most of the moves elsewhere in this library (rise shots, dolly zooms, tiny planets) may already exist as a one-tap version here.
- **People Swap** is worth checking whether it counts as a generative AI effect (and so uses a limited allowance) before relying on it — see [AI Warp & AI Effects](ai-warp-and-ai-effects.md) for how to check.
