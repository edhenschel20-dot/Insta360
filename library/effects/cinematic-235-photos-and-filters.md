# Cinematic 2.35:1 Photos & Filters

A set of single-lens photo extras added in the winter 2025 firmware (v1.7.43): a wide 2.35:1 cinematic photo format, decorative style borders, and 8 in-camera filter looks (including one called "NC Film").

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes, firmware v1.7.43 or later

## Shoot it
1. Photo mode > Single-Lens > pick the **2.35:1** cinematic aspect ratio for a wide, letterboxed still.
2. Browse the 8 in-camera filter looks (menu names vary by app version) and pick one before or after the shot, depending on the app version.

## Edit it (phone app)
- Style borders can be added to any photo afterwards in the app's photo editing screen.
- Filters can usually be applied or changed after the fact too — check both in-camera and in-app options.

## Settings
- Requires firmware v1.7.43 or later.

## Tips
- The 2.35:1 crop is single-lens, so it's a "decide at shoot time" choice — for scenic shots you're not sure about, take a normal 360 photo as well so you can crop to 2.35:1 (or anything else) later instead of committing in-camera.
