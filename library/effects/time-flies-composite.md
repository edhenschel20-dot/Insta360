# Time Flies (Real-Time You + Timelapse Sky)

You standing still in real time while clouds race overhead — two clips composited into one.

**Difficulty:** Hard (desktop compositing) · **Works on X5:** Yes, but needs a desktop NLE

## See it

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZx1yj1pUvA&t=1093s) · Gimbal Guru

## Shoot it
1. Clip A: normal video of the scene with you in it, camera locked off.
2. Clip B: a timelapse from the exact same spot and framing (see [Motion Timelapse](motion-timelapse.md)).

## Edit it (desktop only)
In a desktop NLE (not the phone app), luma-key the sky out of clip A and drop clip B's timelapse sky in behind it.

## Settings
- Match resolution and framing between the two clips as closely as possible — this is the part that makes or breaks the composite.

## Tips
- This is the one effect in the library that genuinely needs desktop software, not the phone app — plan for it, don't expect a quick phone edit.
