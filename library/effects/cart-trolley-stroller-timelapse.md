# Cart / Trolley / Stroller Direction-Lock Timelapse
A shopping trolley, pushchair, luggage trolley, or golf cart glides through a place in timelapse, always facing forward no matter which way it turns.

**Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Mount the camera low on the cart — a flexible tube or a simple clamp both work.
2. Timelapse mode, 11K, 2 second interval. Fit an ND16 filter in bright light.
3. Turn on **Direction Lock** so the view stays pointed forward through every corner, rather than swinging with the cart's turns.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Little reframing needed — Direction Lock does most of the work.
2. Worth filming the same route twice: once locked forward, once locked backward on you, for a two-shot pair in the edit.

## Settings
- Timelapse 11K, 2 s interval, Direction Lock on, ND16 in bright light. Full reference: [Timelapse & TimeShift settings](../settings/timelapse-and-timeshift.md).

## Tips
- Different from [Direction-Lock Carlapse](direction-lock-carlapse.md): that page shoots normal video and speeds it up afterwards in the edit; this one shoots directly in Timelapse mode, which suits a slower-moving cart or trolley better than a car.
- An easy one to set up once at the start of a supermarket run, theme park queue, or golf round, then forget about.
