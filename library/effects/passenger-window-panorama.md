# Passenger-Window Panorama
Hold a stick out of a train, bus, or stopped car window and sweep the reframe from the front of the vehicle round to the view outside.

**Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Hold the 3 m (or normal) stick fully out of the window, kept still.
2. **Safety:** only where nothing can hit the stick — a train, an open-top bus, or a car stopped at a lookout. **Never at road speed.**

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe every 2 seconds: front of the vehicle › side › back › the view (a lake, mountains, coastline) › hold.

## Settings
- No special mode — a normal 360 clip, reframed with several keyframes.

## Tips
- This only works because the stick stays still — any wobble at speed both risks the equipment and looks worse than the same shot done from a stop.
- Pairs well as a change of pace alongside [Direction-Lock Carlapse](direction-lock-carlapse.md) for a longer road-trip stretch.
