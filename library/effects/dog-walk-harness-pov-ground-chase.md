# Dog Walk: Harness POV & Ground-Level Chase
What the dog sees on its own walk, plus the low "run straight at the camera" shot from the ground — two easy wins from the same outing.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes (Pet Harness has a ¼" mount base)

## Shoot it
1. **Harness POV:** fit the Insta360 × PGYTECH Pet Harness, X5 on the anti-tilt base (add the riser if your dog is small enough that the lenses would catch its back), screen facing backwards so the stitch seam runs along the spine, and tighten the padded straps snugly.
2. Expect the dog to sulk or freeze the first time it's worn — fit it at home for a few minutes over a few days before the first real walk, with treats.
3. **Ground chase:** put the camera on the ground (mini tripod, low and stable) and get someone to call the dog so it runs straight at the lenses from a distance.
4. **Other quick pet shots from the same walk**, all standard stick/mount tricks: a pendant POV at chest height, a fetch-toy POV (only for a toy the dog won't chew — clamp the camera to it), and a water-bowl POV set at ground level.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Use [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) on the dog for the harness POV clip if it wanders in and out of a fixed frame — though a proper harness mount usually needs no reframe at all, since the camera is already pointed the right way.
2. For the ground chase, see [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](slow-motion-tracked-run.md) — shoot 5.7K60/4K120 and slow the final approach to about 0.25–0.5×.
3. Export 9:16 if it's going straight to the family group chat.

## Settings
- Harness POV: any standard resolution, screen-side backwards as above.
- Ground chase: 5.7K60 or 4K120 for slow-motion headroom.

## Tips
- The harness needs a settling-in period — don't expect a relaxed walk on day one.
- The "run puppy run" ground shot works with almost no setup: mini tripod on the grass, someone at the far end with a treat, dog runs at the lenses.
- Combine with [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](slow-motion-tracked-run.md) for the chase shot and mention this page there if you write more pet content later.
