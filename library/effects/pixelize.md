# Pixelize

A static-camera template that turns a moving person into a floating block of 8-bit pixel art. Rated A-tier fun by Ben Claremont.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## See it
<div class="video-embed"><iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/3-5jjbZubYo?start=357" title="The Best And Worst Insta360 Effects (Ranked)" loading="lazy" allow="fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3-5jjbZubYo&t=357s) · Ben Claremont

## Shoot it
1. Lock the camera off — tripod or a steady surface, no handheld movement.
2. Move around in front of it as normal; the effect is applied afterwards.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Edit > Shot Lab > **Pixelize**.
2. Pick your clip and apply — it's largely one-tap.

## Settings
- No special resolution requirement. A static camera is the one real requirement.

## Tips
- Good one to let the kids ham it up in front of — the sillier the movement, the funnier the pixel-art version looks.
- Combine with a static-camera moment you were already planning to shoot (e.g. a group goodbye wave) rather than setting up a separate take.
