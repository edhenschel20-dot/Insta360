# Fireworks Two-Parter
A sunset-to-dark 11K timelapse that pans onto the first rockets, then you pick the camera straight up and film the rest of the show handheld in PureVideo.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Set the camera on a tripod roughly 90 minutes before sunset, framed so a pan can land on the launch area.
2. Timelapse mode, 11K, 5 second interval. Let it run through sunset and 30–45 minutes past it.
3. When the show starts, take the camera off the tripod and onto the 3 m stick. Switch to **PureVideo 8K30, EV −1, auto white balance** (PureVideo doesn't offer manual exposure) and film the fireworks handheld.
4. One creator's hindsight after trying this: next time, try ordinary video at ISO 100 with a slowish manual shutter instead of PureVideo — the highlights came out smeary. Worth testing if you get a spare minute.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Speed the timelapse clip down to under 10 seconds, with one pan keyframe carrying it onto the launch area.
2. Cut to the handheld fireworks clip — a single fixed forward-facing view is usually enough. See [Parades & Fireworks](../shots/parades-and-fireworks.md).
3. Pull family reactions into a corner with [Split Screen / MultiView](split-screen-multiview.md) rather than swinging the main view onto them.

## Settings
- Timelapse: 11K, 5 s interval, tripod.
- Fireworks: PureVideo 8K30, EV −1, auto WB. Full reference: [Night & low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md).

## Tips
- This fits letting the camera roll: the timelapse runs itself, and the handheld half only needs one setting change made once, before the first rocket.
- Don't chase the fireworks around the sky by hand — pick one framing and let 360 capture the rest; reframe later if the display moves.
