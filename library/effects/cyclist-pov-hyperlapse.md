# Wheel-Centred Handlebar Hyperlapse ("Cyclist POV")
A handlebar view sped up around 8x, with the front wheel kept pinned in the middle of frame throughout — a bike-specific take on the general [Hyperlapse / TimeShift](hyperlapse-and-timeshift.md) technique.

**Difficulty:** Medium (needs a few keyframes to keep the wheel centred as the path curves) · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Clamp the camera to the bars with a 10 cm spacer.
2. Ride for a minute or more of normal riding — nothing special needed at shoot time.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Set clip speed to around 8x.
2. Add keyframes at the start, middle, and end that keep the front wheel centred in frame as the road curves.
3. Narrow the FOV a little toward the end for a "closing in" feel.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K30 for the base footage before speeding up.

## Tips
- Straight sections need no extra keyframes — only add one where the path actually curves.
- Combine with [Front-Wheel POV](front-wheel-pov.md) and [Bike Tail-Mount Drone Chase](bike-tail-mount-drone-chase.md) for a short multi-angle bike edit.
