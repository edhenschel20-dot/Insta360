# Ground Swoop / Portal Transition
The stick dives all the way down to the ground until the frame is filled with dirt, sand or floor — then the next clip starts the same way and rises back up somewhere completely different. "Ground Pull" is the same idea with a bit of wave motion added.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. This is a deliberate, planned shot — not something you get from normal rolling footage.
2. Stick low near the ground, lift it all the way up overhead, then bring it back down — walking forward as you do it, or walking backward through the same motion in reverse for the second half.
3. Repeat the same low-high-low arc at the second location.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. At each low point, keyframe the camera rotating roughly 90° down into the ground so the frame fills with the surface.
2. At the top of the arc, keyframe level again.
3. Use ease curves between keyframes so the dive/rise feels smooth rather than jerky.
4. Cut (or cross-fade) the two clips together at the point where both frames are filled with ground.

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at — this is entirely a keyframing exercise.

## Tips
- This is one of the harder transitions in the library to get looking right — expect a couple of takes to nail the timing of the low point.
- The "opposite" version, diving up into the sky instead of down into the ground, is [Sky Transition](sky-transition.md) — often an easier win for the same idea.
