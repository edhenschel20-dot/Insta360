# Tide-Pool Dip
The camera goes into the rock pool: anemones and crabs below, the kids' faces peering in above.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes (dive case, or bare — the X5 is waterproof to 15m)

## Shoot it
1. Lower the X5 (dive case, or bare in a shallow pool) into the water on a short stick.
2. Half in, half out at the waterline: see [Over-Under Split Shot](over-under-split-shot.md), lining the split up on the housing seam — faces above, creatures below.
3. Pushed fully under and into a crack: see [Crevice Probe / Bug View](crevice-probe-bug-view.md).
4. Keep the camera off the animals and the kids off the barnacles.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. [Split-Screen MultiView](split-screen-multiview.md): faces above the water, pool life below.

## Settings
Full reference: [Snorkelling & water settings](../settings/snorkelling-and-water.md).

## Tips
- A drop landing on the above-water lens is worse than the pool itself being a bit cloudy — dunk and lift in one smooth move rather than easing it in.
- Good one to pair with a wider [Beach Base-Camp Cam](beach-base-camp-cam.md) rolling at the towels nearby.
