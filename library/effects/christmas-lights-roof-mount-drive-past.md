# Illuminations Roof-Mount Drive-Past
The camera rides on the car roof and rolls slowly past a Christmas lights display, illuminations tunnel, or any lit-up drive-through.

**Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Works on X5:** Likely (demoed on the X2; settings below updated for the X5)

## Shoot it
1. Fit a magnetic or suction mount on the roof — a magnet plus a small spacer to lift the camera clear of the roofline works well.
2. Drive at walking pace past the display.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Fixed view forward or to the side, whichever faces the lights.
2. Pull the kids' reactions in with [Split Screen / MultiView](split-screen-multiview.md) rather than swinging the main view onto them.

## Settings
- **PureVideo 8K30, EV −0.7, lock white balance** after it settles (older X2 advice used manual exposure throughout; this is the X5-updated version). Full reference: [Night & low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md).

## Tips
- Roof mounts are exposed to wind at speed — walking pace only, and check the mount before setting off.
- Pairs with [Night Cruise GTA Shot](night-cruise-gta-shot.md) for a chase-cam angle from the same drive.
