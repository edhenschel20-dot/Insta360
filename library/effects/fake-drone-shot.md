# Fake Drone / Aerial Shot

An overhead "drone" shot following you or the scene — no drone required, just the stick held straight up.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Put the X5 on the 3 m Extended Edition Selfie Stick (or the standard stick for a lower "drone"). Hold it straight up, lenses parallel to the stick so it disappears.
2. Walk forward in a straight line at a constant height — that's the whole shot.
3. For the "pull-out and rotate" variant, hold the stick overhead and slowly turn on the spot instead of walking, or add a roll (rotation) keyframe in the edit.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe the start view and the end view, FOV set to "Mega".
2. Optional: add a third keyframe halfway for a gentle curve.
3. For the pull-out variant: start on a tight, natural-view framing of the subject from above, then a second keyframe zoomed right out with a bit of roll added — reads as a drone climbing away.

## Settings
- 8K30 for the most detail on the "aerial" scenery.

## Tips
- Combine with [Rotating Reveal](rotating-reveal.md) for a "drone orbits then reveals" feel.
- The 3 m stick is the single accessory that sells this effect — most people can't tell it from a real drone.
