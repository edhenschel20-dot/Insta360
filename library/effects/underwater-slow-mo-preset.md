# Underwater Slow-Mo Preset
A 5.7K60 preset saved to the camera's Q/power button, so you can switch to slow motion underwater with one press — because once the dive case is on, you can't change settings.

**Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Before the dive, save a **5.7K60** preset to the Q/power button — the X5 needs 5.7K to get 60fps (the X6 can do 8K50, the X5 can't).
2. Also worth saving alongside it: a dawn/dusk preset and a night-dive preset, so all your likely light conditions are one button-press away.
3. Once the dive case is fitted, you're locked into whatever preset you picked — there's no reaching in to change settings mid-dive.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Slow the clip to 50% for turtles, fish, or anything else worth lingering on.

## Settings
- 5.7K60 preset on the Q/power button. Full reference: [Underwater diving settings](../settings/underwater-diving.md).

## Tips
- Set this up the night before a dive day, not at the dive site — it's a one-time job per trip, not a per-dive one.
- Remember the dive-case battery can't be swapped underwater — one preset covers the whole tank, so pick it before you get in.
