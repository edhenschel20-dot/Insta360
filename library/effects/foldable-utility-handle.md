# Foldable Utility Handle (No-Stick Venues)
Theme parks, some luau lawns and other venues ban selfie sticks. This folding case-handle keeps the X5 usable and pocketable when a stick isn't allowed through the gate.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. The Foldable Utility Handle is a case that folds out into a short grip; it covers the lenses when closed, has a lanyard loop, and fold-out feet so it can stand on a table.
2. It passed bag check at a theme park (worth confirming at the venue you're visiting, but it's a good sign).
3. **Hold it at arm's length for faces** — there's no distance from the lens like a stick gives you, so close-up framing needs a longer arm reach than you'd use with a stick.
4. Not for dark rides (too dark for it) — expect more jitter on a night walk than you'd get from a properly braced stick.
5. Not the same thing as the FreeFrame stick, which is single-lens.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe as normal — no special settings needed beyond accounting for the closer holding distance.
2. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) pairs well with this, since the handle itself gives you less framing control at shoot time than a stick would.

## Settings
- Any standard resolution — this is an accessory, not a camera mode.

## Tips
- Good for any no-stick venue, not just theme parks — a museum, an indoor show, or a luau lawn where sticks are turned away at the entrance.
- Fold-out feet mean it also works as a mini standing mount on a table if you need both hands free for a moment.
