# Swim-Through Doorway / Arch Transition
You pull the camera through a wreck doorway, rock arch, or cave mouth, and cut the moment the opening fills the frame — the scene on the other side reads as a hard transition.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes (with the dive case underwater)

## Shoot it
1. Frame your buddy or the kids approaching from the other side of the opening before you start swimming or walking through it.
2. Swim or walk through steadily; cut the clip at the point where the opening fills the frame.
3. **Above water:** the same move works through a cave mouth, under a pier, or through any archway.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Cut hard at the point of maximum fill, straight into the next clip.

## Tips
- Insta360's own advice is that swim-throughs and caves are exactly where 360 capture shines — you get the whole opening, not just what a flat lens happened to point at.
- Works well as the link between two dive/snorkel locations in an edit.
