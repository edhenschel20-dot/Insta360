# Roll / Rotation Transition
The camera rolls 180° right at the end of one clip and keeps rolling into the start of the next — a spin-cut between two scenes.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. No special shooting needed — this is an editing-only transition, so any two clips with a natural break point work.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. On clip A, add two keyframes near the end: roll 0° at the first, roll 180° at the second, 1–2 seconds apart.
2. On clip B, mirror it at the start: roll 180° at the first keyframe, roll 360° (i.e. back to level) at the second, same spacing.
3. Use a fast curve (menu names vary by app version — look for something like "Quick In/Out") so the roll accelerates into the cut.
4. Cut between the clips on the blurriest point of the roll.

## Settings
- Works at any resolution you already shot at.

## Tips
- This is the manual, fully-controllable version of the transitions bundled in the app's transitions library — see [Spin Transition](spin-transition.md) for the simpler pan-whip version, and [Whip-Pan Transition](whip-pan-transition.md) for the directional variant.
- Matching the roll speed on both sides of the cut is what sells it — mismatched speeds are the most common reason this looks off.
