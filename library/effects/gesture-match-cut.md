# Gesture Match Cut
A clap, click, high-five or hand swipe across the lens in one location cuts straight to the same gesture in another — the motion "matches" across the join so it barely reads as a cut.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes (manual route); Shot Lab's **MatchCuts** template is Android-only and flat video per the app manual — check your app

## Shoot it
1. Pick a clear, repeatable gesture: a clap, a finger click, a high-five with someone else, or swiping your hand right across the lens.
2. Do the same gesture, at the same rough distance from the camera, in both locations — record with sound so you can hear exactly when it happens.
3. A hand-swipe across the lens is the easiest version to time, because it also acts as a natural wipe.

## Edit it (phone app)
**One-tap route (if available):** Shot Lab › **MatchCuts** — per the app manual this is currently Android-only and works on flat (not 360) video, so check it's offered on your setup before planning around it.

**Manual route:**
1. Scrub both clips to find the exact frame where the gesture completes (hands meet, fingers click, hand fully covers the lens).
2. Trim clip A to end right there, and clip B to start right there.
3. Cut directly — no transition effect needed, the gesture itself sells it.

## Settings
- Record with sound on for claps/clicks — hearing the moment makes it much faster to find the exact frame.

## Tips
- With kids, a high-five is the easiest version to get right on the first take — a clap needs more precise timing to land on the same frame both times.
- The hand-swipe version doubles as a simple wipe transition if the gesture match doesn't line up perfectly.
