# Spatial Capture (3D scene)

You walk slowly round a place for up to two minutes with the camera
rolling. Insta360's cloud turns the clip into a **3D model of that spot**.
Afterwards you can move round it on your phone, send someone a link so they
can explore it too, or make a video that flies a virtual camera through it
along a path you choose. It's a 3D memory of a *place*, not of a moment.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes (X6, X5 and X4 Air) · 💎 **4
free a month**, US rollout from 2026-09-28

!!! note "Not free and not on your phone"
    Some articles say spatial photos are free because they're made on your
    phone. That's a **different thing** (for example, iPhone "spatial" photos
    or 3D-photo apps). Insta360's **Spatial Capture** uploads your clip and
    builds the 3D scene **in Insta360's cloud**. You get **4 free a month**.
    The count resets on the 1st, and unused goes don't carry over. After
    that, one report says each extra costs **50 Diamonds** (the app's paid
    currency). Check the price in the app before you go past 4.

## Good for, and not
| Good | Poor |
|---|---|
| Places that stand still: a banyan tree, a church or old building, a hotel lanai, a garden, a lookout, a beautiful dining room before it fills up | People, waves, waterfalls, anything moving: they come out smeared or missing |
| Places you want to "walk round again" later | Family moments. Film those normally |
| Somewhere about the size of a big room (Insta360 suggests up to about 100 m²) | A whole beach or a whole town |

## Shoot it
1. **Mode:** normal Video (the TRICK preset, 5.7K30, is fine).
2. **Go when it's quiet**, first thing in the morning if you can. People who
   keep walking past are usually dropped by the processing. People who stand
   or sit still come out as ghostly smudges (that's how this kind of 3D scan
   behaves in general; Insta360 hasn't said exactly how its version handles
   people). **If someone settles in where you're filming, skip that side and
   come back.**
3. **Use the 114 cm stick, held overhead.** Walk slowly **back and forth
   across the space** in lines **no more than about 2 m (6 ft) apart**, like
   mowing a lawn.
4. Then **lower the camera to chest height** and walk slowly round the main
   thing, the trunk of the banyan for example.
5. **Keep it under 2 minutes.** That's the most it will process. If you go
   longer, you pick a 2-minute stretch later.
6. Bright, even light works best. Avoid deep shade next to full sun.

## Make it (phone app)
1. Album › **Spatial Capture** › **Create**. The app shows which clips are
   suitable.
2. Pick the clip (or a 2-minute stretch of it) › **Generate**.
3. It uploads and processes in the cloud, which takes **about 1–2 hours**.
   You can close the app, and it notifies you when the scene is ready.
4. Then you can:
   - **explore it** with your finger, an on-screen joystick, or by tilting
     the phone;
   - **share a link**, so others can explore it with no app needed;
   - **make a video**: set a camera path and effects, and save it.

Menu names are from a September 2026 walk-through. Check them on your phone
in the [practice drills](../shots/practice-drills.md).

## On the trip
- **Film it there, make it at home.** Only the filming has to happen in
  Maui. Processing can wait until you're home, and a new month brings 4 new
  free goes. (The upload works over your phone's data too, if you'd rather
  see it the same day.)
- **Pick 2 or 3 places**, not ten. Good Maui ideas: a big banyan early in
  the morning, the hotel lanai, the restaurant room before your dinner
  sitting, a quiet church or old building in town.
- **Film two goes at each place.** Then you can keep the cleaner one.
