# Waterfall Plunge-Pool
The camera goes under the curtain of a waterfall, or sits at the surface of the plunge pool with the falls above and the rocks below.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes (dive case for going under)

## Shoot it
1. **Under the falls:** X5 in the dive case, swim under the curtain of water — expect it to be loud and turbulent close up, and the water is often chilly even somewhere warm.
2. **From the pool edge:** hold the stick out into the spray rather than swimming under, if you'd rather stay dry.
3. **Over-under at the surface:** see [Over-Under Split Shot](over-under-split-shot.md) at the surface of the plunge pool, falls above and rocks below.
4. Spray on the lens: rinse and shake it off, don't wipe — a dry wipe just smears it.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Lens facing the falls, with the seam running along the stream rather than across it.
2. Combine with [Up-and-Over Reveal](up-and-over-reveal.md) from behind you, revealing the falls.

## Settings
Full reference: [Underwater diving](../settings/underwater-diving.md) for going under; [Snorkelling & water](../settings/snorkelling-and-water.md) if you're staying at the edge.

## Tips
- A stick held out over a cliff edge above the falls gives a drone-style look at the drop without swimming under it at all.
- Plant a Throwpod or stick on the rocks nearby and let it roll while everyone swims — you don't need to hold it the whole time.
