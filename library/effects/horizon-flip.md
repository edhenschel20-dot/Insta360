# Horizon Flip

A one-tap effect that mirrors the horizon above the frame while the view spins — the "folding city" look from Inception. Rated S-tier by Ben Claremont for being hard to mess up.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Any normal walking clip works — nothing special needed at shoot time.
2. A clip with a clear horizon and some visible sky or ground detail reads best once flipped.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Edit > Shot Lab > **Horizon Flip**.
2. Apply it to your clip and tweak the in-app sliders if offered — it's largely automatic.

## Settings
- No special camera settings — works from normal footage, any resolution.

## Tips
- One of the easiest Shot Lab effects to get a good result from first time — a good one to show the kids.
- Menu names vary by app version; if "Horizon Flip" isn't where you expect, check under Shot Lab's current list.
