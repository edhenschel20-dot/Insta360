# Bicycle Tiny Planet
You ride along on top of your own miniature planet, the whole road curling away beneath you — the bike version of the standard [Tiny Planet & Inverted Tiny Planet](tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) technique.

**Difficulty:** Medium (the "point the phone down while riding" edit step takes a little practice) · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Clamp the stick to the head tube, extended by about one and a half sections.
2. Ride normally for 30 seconds or so.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Set an initial keyframe on the ride.
2. Switch to the hand-movement (viewfinder) reframe mode: hold record, turn your body to the right and tilt the phone down — the view rolls into a tiny planet with you sitting on top.

## Settings
- Any resolution already shot at.

## Tips
- Best on smooth, quiet paths — the planet effect makes any wobble in the ride much more obvious.
- Pair with [Bicycle Selfie TimeShift](bicycle-selfie-timeshift.md) for a reveal-then-planet sequence.
