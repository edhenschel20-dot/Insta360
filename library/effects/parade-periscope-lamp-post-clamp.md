# Parade Periscope & Lamp-Post Clamp
The stick goes up over the crowd for a "drone" view of the floats, then between floats the camera clamps to a lamp-post or barrier and rolls hands-free.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Raise the stick straight up over the crowd for a drone-style pass as a float goes by — one lens toward the floats.
2. Between floats, clamp the camera to a lamp-post, barrier, or pushchair handle and let it roll hands-free rather than roaming with it.
3. When people are packed in behind you, keep the stick at head height instead of raised — the crowd-bottleneck moment in the source video is the reason to plant early and let it roll rather than try to move through the crowd.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe the raised-stick sections wide, following the floats.
2. Reframe the clamped sections as a fixed view, cutting to the best few minutes rather than the whole hands-free stretch.

## Adapt it
- The X5 doesn't have magnetic mounts like some action cams — use a proper clamp on the lamp-post, barrier, or pushchair handle instead of trying to stick it on.

## Settings
- Standard video, 5.7K30 is plenty for a static or periscope shot; bump to 5.7K60 if you're following fast-moving floats.

## Tips
- The periscope shot is worth trying twice — once raised, once from the standard stick height — since crowds can block the low angle entirely.
- Pairs with [Lighted Parade](../shots/lighted-parade.md) for a night parade.
