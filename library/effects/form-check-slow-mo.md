# Form Check Slow-Mo
Slow-motion of your own swing, stroke, or riding position, reframed to face-on, down-the-line, and overhead angles — all from one camera you set up once and never had to point.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Set the X5 on a tripod or mini-stand a few metres from the tee, hitting net, or bike, at 5.7K60 (or Bullet Time 4K120 for even slower playback).
2. Swing, putt, or ride normally in front of it — one clip is enough for several angles.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe the same clip three separate times: face-on, down-the-line, and from directly overhead.
2. Set clip speed to 0.25–0.5x through the moment that matters (impact, the top of the pedal stroke).
3. See [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](slow-motion-tracked-run.md) for the general slow-mo technique this builds on — this page is specifically about reviewing your own technique from multiple angles out of one clip.

## Tips
- Trace Velo used exactly this to adjust a bike fit from Bullet Time slow-mo footage — the same idea works for a golf swing, even though no dedicated golf swing-analysis tutorial exists yet (see the gap list's "Not found" section).
- Golf etiquette: set the tripod up off to the side, not in anyone's line, and don't hold up the group behind you reviewing footage on the tee.

## More tips: golf swing-analysis positions
See [Swing-Analysis Camera Positions](golf-swing-camera-positions.md) for
the full page: **face-on** = camera at hand height, at right angles to
the target line; **down-the-line** = behind the hands, lens at hand
height, pointing down the target line through them; a third **rear view
at belt height** shows hip and spine tilt. **A 16-inch camera offset is
enough to change the apparent swing plane**, so plant it in the same spot
each session. See [Golf Round](../shots/golf-round.md) for the full plan.
