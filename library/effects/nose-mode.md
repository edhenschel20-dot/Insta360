# Nose Mode

An exaggerated, funny camera-on-face selfie — famous for making people ask "what is that?"

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Check app version (menu names vary by app version)

## Shoot it
Shot Lab **Nose Mode**, or hold the camera right at face level, about 30 cm away.

## Edit it (phone app)
The template does the rest — pick it, follow the on-screen recording guide.

## Settings
- No special resolution requirement.

## Tips
- A quick, low-effort novelty shot — good as a one-off rather than something to overuse.
