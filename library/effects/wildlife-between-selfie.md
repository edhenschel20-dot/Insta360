# Wildlife-Between Selfie
Let a turtle or fish swim between you and the camera — the animal becomes the subject, you're just the background.

**Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Let the animal come to you — never chase it. If it stays, you can move in a little.
2. The X5 focuses close, so a fish at 30 cm away is still sharp.
3. Keep rolling rather than stopping to react — this is exactly the "things happen behind you" case from letting the camera roll: a turtle can cross the frame well after you've stopped watching for it.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe on the animal once it's passed through, or let [AI Director](ai-director.md) surface the moment for you.

## Adapt it
- **Kids:** the same trick works with a child swimming between you and the camera instead of wildlife.

- This is a good one to leave running throughout a dive or snorkel rather than setting up specially for — you often don't know it happened until you review the footage.
