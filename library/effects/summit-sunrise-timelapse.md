# Summit Sunrise Timelapse in the Cold
A sunrise-above-the-clouds timelapse from a cold, dark summit — without frosted lenses, a snapped tripod, or a dead battery before the sky changes.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Arrive earlier than you think you need to — you'll be adjusting a cold camera for up to 40 minutes before it's properly light.
2. Wear gloves. Plastic tripod and clamp parts snap in the cold if you yank them — go gently.
3. If the lens frosts, breathe on it and wipe gently with a cloth — never scrape it.
4. Rubber-band a hand warmer to the camera body (under the mini-tripod head, not over a lens).
5. Start the timelapse about 30 minutes before sunrise and let the camera build the whole clip itself.
6. Battery: cold roughly halves it. Keep the spare in an inside pocket, not your bag.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Speed the result down to under 10 seconds.
2. One slow pan from the clouds to the family, rather than a static frame the whole time.

## Settings
- Timelapse 11K, 5-second interval (2 seconds if you only have 30 minutes), Vivid, EV −0.3, white balance manual 6500K (auto WB jumps around as the light changes), low-light stabilisation off.
- Clean both lenses before you start — you won't get a second chance once the sky is doing something.
- Full reference: [Timelapse and timeshift](../settings/timelapse-and-timeshift.md) and [Night Interval Timelapse](night-interval-timelapse-72mp.md).

## Tips
- The gear failure modes here are about cold, not about the camera — a snapped tripod leg or a dead battery loses you the whole shot, so treat the kit gently and keep the spare battery warm.
- This is a genuinely different situation from a normal timelapse: budget real setup time, don't rush it in the dark.
