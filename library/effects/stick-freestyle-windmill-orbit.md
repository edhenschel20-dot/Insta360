# Stick Freestyle / Windmill Orbit
Move the stick around freely — wave it around, windmill it over someone's head, swing it in loops — and let the edit keep the subject centred afterward, so the world tumbles chaotically around a fixed point.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Swing, wave or windmill the stick around freely — over someone's head, in loops, wherever feels fun. There's no fixed path to hit, unlike a clean orbit.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe to keep the subject (you, or whoever you swung it around) centred through the whole clip, however chaotic the stick path was.
2. Add slight extra rotations on top if you want the tumbling feeling more pronounced than the raw footage already gives you.

## Settings
- 5.7K30, FlowState + Horizon Lock on to keep the world reasonably level even through fast movement.

## Tips
- This is the loose, playful cousin of [360 Orbit (Hand-Swung)](hand-swung-360-orbit.md) — that one is a single clean circle at shoulder height; this one is deliberately messier, and the edit does more of the work to keep it watchable.
- Good with kids — waving the stick "windmill" style over their head while they look up is an easy, low-effort version to try.
