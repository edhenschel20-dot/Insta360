# Tiny Planet Flip / Planet Landing

The view flips from tiny planet to normal (or the reverse) — either as a "landing" within one shot, or as a transition between two clips.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Works on any clip; a static camera with movement somewhere in the scene works best for the transition version, but any tiny-planet-eligible clip works (see [Tiny Planet & Inverted](tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) for what makes a good one).

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe 1: tiny planet (FOV max, tilt straight down).
2. Keyframe 2, 1–2 seconds later: normal view.
3. Set the curve between them to a snappy one (e.g. "Quick In/Out" — menu names vary by app version) for a fast flip, or a slower ease for a gentle "landing".
4. For a transition between two clips: cut to the next clip right on the fastest part of the move.

## Settings
- Works on any resolution you already shot at.

## Tips
- Use as an opening "descending onto the scene" shot, or as a scene-change transition mid-edit.
- Pairs well with [Time Freeze](time-freeze-animated-photo.md) as a title card.
