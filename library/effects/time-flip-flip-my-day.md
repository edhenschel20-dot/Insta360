# Time Flip & Flip My Day
Two related one-tap Shot Lab templates that stitch several locations together with a half-turn spin at each cut. **Time Flip** does this across a handful of places; **Flip My Day** extends it into a full day-in-the-life sequence.

**Difficulty:** Hard (needs real planning) · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. At each location, spin 180° on the stick (half of a full rotation) — the app stitches the halves of each clip together at the cut.
2. This only works well if the locations genuinely look different from each other — two similar backdrops (two beaches, say) make the flip much less convincing.
3. **Flip My Day** needs a wider spread of scenes across an actual day (morning, midday, evening) to read as a story rather than just a location montage.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Edit › Shot Lab › **Time Flip** (or **Flip My Day**) › Use this theme.
2. Pick your clips, in the order you want them to appear.
3. Export.

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at.

## Tips
- Ben Claremont rates both of these D-tier — they need real planning (genuinely different-looking scenes, shot in the right order) to pay off, and are more fiddly than they look for the result you get.
- If you want an easier version of "spin at each location," try [Same Pose, Many Places (Spin Me Around)](same-pose-many-places.md) first — same idea, easier to shoot and edit well.
