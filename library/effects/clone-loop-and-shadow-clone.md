# Clone Loop & Shadow Clone

Two related static-camera templates for cloning yourself in one frame:
- **Clone Loop:** walk around the camera doing an action while it pans — you end up cloned across the pan. Rated A-tier by Ben Claremont.
- **Shadow Clone:** do different actions in different spots (10–15 seconds each) and the app composites all of you into one shot. Rated C-tier — Ben says it works, but takes more effort to get right.

Not the same as [Clone Trail](clone-trail.md), which is a simpler "several copies trailing behind you as you walk" effect from a fully static camera.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. **Clone Loop:** camera on a tripod that pans (or handheld on a very steady arc); walk around it performing an action as it goes.
2. **Shadow Clone:** camera fully static (tripod); do a distinct action in each of several spots within frame, holding each for 10–15 seconds.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Edit > Shot Lab > **Clone Loop** or **Shadow Clone**.
2. Pick your clip; the app finds and composites the repeated passes automatically.
3. Shadow Clone in particular may need a couple of attempts — keep actions and spots clearly separated so the app doesn't blend them oddly.

## Settings
- No special resolution requirement. A steady or static camera is the key requirement for both.

## Tips
- Good for kids' games where the same person can plausibly be "cloned" doing different things — races, a group photo where one person appears several times, etc.
- If Shadow Clone comes out messy, Clone Loop is the easier, more reliable of the two to start with.
