# Bicycle Selfie TimeShift
The clip starts looking straight down the road, zooms out and swings round to you riding, then speeds up 8x for the rest of the ride.

**Difficulty:** Medium (several keyframes to land in sequence) · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Clamp the stick to the head tube.
2. Ride for about 2 minutes to give the edit room to work with.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe 1: forward-facing, linear.
2. About 1.5 seconds later: pinch out to zoom wide.
3. About 1 second after that: pan round to face yourself.
4. Keep adding a keyframe every 5–10 seconds afterwards to stay centred on you.
5. From the reveal onward, set clip speed to 8x, stopping about a second before the end.
6. Export and check the motion blur reads well at full speed.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K30 for the base footage.

## Tips
- This is a deliberate, planned sequence rather than "let it roll" footage — worth a couple of takes to land the timing.
- Pairs with [Bicycle Tiny Planet](bicycle-tiny-planet.md) or [Wheel-Centred Handlebar Hyperlapse](cyclist-pov-hyperlapse.md) as a closing shot.
