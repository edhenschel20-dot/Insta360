# Front-Wheel POV
The camera rides on the front wheel or fork, looking up at the pedals and rider — a low, mechanical point of view you don't get from a handlebar or chest mount.

**Difficulty:** Medium (mount needs to be solid — this spot takes the most vibration on the bike) · **Works on X5:** Likely (demoed on the lighter X3; clamp tight and stick to smooth paths on the heavier X5)

## Shoot it
1. Fit a spacer and clamp to the front fork or wheel hub.
2. Ride for 30 seconds or more — smooth, sealed paths only, since this mount feels every bump hardest.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Use the hand-movement (viewfinder) reframe: hold record, then tilt the phone to look up at yourself, zoom into the pedalling, and back out to the wheel.
2. Works well on kids' bikes and balance bikes too — the low angle suits their size.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or higher for the closest, most detailed crop on the wheel/pedals.

## Tips
- Check the clamp before every ride — it's the mount most likely to work loose from vibration.
- Pairs well with [Wheel-Centred Handlebar Hyperlapse](cyclist-pov-hyperlapse.md) for a two-shot bike sequence.
