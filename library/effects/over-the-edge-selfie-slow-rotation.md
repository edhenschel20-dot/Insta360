# Over-the-Edge Selfie with Slow Rotation
Hold the stick out over the water from a pier, bridge, or pool edge, one lens toward you, and slowly rotate it in your hand for a mini drone-like orbit.

**Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Hold the stick out over the water or the drop, with one lens facing back toward you.
2. Slowly rotate the stick in your hand — a full turn over 10–15 seconds is plenty — for a gentle orbit around yourself.
3. **Pool version:** hang the camera out over the water while the kids jump in, for the same look from a much safer height.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe to keep yourself roughly centred through the rotation; the orbit does most of the work.

## Settings
- No special mode — a normal 360 clip.

## Tips
- Works especially well right at golden hour with a view behind you (a bay, a harbour, a sunset).
