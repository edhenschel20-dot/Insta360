# Over-Under Split Shot
Half the frame shows the sky, half shows the reef below — one still or clip, split right at the waterline.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes (with the dive case)

## See it

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pHZSWWOCyt8&t=531s) · Scott Bauer Below

## Shoot it
1. Calm water only — any chop ruins the split.
2. With the Invisible Dive Case fitted, rest the X5 at the surface so the waterline sits right on the housing seam.
3. Hold still; let the water settle around the case before you start recording properly.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe to the lens whose view actually shows the split — it won't be centred by default.

## Adapt it
- **Pool version:** the same trick works with the kids' legs underwater and their faces above — good for a shallow pool day.

## Settings
- Dive Case Mode on. Full reference: [Underwater diving settings](../settings/underwater-diving.md).

## Tips
- The battery can't be swapped inside the dive case, so try this early in a dive or snorkel session rather than at the end when charge is low.
- Pairs well with [Wildlife-Between Selfie](wildlife-between-selfie.md) on the same swim.
