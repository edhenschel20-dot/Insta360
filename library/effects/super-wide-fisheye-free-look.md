# Super-Wide, Fisheye-Free Look
An almost-full-scene wide shot without the curved, fisheye look you'd normally get from pushing FOV that wide.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Likely (confirmed in Studio; app route not independently confirmed)

## Shoot it
1. No special shooting needed — works on any wide scene you've already got: beach, coastline, a big view.

## Edit it (phone app or Studio)
1. **Studio (confirmed):** keyframe FOV to 135 and set distortion control to 0.1 — this pulls most of the fisheye bend out while keeping the extra-wide frame.
2. **Phone app:** use the widest Dewarp/Linear projection option and pull the framing back — the exact control names vary by app version, so look for a "distortion" or "linear" toggle alongside FOV.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K — more resolution gives more room to crop into once the distortion is pulled out.

## Tips
- This is the shot to reach for on a big open view (beach, coastline, helicopter cabin) where you want as much scene as possible without it looking obviously like a 360 fisheye.
- If the app route doesn't get you all the way there, Studio is worth the extra step for a hero shot.
