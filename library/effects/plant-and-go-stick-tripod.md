# Plant-and-Go Stick Tripod
Leave a small folding tripod screwed onto the bottom of your selfie stick permanently. At every stop — a lookout, a waterfall, getting the kids out of the car — it's ten seconds to pop the legs, stand it up, and keep rolling, instead of digging out a separate tripod.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Screw a small folding mini tripod onto the bottom of a stiff (carbon, not bendy aluminium) invisible stick, and leave it there — it stays invisible in shot and just gives you a slightly longer stick when the legs are folded.
2. Keep the whole thing somewhere grab-fast, like a door pocket, so stopping the car doesn't mean digging through a bag.
3. At each stop: pop the legs, stand the camera, and go help with the kids — the camera keeps rolling on its own the whole time you're out of shot doing that.
4. Film more than you think you need. This isn't a clip-style camera — 10–20 minute continuous rolls at each stop give you far more to find good moments in later than a string of 5-second clips.
5. Hold the stick low, roughly at the kids' eye height, rather than up over your head — see [Kid-Height Invisible Cinematographer](kid-height-invisible-cinematographer.md).

## Edit it (phone app)
1. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) whoever's active in each stop's clip.
2. If you're offloading each evening, sort into folders per day so a trip's worth of stops doesn't turn into one unsorted pile.
3. Reframe straight from the Quick Reader without offloading first if you just want to check a shot on the go — see [Quick edit: steer by phone](../editing/quick-edit-steer-by-phone.md).

## Settings
- Any standard resolution — this is a kit habit, not a camera mode.
- Two large cards (128 GB+) plus a card reader if the trip involves several long stops in one day.

## Tips
- The single biggest win here is that the tripod is never a separate thing you have to remember or dig for — it's always on the stick.
- Mic side up: if you're also running a mic (see [Mic the Kid](mic-the-kid-wireless-mic-deep-track.md)), keep the mic-carrying side of the stick uppermost when it's held low.
- A stiff carbon stick stays properly invisible in the stitch; a bendy aluminium one can show as a black dot in your hand when it flexes.
