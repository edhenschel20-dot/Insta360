# Street Lapse & Flash Dash

You isolated from a background that streams past (Street Lapse), or rocketing forward with a lightning trail (Flash Dash).

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Check app version (menu names vary by app version)

## Shoot it: Street Lapse (two clips, camera doesn't move)
You stand still while the street streams past you in fast motion.

1. **Stand the 2-in-1 stick and tripod** on the pavement edge, out of
   everyone's way. **Don't touch it again** until both clips are done.
2. **Clip 1 (about 20 seconds):** stand still about an arm's length from the
   camera. Wear something light-coloured, because dark clothes against a dark
   background confuse the app.
3. **Clip 2 (1–3 minutes):** step out of the scene and let the street carry
   on.
4. Use **Video mode (the TRICK preset, 5.7K30)**. Timelapse mode isn't
   accepted by this template.

## Shoot it: Flash Dash
Walk, skate or ski steadily with the stick extended. Several people can be in frame for a Flash Dash together.

## Edit it (phone app)
Edit > Shot Lab > **Street Lapse** (pick both clips) or **Flash Dash**.

## Settings
- **Video mode, 5.7K30.** Street Lapse doesn't take Timelapse-mode clips.
- Flash Dash: a steady pace matters more than resolution.

## Tips
- Good closing shot for a highlights reel — reads as "superhuman speed" with zero manual editing.
