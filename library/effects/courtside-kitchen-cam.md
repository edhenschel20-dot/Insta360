# Court-Side "Kitchen Cam"
One camera clamped at the net post or side fence covers the entire court, so you can zoom in on line calls and rallies later without ever moving it during play.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Clamp the camera on the net post or side fence at roughly head height.
2. Start recording and let it roll for the whole session — no need to touch it between points.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Start with a fixed, wide keyframe covering the whole court.
2. Add punch-in keyframes on specific points you want to review (a line call, a good rally).
3. For doubles or two courts at once, use [Split Screen / MultiView](split-screen-multiview.md) to show both ends together.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K30 so there's enough resolution left to zoom into a line call after the fact.

## Tips
- Because it never moves, one clamp and one battery covers an entire match or practice session — set it and forget it.
- A tennis version of this exists too (CourtCraft's 2023 X3 test), though pickleball has the more current write-up.
