# Spin Transition

The view whips round at the end of one clip and settles at the start of the next — a fast, blurry cut between two scenes.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Best with two clips that both have some movement in them already — nothing special needed at shoot time.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. On clip A, add a fast spin keyframe right at the end (or use the Record-mode spin button — see [360 Spin and Barrel Roll](spin-and-barrel-roll.md)).
2. On clip B, add a matching spin keyframe at the very start.
3. Cut between the two clips on the blurriest part of the spin so the eye doesn't catch the join.
4. Turning on Motion ND helps hide the cut further.

## Settings
- Works on any resolution.

## Tips
- This is a good default transition between situation clips (e.g. hike b-roll to summit reveal) without needing a fade or a hard cut.
- This is the whip-pan/blur version. For a slower 180° twist instead of a whip-blur, keyframe roll 0°→180° at the end of clip A and 180°→360° at the start of clip B with matching spacing — same idea, a visibly rotating cut rather than a blur.
- Zero-effort option: skip keyframes entirely — Edit > Create a Video > tap the box between two clips > pick a built-in transition (e.g. Zoom Cut, Blur Cut) — Ben Claremont [demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0vsCPu4ZlxY&t=654s).
