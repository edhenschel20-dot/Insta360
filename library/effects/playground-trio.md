# Playground Trio
Three playground shots from the same ten minutes: the world swinging past on the swing, the slide seen through the child's eyes, and a free bullet-time spin on the roundabout.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. **Swing:** clamp a short stick to the swing chain, or have the child hold it themself. Stand behind and push as normal — you'll be in shot, and that's fine.
2. **Slide:** the child holds the stick out ahead of them as they go down, or you hold it at the bottom pointing up the slide for a reveal as they burst out at the end — see [Object Reveal / Push-Through](object-reveal-push-through.md).
3. **Roundabout:** camera on the mini tripod in the centre, kids on the edge, spin it as normal.
4. Shoot all three at 5.7K60.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. **Swing:** turn Direction Lock **off** so the world pendulums around the child instead of staying level — see [Direction-Lock Carlapse](direction-lock-carlapse.md) for the opposite (lock-on) case.
2. **Slide:** [Object Reveal / Push-Through](object-reveal-push-through.md)'s two-keyframe approach for the reveal at the bottom.
3. **Roundabout:** reframe as a [Turntable Bullet Time](turntable-bullet-time.md) — the spin gives you the same no-cord bullet-time look.

## Settings
- 5.7K60 for all three shots.

## Tips
- Ten minutes at a playground is enough for all three if you've got the family there anyway — no extra setup time needed beyond the stick and mini tripod you're probably already carrying.
