# Auto Edit 2.0 & Moments Pro

Two related but separate features:
- **Auto Edit 2.0:** pick your clips, tap once, and the app assembles a themed edit with music and transitions — then you can switch the template or reorder clips afterwards.
- **Moments Pro:** a cloud-based edit described in words rather than picked from templates. This needs a paid Insta360+ subscription tier and outputs at 1080p — the cheapest sensible option is to skip it and use free Auto Edit instead.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
- No special shooting needed — this works on clips you've already recorded, the same way you normally shoot.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Edit > **Auto Edit** > select the clips you want included.
2. The app picks a template automatically; tap to switch templates or drag to reorder clips if you want something different.
3. Ignore **Moments** unless you already have (or want) an Insta360+ subscription — its cloud edits are a paid feature, and free Auto Edit 2.0 covers the same "quick first pass" need at no cost.

## Settings
- No special camera settings — this is a post-shoot editing feature.

## Tips
- Good as a fast first draft for a day's worth of clips (e.g. a beach day or a scenic drive) before you go in and do any manual reframing.
- If you want more control over pacing than the auto templates give, fall back to building the timeline by hand.
- **Night-walk clean-up:** one creator ran a night-walk clip through Auto
  Edit specifically to tidy up shaky or dark stretches automatically,
  rather than reframing them by hand — a reasonable use even when you're
  not building a full themed edit.
