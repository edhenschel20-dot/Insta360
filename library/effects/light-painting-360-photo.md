# Light Painting 360 Photo
A long-exposure 360 photo where torch or glow-stick trails hang in the air around the camera — usually exported as a tiny planet.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Likely (demoed on Theta X / ONE X; confirm the X5's manual photo shutter range in-app)

## Shoot it
1. Find somewhere properly dark. Photo mode, manual: roughly **20 second shutter, lowest ISO, 5 second self-timer**.
2. Stand about 2 m from the camera and draw with a phone torch, switching it off between letters or shapes. Text needs to be written **backwards** — the 360 lens sees you mirrored.
3. **With kids:** give each child a glow stick and one shape to draw while everyone else stays frozen and out of the torch's path. A coloured light walked in a circle round the camera makes a ring.
4. Export as a tiny planet so the light trails read as one composition around you.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Export the still, then run it through [Tiny Planet & Inverted Tiny Planet](tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) framing.

## Settings
- Photo, manual: ~20 s shutter, lowest ISO, 5 s self-timer. See [Night & low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md).

## Tips
- Practise the shape or word once with the torch off before the real take — it's easy to run out of dark before you finish.
- This is the closest thing this library has to a bonfire/luau light trick — no dedicated tutorial exists for glow sticks at a fire event, but the same settings and backwards-writing rule apply.
