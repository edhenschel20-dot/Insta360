# Snow-Day Mount Swap (Sledding)
Kids sledding, filmed from a different mount each run — the sled's front, a helmet, a chest strap, a backpack — so an afternoon of runs doesn't turn into the same shot repeated ten times.

**Difficulty:** Medium (needs the mounts on hand, and a minute to swap each time) · **Works on X5:** Yes (mounts are generic action-cam mounts)

## Shoot it
1. **Stick:** the farther the stick holds the camera from the rider, the cooler the shot tends to look.
2. **Sled mount:** a motorcycle-style U-bolt mount on the sled's front or back.
3. **Helmet mount:** under the visor edge for the most realistic POV.
4. **Chest mount:** shows hands and feet, useful for seeing what the child's actually doing with their hands on the sled.
5. **Backpack mount:** off to one side for a third-person, "watching yourself" angle.
6. **Switch mounts through the day** — using the same one for every run makes every clip look the same; rotating between them is what gives you variety from one afternoon.
7. **Use the same run twice:** film the same sledding run once from behind the first child, then again from behind a friend following them — two different-feeling clips from one hill.
8. Cold-weather basics: keep the spare battery in an inside pocket, and only mount the camera just before the run rather than leaving it out in the cold longer than needed.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) the rider on any of the mount types where the camera isn't already pointed straight at them.
2. [Slow-Motion Tracked Run](slow-motion-tracked-run.md) works well on the sled-mount or backpack angle for the final stretch of a run.

## Settings
- Standard resolution; battery kept warm (inside pocket) between runs.
