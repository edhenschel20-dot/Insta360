# Ground-Level Inverted Planet Photo
Palms, trees, or towers all point inward toward the centre of a ring — shot from a camera sitting on the ground, not from you walking a loop around it.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Set the camera on a mini tripod or a ground stake, right down at ground level.
2. Pick a spot surrounded by tall things — a palm grove, a forest, a courtyard with columns.
3. Use the self-timer if you want to step out of the shot — or stay in it; standing in the middle works fine and you being in the capture is expected, not a problem to avoid.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Apply the tiny planet reframe but tilt straight up instead of down — see [Tiny Planet & Inverted Tiny Planet](tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) for the base technique.
2. Export as the inverted planet view.

## Settings
- Photo mode, 72 MP, HDR photo on if there's sky in shot.

## Tips
- If you're in the shot, you effectively become one of the "towers" the ring points to — no need to always step out.
- The moving version of an inverted planet (walking laps rather than a static ground shot) is the "flower tunnel" / "rabbit hole" variant covered in [Walking Tiny Planet](walking-tiny-planet.md).
