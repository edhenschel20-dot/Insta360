# Roll Planet / Spin View / Jump Planet

Three related one-tap templates that turn a tiny planet into a moving shot.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Check app version (menu names vary by app version)

## Shoot it
- **Roll Planet:** camera static on a tripod, you walk a 2 m circle around it.
- **Spin View:** one 360 photo of you at 45–60° to the stick — the template animates it (8 variants available).
- **Jump Planet / Giant Jump:** you jump next to a static camera.

## Edit it (phone app)
Edit > Shot Lab > pick the matching template. The app converts your capture into a tiny planet and animates the roll/spin/jump automatically.

## Settings
- Photo mode for Spin View; static tripod video for the other two.

## Tips
- These build on the manual [Tiny Planet & Inverted](tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) technique — try the one-tap version first, then go manual if you want more control over the rotation.
