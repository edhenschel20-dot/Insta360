# Boat-Entry Roll-Through
The camera keeps rolling from the deck, through the backward roll off the boat, into the water and onto whatever's waiting underneath.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes (dive case)

## See it

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GngrCVfnwpM&t=836s) · The Bucket List Family

## Shoot it
1. Start recording before you even sit on the tube — hold the X5 (in the dive case) to your chest for the backward roll off the boat.
2. Extend the stick once you're under, rather than before you go in.
3. Kids: the same idea from the swim step rather than a full backward roll, holding a floaty.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Leave it as one continuous clip from boat chatter through to the first fish — the whole point is not having to cut between "before" and "after".
2. If you want a hard cut anyway, this is a natural spot for the [Portal Water Jump](portal-water-jump.md) transition.

## Settings
Full reference: [Underwater diving](../settings/underwater-diving.md).
- Dive Case Mode on before you seal the case — most settings lock once it's sealed.

## Tips
- This only works if you're already recording before the roll — decide that before you're sitting on the edge of the boat, not after.
- The entry itself often ends up being more interesting in the edit than you'd expect — don't trim it out by default.
