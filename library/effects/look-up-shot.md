# Look-Up Shot

Towering trees or buildings converge overhead as you move underneath them.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Hold the camera low, moving forward at a steady pace — nothing else needed, this can come from normal rolling footage under trees or buildings.

## Edit it (phone app)
One keyframe: tilt up, wide FOV.

## Settings
- Any resolution you're already shooting at.

## Tips
- Works especially well combined with [Object Reveal / Push-Through](object-reveal-push-through.md) or the doorway tilt in [Doorway/Tunnel Reveal](../shots/travel-and-city.md) — ceiling-to-vista tilts are a signature 360 move.
