# Rotating Reveal

The camera glides forward, then the view swings round to show what's behind or ahead of you — a landscape, or the family following on.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Just walk or ride forward smoothly, stick up, at a steady height. Nothing else to do — the 360 capture already has both directions.
2. If you want a deliberate version later (see intro), keep the walk steady and don't swing the stick side to side.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Scrub to where you want the reveal to start. Set the framing looking forward, tap a keyframe.
2. Move 2–3 seconds later, point the view at the subject (or away from it, to reveal scenery), tap another keyframe.
3. Tap the segment between the two keyframes and pick an ease-in/ease-out curve so the swing isn't robotic (menu names vary by app version).
4. Set FOV to the wide "Mega" option for extra depth.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K30 video is plenty of resolution to reframe from later.
- No special mode — this is a normal 360 clip, reframed after the fact.

## Tips
- This is the cheapest "wow" move in the whole library: two keyframes, one ease curve.
- Works from any normal rolling footage — no need to plan the shot in advance.
- **Turnaround variant:** hold on a nearby building or feature for about 3
  seconds, pan for 2 seconds, then tilt up to reveal the wider scene above
  it — a quick, structured take on the same idea.
