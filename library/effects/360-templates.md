# 360 Templates

A newer submenu next to Shot Lab (added around April 2026) offering whole-edit templates built around camera-movement styles — described as things like a "globe-style Earth zoom into your clip" and "planet spinner" intros, rather than one-off single-clip effects.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes (in the app)

## See it
_No verified example video yet. Add one with the "Add a video" form._

## Shoot it
1. No special shooting needed — like other one-tap templates, this works on clips you already have.
2. A clip with a recognisable location or landmark suits the "globe zoom" intro style best.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Edit tab > **360 Templates** (separate from Shot Lab).
2. Use this theme > pick your clips.
3. Individual template names inside this menu aren't published anywhere yet — scroll the list in the app to see what's currently on offer, and consider screenshotting it for reference.

## Settings
- No special camera settings — this is a post-shoot editing menu.

## Tips
- If you can't find "360 Templates" in your app version, it may be listed under a slightly different name — check both the Edit tab and Shot Lab.
- Good for a "trip intro" clip on travel footage since it's built around location reveals.
