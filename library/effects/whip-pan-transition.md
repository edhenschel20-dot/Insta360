# Whip-Pan Transition
A fast pan whips the view sideways (or up/down) at the end of one clip and settles into the next — the classic "whip pan" cut, done with keyframes instead of a physical camera swing.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Editing-only — any two clips work, nothing special to do while filming.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. On clip A, add two keyframes 0.5 seconds apart near the end, panning 180° between them.
2. On clip B, mirror the pan at the start: same 0.5-second spacing, same direction, so the motion continues rather than reversing.
3. Use a "Quick In/Out" style curve (menu names vary by app version) for the snap.
4. Cut on the blurriest point of the pan.
5. Works with the pan going any direction — left/right, or up/down for a vertical whip.

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at.

## Tips
- Direction matters: keep the pan going the same way across the cut (both left-to-right, say) or it reads as a bounce rather than a whip.
- See [Roll / Rotation Transition](roll-rotation-transition.md) for the rolling (rather than panning) version of the same idea, and [Spin Transition](spin-transition.md) for the app's simpler built-in preset.
