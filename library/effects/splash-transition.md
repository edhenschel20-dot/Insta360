# Splash Transition
Water hits the lens in one location; the cut lands as the water clears, and you're suddenly somewhere else.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## See it

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dCVNhJuPWMI&t=165s) · Gimbal Guru

## Shoot it
1. The X5 is natively waterproof for casual splashing — no case needed for this one.
2. Splash water directly onto the lens (a wave, a cupped-hand splash, a kid's water gun) in location A.
3. Do the same splash in location B.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Find the frame in clip A where the lens is fully covered in water.
2. Find the matching frame in clip B where the water is just starting to clear.
3. Cut exactly there — the water covering the lens hides the join.

## Settings
- Dry the lens between takes if you're doing more than one — residual droplets change how the splash reads.

## Tips
- Great one for the beach or the pool with the kids — it's forgiving because a splash naturally blurs and distorts the frame, so the cut doesn't need to be frame-perfect.
- Pairs with the underwater dip/surface version — see [Portal Water Jump / Dive Match-Cut](portal-water-jump.md) for the "go under, come up elsewhere" variant.
