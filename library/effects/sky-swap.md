# Sky Swap

Replaces a flat, grey sky with an atmospheric or starry one — fixes the thing that kills most 360 stills.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Check app version (menu names vary by app version)

## Shoot it
1. Open space, camera on a stick or tripod.
2. More than 15 seconds of footage with lots of visible sky in frame.

## Edit it (phone app)
Edit > Shot Lab > **Sky Swap** > pick Atmospheric/Starry (static) or Cube (interactive) templates.

## Settings
- Any resolution; the key requirement is a large, clean patch of sky in the shot.

## Tips
- Use sparingly — an obviously swapped sky looks like what it is if overdone.
- Sky Swap is described elsewhere as cloud-based AI; no source confirms whether it shares any cost/limit with [AI Warp & AI Effects](ai-warp-and-ai-effects.md) — worth a glance in-app before relying on it repeatedly.
