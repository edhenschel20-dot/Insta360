# Split Screen / MultiView

Two to four views of the same moment shown at once — front and selfie, or two people facing opposite lenses.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Nothing special — works on any clip where two things worth seeing are happening in different directions (a reaction and what caused it, two people talking).
2. If you're planning it, plant the stick between the two subjects so each faces a different lens.

## Edit it
Full step-by-step lives in [Multi-View editing how-to](../editing/multi-view.md) — the short version: pick a layout, drag the selection boxes, adjust each frame's zoom, optional blend edge or picture-in-picture.

## Settings
- 5.7K30, PureVideo if indoors — see [Events & indoors settings](../settings/events-and-indoors.md).

## Tips
- This is the best fixed answer to "who was watching the fireworks" — no tracking needed at all. See [Parades & Fireworks](../shots/parades-and-fireworks.md).
