# Direction-Lock Carlapse
The car (or bike, or golf cart) stays locked in the same spot in frame while the road behind streams past, sped up into a hyperlapse — Direction Lock does the "stay pinned" part automatically.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Stick out the window, braced against the door frame or armrest so it doesn't bounce around.
2. Turn on **Direction Lock** in the camera or app settings before you start (menu names vary by app version) — this keeps the view pointed the same way relative to the car regardless of small camera wobble.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. One keyframe is usually enough since Direction Lock is doing the heavy lifting.
2. Set clip speed to 16–32×.
3. Turn on Motion ND for the motion blur that sells the speed.

## Adapt it
- **Bicycle:** the same bracing trick works clipped to the handlebar at low speed.
- **Golf cart:** works the same way, held or clamped to the frame — good for a scenic drive around a course.

## Settings
- 5.7K30, Direction Lock on, Motion ND for the blur.

## Tips
- This is a good default for any stretch of road where nothing specific is happening — turn it into hyperlapse material rather than reframing a boring straight drive by hand.
- Pairs with [Hyperlapse / TimeShift](hyperlapse-and-timeshift.md) for the general version of this technique without the car-specific bracing.
