# AI Director

A free, on-camera feature: while your X5 is charging (from 80% battery or above), it analyses the clips you shot that day and sends a finished, edited clip to your phone. Worth knowing about specifically because it can help with the "AI edit lingers on the family instead of the action" problem — you get an automatic first pass, then tweak it afterwards rather than reframing everything by hand.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes, since firmware 1.13.21 (2026-09-15) with app 2.34.0 or later

## Shoot it
1. Turn AI Director on in the camera menu (look for Battery-life-optimised or Efficiency mode — menu names vary by app version).
2. Film in roughly the order you want the story told — the feature seems to reward clean starts and stops (a voice cue or gesture) between distinct moments, rather than one continuous unbroken roll.
3. Let the camera roll as you normally would the rest of the time; nothing else changes at shoot time.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Charge the camera to 80%+ and leave it — AI Director processes automatically while charging.
2. A finished clip appears on your phone; open it and trim, reorder or swap out any section you don't like (this is exactly the fix for the "too long on the family" problem — treat the auto-edit as a rough draft, not the final cut).

## Settings
- No special resolution settings — this works on whatever you already shot.
- Requires firmware 1.13.21+ and app 2.34.0+ on the X5.

## Tips
- Because it runs while charging, there's no shoot-time cost at all — turn it on and forget about it.
- If the auto-edit swings toward people over action (Ed's known issue with the app's other AI auto-edit), this is worth comparing against manually reframing the same footage — it may or may not have the same bias.
