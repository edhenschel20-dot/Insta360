# Glow Sticks & Light-Up Toys Slow-Mo
Kids waving glow sticks or light-up toys at dusk, slowed to a quarter speed so the light smears into ribbons instead of flashing past.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

**[general advice]** — no 360-specific glow-stick tutorial exists. Built from [Slow-Mo Particle Shot](slow-mo-particle-shot.md) and [Light Painting 360 Photo](light-painting-360-photo.md).

## Shoot it
1. **While there's still sky light (dusk):** camera low on the mini tripod in the middle of the kids, 4K120 (Bullet Time mode uses the same 4K120). Have them wave the glow sticks or light-up toys past the lenses on all sides — particles passing on every side is what sells the slow-mo.
2. **After full dark:** switch to PureVideo 5.7K30, EV −0.7, and give up on slow-mo — glow sticks are too dim for 120fps once it's properly dark.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Set clip speed to 0.25x through the best sweep of light.
2. [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) one child if several are waving sticks at once.

## Settings
Full reference: [Bullet time & slow motion settings](../settings/bullet-time-and-slow-motion.md).

## Tips
- Still-photo version: see [Light Painting 360 Photo](light-painting-360-photo.md) — a 20-second shutter with each child drawing a shape.
- The light-level cutoff is the key judgement call here: once it's fully dark, stop chasing slow-mo and switch to night settings instead.
