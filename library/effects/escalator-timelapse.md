# Escalator Timelapse
Stand still on an escalator or moving walkway while a timelapse turns the building around you into a warping stream — airports, malls, hotel lobbies.

**Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. **Longer, gentler warp:** 5.7K timelapse, 0.5 second interval, ND64 filter, for a longer ride at the same overall clip length.
2. **Shorter, wilder warp:** 11K, 2 second interval instead.
3. Hide the hand holding the stick behind the person in front of you, or set a mini tripod on the step ahead, and hold perfectly still.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Little editing needed — the timelapse interval does the warping; trim to length.

## Settings
- 5.7K, 0.5 s interval, ND64 (gentler) or 11K, 2 s interval (wilder). Full reference: [Timelapse & TimeShift settings](../settings/timelapse-and-timeshift.md).

## Tips
- Airports, shopping malls, and hotel lobbies with long escalators all work well for this.
- Pairs with [Cart / Trolley / Stroller Direction-Lock Timelapse](cart-trolley-stroller-timelapse.md) as a pair of "let a moving thing do the work" shots from the same travel day.
