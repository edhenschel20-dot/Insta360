# Legless Tiny Planet via Ground Stake
A tent-peg style ground stake holds the camera up instead of a tripod, so the tiny planet at the bottom of the frame has no tripod legs poking into it.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Likely (any ¼" ground spike works; not an official Insta360 accessory)

## Shoot it
1. Push a ¼"-threaded ground stake into turf or sand, screw the stick on top.
2. On a golf course, use the fringe or rough beside the green — never the green itself.
3. Beach and garden lawns both work well too.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Apply the standard tiny planet reframe — see [Tiny Planet & Inverted Tiny Planet](tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) for the base technique (FOV to max, tilt straight down).
2. Because there's no tripod in frame, the whole bottom of the planet is clean ground/turf.

## Settings
- Any resolution already shot at; a patterned surface (turf lines, sand) makes a better-looking planet.

## Tips
- Pairs well with [Walking Tiny Planet](walking-tiny-planet.md) if you want a moving version from the same spot.
- Golf etiquette: never stake into the green, and lift it the moment you're done — don't leave a group waiting on you to unscrew a mount.
