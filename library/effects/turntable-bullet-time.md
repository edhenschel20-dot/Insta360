# Turntable Bullet Time
The classic [Bullet Time](bullet-time.md) spin, but nobody has to hold a cord overhead — the camera sits on a rotating platform and spins around you instead.

**Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes (Bullet Time mode)

## Shoot it
1. Bullet Time mode.
2. Set the camera on a rotating platform — a phone-spinner tripod head, a lazy Susan, or a borrowed street-performer's spinner all work.
3. Stand still (with the kids, if they'll hold still) while the platform spins the world around you.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Standard Bullet Time reframe — the app auto-centres you on export.

## Settings
- Bullet Time mode, 4K120 (not PureVideo — see Tips).

## Tips
- Bullet Time uses 4K120, not PureVideo, so this works fine at night as long as you're under decent light (string lights, a stage, a lit patio) — it isn't a low-light mode.
- No arm fatigue and no cord to buy if you already own a small photo turntable.
- For several of these stitched into one reel, see [Bullet Time Mix](bullet-time-mix.md).
