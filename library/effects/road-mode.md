# Road Mode

A dash-cam-style mode: the camera records in a continuous loop, limited only by how much space is left on the SD card, rather than a fixed clip length.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. 360 video menu > **Road Mode**.
2. Mount securely (suction mount on the windscreen or dash, or the invisible stick braced against the wheel/mirror) and start recording — it'll keep going, looping over the oldest footage once the card fills, until you stop it.

## Adapt it
This mode is built for motorcycle and car dash-cam use specifically — continuous, long-duration recording where you genuinely might need hours of coverage (e.g. an incident review). **For an ordinary scenic drive, it's simpler to just buy a second SD card** and shoot normal 360 clips instead — you keep every moment rather than looping over earlier footage, and normal clips are much easier to find and edit afterwards.

## Settings
- Firmware 1.11.6 (2026-04-13) improved this mode — make sure you're on that version or later.

## Tips
- Only reach for Road Mode if continuous, uninterrupted coverage genuinely matters (e.g. dashcam-style safety recording); for capturing highlights of a road trip, ordinary clips reframe and edit far more easily.
- **Road Mode vs single-lens Loop mode as a travel dash cam:** one creator
  compares the two directly as dash-cam options — worth a watch if you're
  specifically weighing Road Mode against single-lens Loop recording,
  though this project still defaults to full 360 and ordinary clips (see
  above) for anything beyond genuine dash-cam use.
