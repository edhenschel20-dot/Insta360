# Ride / Run Stats Overlay
Speed, heart rate, gradient, and power from your watch or head-unit, drawn as a graphic over the footage in the app — no separate overlay app needed.

**Difficulty:** Medium (needs a GPS source linked before or during the ride) · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. The camera needs a GPS source for its own track: either the GPS Preview Remote / Action Remote, or keep the phone app connected while recording so it writes the phone's GPS to the camera.
2. Ride, walk, or play a round as normal — nothing else to do at shoot time.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. In the app, open the clip's Dashboard/stats panel (menu names vary by app version).
2. Link your watch or head-unit app (Garmin, Apple, Coros) — it auto-syncs to the clip by time.
3. Pick which stats to show and where; export as normal.

## Settings
- Requires GPS logging to be on for the clip — check this before you start, since it can't be added after the fact without a GPS track.

## Tips
- Good for a bike ride, a round of golf with a GPS watch, or a run — anywhere you already wear a GPS device.
- See also [Speed-Blur Shutter Trick](speed-blur-shutter-trick.md) for making the same footage feel faster.
