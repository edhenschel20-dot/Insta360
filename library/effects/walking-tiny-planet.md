# Walking Tiny Planet
Put the camera on the ground and run laps around it — reframed as a tiny planet, it looks like you're walking around the top of your own miniature world. Variants flip it inside-out into a "flower tunnel," "rabbit hole" or "wormhole."

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

The inverted variant is at @107s, "flower tunnel" at @213s, and further variants at @292s and @422s.

## Shoot it
1. Set the camera on a mini tripod (or just sitting on the ground), lens up.
2. Run 3–4 laps around it in a circle, keeping roughly the same distance out each time.
3. Open, flat ground works best — beach, field, plaza — so there's nothing close by to loom awkwardly in the wrap.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Apply the tiny planet preset (see [Tiny Planet & Inverted](tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) for the base technique — FOV to max, tilt straight down).
2. Because you're moving around the camera rather than the camera being still, keyframe the roll every few seconds to keep yourself sitting on top of the planet rather than sliding round its side.
3. For the inverted variants (flower tunnel, rabbit hole, wormhole), flip to tilt straight up instead of down, using the same laps.

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at. A patterned or textured ground (sand, tiles) makes a noticeably better planet.

## Tips
- Open areas like a beach or a field give the most room to run a clean lap without anything cutting into frame — a good one for a beach day with the kids.
- This builds on the static [Tiny Planet & Inverted](tiny-planet-and-inverted.md) technique, but needs more keyframing because you (not the scene) are the thing moving.
- **Hamster Wheel** is the inverse of this shot: instead of you walking on
  top of the planet, the camera sits on the ground in the centre of an
  open circle and you (or the kids) walk or run around it once, reframed
  so it looks like you're inside a giant wheel that rolls with you.
- **Bicycle version:** camera clamped to the head tube, extended out about
  a section and a half, ride 30 seconds; in the edit, hold record and
  physically turn your body and point the phone down to roll the view
  into a planet with you riding on top.
