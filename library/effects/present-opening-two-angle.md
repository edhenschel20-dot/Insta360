# Present-Opening Two-Angle
Get the person opening a gift and the giver watching them, from a single camera planted on the table — no one has to hold a phone, and you don't have to choose one face over the other while it's happening.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Plant the camera mid-table (or beside the tree, or wherever presents are being opened), lenses roughly toward both the opener and the giver.
2. Say "mark" as the paper comes off, so the moment's easy to find later — see [Hands-Off Triggers](hands-off-triggers.md).
3. Let it keep rolling through several gifts rather than stopping and restarting for each one.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Split the clip at the unwrapping moment and point one segment's fixed view at the opener, the next segment at the giver's reaction — the same trick as [Perspective-Shift Run](perspective-shift-run.md), applied to a table instead of a walk.
2. Or use [Split Screen / MultiView](split-screen-multiview.md) to show both faces at once instead of cutting between them.

## Settings
- Any standard resolution; PureVideo if indoor lighting is dim — see [Events & indoors settings](../settings/events-and-indoors.md).

## Tips
- Works for birthdays as well as Christmas — same table, same two-angle trick.
- Pairs well with [Christmas-Morning Tree Cam](christmas-morning-tree-cam.md) if you're already running that setup.
- **[general advice]:** no 360-specific tutorial exists for a birthday or present-opening shot; this is the existing reframe-splitting technique applied to a new situation, plus raw party uploads as a look reference.
