# Time Freeze (Animated 360 Photo)

A frozen moment that the camera flies around — built from a single still photo, not video.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Take a 360 photo (72 MP) of an action pose or a group mid-gesture — arms out, mid-laugh, mid-jump.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Open the photo and use Record/Quick Edit (swipe, or move the phone) to trace a camera path through the still, then export as video.
2. Or use the one-tap **Animate** effect for 360 photos if your app version has it (menu names vary by app version).

## Settings
- Shoot in Photo mode, 72 MP, ideally with HDR photo on for a bright sky.

## Tips
- Pairs well with [Tiny Planet Flip / Planet Landing](tiny-planet-flip-transition.md) as a title-card opener for a longer edit.
- Because it's a photo, there's no motion blur to fight — good for indoor or lower light.
