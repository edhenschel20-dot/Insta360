# Starlapse

A dedicated camera mode for night skies: either star trails (long streaks) or a smoother star-motion timelapse, shot on a tripod and processed afterwards.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Camera mode > **Starlapse** (available on X3 through X6, including the X5).
2. Tripod, somewhere genuinely dark — light pollution is the main thing that ruins this shot.
3. Set interval, shutter speed and ISO per the in-app guidance or the settings link below; leave it running for as long as you can (longer runs give more visible star movement or longer trails).

## Edit it (phone app or Studio)
1. Process directly in the app, or in Insta360 Studio — note the workflow changed around August 2026, so if your Studio version looks different from an older tutorial, follow the in-app prompts instead.
2. Choose star-trail (streaks) or star-motion (smoother drift) processing depending on the look you want.

## Tips
- A tripod is non-negotiable here — any hand movement ruins the long exposures.
- Pairs well with any night event where you're staying in one spot for a while (fireworks, a campsite, a beach after dark).
- **Timed capture** can start a Starlapse (or timelapse) later without you
  needing to be there to press the button — up to 10 hours out.
- **Alternative:** a plain Photo › Interval sequence (ISO 800, 30 s shutter)
  is a simpler manual way to get a star-motion look if Starlapse mode
  doesn't suit the scene.
- **Which mode for the night, in general:** PureVideo = fast, no thinking ·
  Manual video = predictable light · Interval photos = max quality when you
  can plan the shot · Starlapse = for things your eyes can't otherwise see
  (Gaba_VR's rule of thumb).
