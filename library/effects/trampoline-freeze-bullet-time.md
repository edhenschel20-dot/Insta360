# Trampoline Freeze & Bullet Time
Two easy trampoline shots: the jump frozen right at its peak, or the whole garden spinning around the bouncing child.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## See it
_No verified example video yet. Add one with the "Add a video" form._

## Shoot it
1. Set the camera on the mini tripod on the trampoline mat itself, at the edge, lenses toward the jumper — the mat throws the camera about a little as they bounce, which is part of the look, not a problem.
2. Or, for a self-orbit version, have the child hold a short stick while they bounce.
3. Shoot 4K120 for slow-motion headroom on the freeze.
4. Keep the camera inside the safety net, and keep the tripod legs flat so nothing catches a foot.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. For the freeze: apply [Freeze Go](freeze-go.md) at the peak of a jump.
2. For the spin: slow the clip to around 0.25× and reframe as a [Low-Angle Ground Spin](low-angle-ground-spin.md) around the bouncing child.

## Settings
- 4K120 for slow-motion.

## Tips
- Net safety comes first: camera inside the net, tripod legs flat, and don't let a trampoline session become a reason to leave the mat unsupervised.
- **[general advice]:** no 360-specific tutorial exists for trampoline shots specifically; this combines the existing Freeze Go and Low-Angle Ground Spin techniques with the many raw trampoline clips online as a look reference.
