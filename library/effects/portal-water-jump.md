# Portal Water Jump / Dive Match-Cut

You jump into water in one place and the cut makes it look like you surface somewhere else entirely.

**Difficulty:** Medium–Hard · **Works on X5:** Yes

## See it
<div class="video-embed"><iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/PZx1yj1pUvA?start=158" title="10 sensational Insta360 X5 tricks for 2026" loading="lazy" allow="fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZx1yj1pUvA&t=158s) · Gimbal Guru

## Shoot it
1. This is a deliberate, planned shot — not something you get from normal rolling footage.
2. Clip A: jump in, and dunk the camera under as you enter the water.
3. Clip B: at the second location, do the reverse — camera comes up out of the water as you surface.
4. The X5 is natively waterproof for casual use; the Invisible Dive Case gives sharper results if you're going deeper. See [Underwater/diving settings](../settings/underwater-diving.md).

## Edit it (phone app)
Cut exactly at the moment the lens crosses the water surface in both clips, so the "splash" lines up.

## Settings
- 5.7K30, PureVideo/HDR if there's glare off the water — see [Snorkelling & water settings](../settings/snorkelling-and-water.md).

## Tips
- Practice the timing of the dunk/surface a couple of times before the take that matters — the cut only sells if both halves match in speed.

## Variants
- **Underwater Transition:** dip the camera fully under the water in one place, cut as it surfaces somewhere else — Gimbal Guru [demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_ZB5rn8N_4&t=328s).
- **Dive Transition:** the person dives *up* into shot 2 instead of surfacing normally — [demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZx1yj1pUvA&t=644s).
