# Bike Tail-Mount Drone Chase
Mounted behind the saddle, the camera looks like a drone hunting to keep up with you — a bike-specific version of [Fake Drone / Aerial Shot](fake-drone-shot.md).

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Fit the Insta360 Bike Tail Mount Kit (seat post + saddle rails), or clamp the stick to the rear tube, extended about one and a half sections.
2. Smooth tarmac only — it gets too bouncy on gravel.
3. Keep well clear of other riders and cars: don't extend the 3 m stick version near traffic or a group ride.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Switch to viewfinder (hand-movement) reframe mode.
2. Zoom in and out and rock the phone gently as you "direct" the shot, to fake a drone hunting to keep up with you.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K30.

## Tips
- The 3 m stick version looks wilder but is genuinely more to manage — only use it somewhere quiet, away from other people or vehicles.
- Combine with [Wheel-Centred Handlebar Hyperlapse](cyclist-pov-hyperlapse.md) for a front-and-back pair of shots from the same ride.
