# Car Exterior Stick & Drive-By Shots
A set of chase-car-style angles built without a chase car: front three-quarter, rear three-quarter, a wheel close-up, a top-down roof shot, and a roadside drive-by as the car passes a fixed camera.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. **Front/rear three-quarter and wheel angles:** extend the stick out the window, braced on the wing mirror; hold steady, roughly 30 seconds per angle.
2. **Roof top-down:** suction-cup mount on the roof, pointed down.
3. **Roadside drive-by:** set the X5 on a tripod at the roadside; the car drives past — pan to follow it in post rather than trying to pan live.
4. Details that sell it: catching the foot on the pedal, and the reflection in the wing mirror.
5. **Safety:** watch road signs and traffic at all times — this is not a "set and forget while driving" shot, it needs a passenger or a stopped moment to rig safely.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe each angle to the part of the 360 sphere you actually want (the wheel, the mirror reflection, the roof-down view).
2. For the roadside drive-by, pan the frame to follow the car as it passes, since the camera itself didn't move.

## Adapt it
- **Golf cart:** the same bracing-on-the-frame trick works at low speed for a fairway drive-by.
- **Bicycle:** works the same way clipped to the handlebar or frame, again at low speed.

## Settings
- 5.7K30. See [Fast motion & driving settings](../settings/fast-motion-and-driving.md).

## Tips
- Roughly 30 seconds per angle is plenty — you're picking the best few seconds of each in the edit, not using the whole take.
- Pairs with [Driving & Road Trips](../shots/driving-and-road-trips.md) for the rest of a road-trip day's plan.
- **Bumper/GTA chase-cam recipe:** triple/electric suction cup on the boot
  or bonnet, stick extended two sections pointing up; in the edit, turn on
  Direction Lock, set speed to 4×, one keyframe with the car centred, and
  Motion ND on. The Electric Suction Cup Mount (self-pumping, warns of
  pressure loss) is the current official version of this mount.
- **At night:** the same recipe works in PureVideo for a "night cruise"
  look — same reframe as the daytime version.
- **Older X3-era variants worth knowing:** Fly Lapse shot from the car,
  head-strap driving POV, and MultiView pairing this shot with a
  dash-cam-style interior mount.
