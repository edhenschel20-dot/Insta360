# Zoom-In Transition (Zoom Cut)
The view pushes in tight at the end of clip A, then clip B starts wide and settles — a zoom-driven cut between scenes. The app also has a one-tap preset ("Zoom Cut") that does the same job without keyframes.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Editing-only — any two clips work.

## Edit it (phone app)
**One-tap route:**
1. Edit › Create a Video › add both clips › tap the white box between them in the timeline.
2. Pick **Zoom Cut** from the transitions library.

**Manual route (more control):**
1. On clip A, keyframe FOV from 110° down to 80° over the last second.
2. On clip B, keyframe FOV from 140° back to 110° over the first second.
3. Adding a slight roll alongside the zoom (a few degrees either way) makes the cut feel less flat.

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at.

## Tips
- Start with the one-tap **Zoom Cut** preset — it's a genuinely zero-effort option for a scene change and looks nearly identical to the manual version.
- Only bother with manual keyframes if you want a specific FOV combination the preset doesn't give you.
