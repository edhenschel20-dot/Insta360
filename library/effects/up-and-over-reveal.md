# Up-and-Over Reveal
The camera swings up and over from behind you, landing on a waterfall, bay, or viewpoint in front — a crane-like arc rather than a straight lift.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Fully extend the 3 m stick, held down and behind you.
2. Slowly bring it up and over your head in one smooth arc, ending pointed forward at the view.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe 1: on you, zoomed out, stick low behind you.
2. Keyframe 2: at the top of the arc, overhead.
3. Keyframe 3: turned forward, settled on the view.

## Settings
- No special mode — a normal 360 clip, framed with three keyframes.

## Tips
- Different from [Rise Shot (Crane)](rise-shot-crane.md), which lifts straight up into the sky — this one arcs forward and lands on a view rather than the sky.
- A good closing shot for a beach day or a scenic stop on a drive.
