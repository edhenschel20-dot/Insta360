# Swing-Analysis Camera Positions
Where to plant the camera so a swing replay is genuinely useful: face-on at hand height, down-the-line through the hands, or a rear view at belt height for hip and spine tilt.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. **Face-on:** camera at hand height, exactly 90° to the target line — use an alignment stick on the ground to check it.
2. **Down-the-line:** behind the hands, lens at hand height, pointing at the target through your hands.
3. **Rear view:** belt height, shows hip and spine tilt — a third option from the same setup if you want it.
4. A 360 camera can't be in two spots at once: plant it face-on for driver sessions and down-the-line for irons, and reframe the other angles as best you can from the same clip.
5. Plant it in the same exact spot each session — 16 inches of camera offset changes the apparent swing plane, so consistency matters more than which single angle you pick.
6. Wear light clothing for slow-mo — an all-black outfit can turn into a silhouette against some backgrounds.
7. Practice range only, never mid-round.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. 5.7K60 (or 4K120), then slow to 0.25–0.5x through impact.
2. See [Form Check Slow-Mo](form-check-slow-mo.md) for the general reframe-to-multiple-angles technique this extends — that page had no placement rules; this page is those rules.

## Tips
- The one-camera, two-angle limitation is real — decide which angle matters more for that session before you set up.
- Golf etiquette still applies: tripod off to the side, not in anyone's line.
