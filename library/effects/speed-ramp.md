# Speed Ramp

Fast, then real-time (or half-speed) at the key moment, then fast again.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
Normal video — nothing special needed at shoot time. This is entirely an editing decision, so it works well on footage you already let roll.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Use the Speed tool on the clip, adjusting per segment (0.25x–8x).
2. A common recipe: 16x for the hyperlapse-y lead-in/lead-out, dropping to 1x or 0.5x for the key moment in the middle.

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at.

## Tips
- Great for turning a long "let it roll" clip into a highlight without cutting anything out — just change speed around the good bit.
