# Whitewater Follow-Cam & Hand-Off
Wade behind a child riding a bodyboard down the whitewater, then hand the camera off so the next clip is their own POV.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7kQaWovU63M&t=658s) · Stoked For Travel

## Shoot it
1. **Follow cam:** stand in the water behind the rider with the camera on a floaty handle or short stick, and walk or swim after them as they ride the whitewater in.
2. **Hand-off:** film the ride in from the water, then pass the camera to the rider for their own POV on the paddle back out.
3. **Selfie-over-the-board:** stick above your head, looking down along the board, for a third option from the same session.
4. You don't need to aim carefully with the X5 — hold the stick low over the water, let the 360 catch the rider and the beach, and reframe afterwards.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keep the lens toward the child when reframing so they don't end up split across the seam.
2. Combine with the [Splash Transition](splash-transition.md) for the moment whitewater hits the lens.

## Settings

## Tips
- Switching between follow cam, hand-off, and overhead within the same session is what gives you variety to cut between — don't just shoot one angle all day.
- Works just as well for the kids on a floatie or being pulled along, not just bodyboarding.
