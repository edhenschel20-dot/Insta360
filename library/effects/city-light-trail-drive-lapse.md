# City Light-Trail Drive-Lapse
Filmed from inside a moving car, headlights and tail-lights turn into long streaks of light through the city.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Likely (the light-trail look shown here uses a desktop workflow; the in-camera route below is the simpler X5 option)

## Shoot it
1. Mount the camera on a tripod or clamp inside the car, facing out through the windscreen or a side window.
2. Photo mode > **Interval**, RAW, **1 second shutter**, and drive for around 5 minutes through well-lit streets. Overexposed frames recover fine in RAW.
3. **Simpler X5-only route (no desktop step):** use Timelapse mode 11K, 2 second interval, with a manual 1 second shutter (see [Night 11K Timelapse with ND16](night-11k-timelapse-nd16.md) for why shutter = half the interval) — the camera builds the finished video itself, no desktop assembly needed.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. With the Timelapse route, trim and reframe in the app as normal.
2. With the Interval-stills route, the sequence needs assembling on desktop first (heavier than the usual phone-only flow — save it for a planned night out, not every drive).

## Adapt it
- **Passenger seat on a dinner run:** just clamp the camera to the dash or door pillar instead of a tripod.
- **Golf-cart path lights:** the same interval trick works at cart speed on a lit path in the evening.

## Settings
- Interval route: Photo > Interval, RAW, 1 s shutter.
- Timelapse route: 11K, 2 s interval, manual 1 s shutter. Full reference: [Timelapse & TimeShift settings](../settings/timelapse-and-timeshift.md).

## Tips
- The in-camera Timelapse route is the one to reach for on holiday — it needs no laptop.
- Pairs with [Night Cruise GTA Shot](night-cruise-gta-shot.md) if you want a chase-cam angle from the same drive.
