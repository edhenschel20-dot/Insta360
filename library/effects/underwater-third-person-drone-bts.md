# Underwater Third-Person "Drone" & BTS Cam
Two uses of the same stick-out shot underwater: a "drone" view of you and your buddy from above/ahead, and a behind-the-scenes camera showing you filming with a phone.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-twDRkZcJ04&t=105s) · Aaron and Cassie

BTS use at @142s, and exporting one clip two ways at @181s, in the same video.

## Shoot it
1. **Drone view:** hold the stick out in front of or above you and your buddy, one lens turned back toward you both, to show the scale of the reef.
2. **BTS cam:** hold the X5 yourself while someone else films you with a phone — the 360 becomes the behind-the-scenes camera, capturing the whole scene including the phone operator.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Export the same clip twice: 16:9 for a TV/YouTube cut, 9:16 for reels — no need to reshoot for each format.

## Tips
- The BTS use is a nice way to get a "making of" clip for free from a dive you were already filming.
