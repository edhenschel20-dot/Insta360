# Object Reveal / Push-Through

The camera passes through or past a railing, bike, doorway or car, and the subject appears as the camera clears the object.

**Difficulty:** Easy–Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Long stick, move the camera through or alongside the object at a steady pace — a doorway, a bike, a railing, someone's shoulder.
2. Keep going past it in one smooth move; no need to stop.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe 1: framed so the object hides the subject.
2. Keyframe 2: at the end, framed on the subject, clear of the object.
3. Optional: change FOV between the two keyframes (Dewarp to Mega) for extra punch on the reveal.

## Settings
- 8K30 video for a clean, detailed reveal.

## Tips
- Doorways, gates and rows of trees all work — see also the doorway/tunnel version under [people shots](../shots/people-shots-with-the-x5.md).
- Keep the subject at least 1 m from the lens so the stitch line doesn't cut through them.
