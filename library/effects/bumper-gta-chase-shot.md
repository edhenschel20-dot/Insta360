# Bumper / GTA Chase Shot
A third-person, video-game-style chase view of the car — the mount disappears in the reframe, leaving what looks like a drone or chase car following just behind.

**Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Fit a triple or electric suction cup on the boot or bonnet, stick extended two sections, pointing up.
2. Drive for 2 minutes or more.
3. **Do's and don'ts:** mount the stick so its arm folds *against* acceleration, not braking — otherwise it sways forward under braking. Never use cheap suction cups for anything above walking pace.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Turn on **Direction Lock**.
2. Set clip speed to 4×.
3. One keyframe with the car centred in frame.
4. Turn on Motion ND for extra blur at speed.

## Adapt it
- **Golf cart:** the same mount and recipe work at cart speed.
- **Bicycle:** works clipped to a bike rack at low speed too.

## Settings
- 5.7K30, Direction Lock on, 4× speed, Motion ND on. Full reference: [Fast motion & driving settings](../settings/fast-motion-and-driving.md).
