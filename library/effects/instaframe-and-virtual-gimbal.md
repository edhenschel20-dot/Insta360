# InstaFrame & Virtual Gimbal

The camera tracks a subject you pick and bakes out a smooth, gimbal-stable flat video in-camera — either 4K30 on its own, or a 1080p flat video plus a full 5.7K 360 backup. Includes Pitch Lock, Follow and FPV gimbal styles, an on-screen joystick, and in-camera 360 Spin / Barrel Roll moves. It can track any subject you select, including kids, not just the person holding the camera.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes, firmware from December 2025 onward

## Shoot it
1. Mode > **InstaFrame**.
2. Tap the subject you want tracked on screen — this can be a child running around, not just yourself.
3. Choose a gimbal mode (Pitch Lock, Follow or FPV) depending on how much camera movement you want to keep.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keep **"360 video backup" ON** — this matches shooting in full 360 and means you still have the complete sphere to fall back on if the tracked flat video misses something.
2. Review the flat output; if the tracking picked the wrong moment or subject, you still have the 360 backup to reframe manually.

## Adapt it
- **Walking / kids running:** works handheld while walking alongside the kids — tap them as the subject and let the gimbal smoothing do the work.
- **Bicycle:** mount on a handlebar bar mount; Follow or FPV mode handles the bumps better than Pitch Lock.
- Most demo videos for this feature show it on a motorbike or vlog rig — the tracking and stabilisation work the same handheld or bike-mounted.

## Settings
- 2.35:1 cinematic aspect ratio option added January 2026 (firmware 1.10.7) if you want a widescreen look.
- Full X5 shooting modes reference is worth a look if you're new to the mode list.

## Tips
- Always leave the 360 backup on — it's the safety net if InstaFrame tracks the wrong thing or loses the subject.
- Good for a single continuous activity (a kid on a scooter, a bike ride) where you want a ready-to-share flat clip without editing.
