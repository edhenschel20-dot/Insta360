# Fly Lapse

A fake FPV-drone hyperlapse, built automatically by Shot Lab from a straight walk.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes (menu names vary by app version)

## Shoot it
1. 5.7K30 (X5 can also do 8K). Invisible Selfie Stick at full length.
2. Walk about 3 minutes in a straight line. Symmetrical streets with close buildings work best.

## Edit it (phone app)
Edit > Shot Lab > **Fly Lapse** > Use this theme. The AI picks segments; preview, then export.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K, stick fully extended.

## Tips
- This is the one-tap version of the manual [Fake FPV Dive](fake-fpv-dive.md) — try this first before hand-keyframing.
