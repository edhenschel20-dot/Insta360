# Dolly Zoom / Horizon Pull

The subject stays the same size while the background rushes in or out — the classic Hitchcock "vertigo" effect.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. When you want a hero shot: walk straight toward (or away from) a subject at a steady pace, camera at their chest height.
2. Keeping the walk speed even matters more than anything else here.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe 1: wide FOV.
2. Keyframe 2 (as you get closer, or further away): narrower FOV — adjust so the subject's size in frame stays roughly constant while the background lurches.
3. Check the one-tap **Dolly Zoom** template in Shot Lab first (see [Other Shot Lab templates](other-shot-lab-templates.md)) — it may do this for you without hand-placed keyframes.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or 8K30, whatever gives a steady walking pace enough detail to reframe from.

## Tips
- 360 makes this trivial compared to a normal camera, because FOV here is just a slider, not a physical lens swap.
