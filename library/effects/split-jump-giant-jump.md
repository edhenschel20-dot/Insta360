# Split Jump & Giant Jump
Two Shot Lab templates that both turn several separate jumps into one impossible-looking clip. **Split Jump:** six jumps in six different places, cut together into one sequence. **Giant Jump:** you jump beside a tripod and land looking giant in the next location.

**Difficulty:** Medium (multiple locations, timed jumps) · **Works on X5:** Likely — both are in the X5's Shot Lab list per the manual; confirm the templates are still present in your current app version.

## Shoot it
**Split Jump:**
1. Stick fully extended.
2. Move the stick backwards as you (or the kids) jump, to give more hang time in frame — shoot 4K120 on the X5 for the smoothest slow-down.
3. Repeat the jump in up to six different locations — six spots around the garden, or six stops on an outing, work fine.

**Giant Jump:**
1. Set the tripod down on the ground.
2. Stand about 1 m from it.
3. Jump two or three times, with a 1–2 second pause between jumps, so the app has a clean read on the motion.
4. Repeat in up to six locations.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. **Split Jump:** Shot Lab › Split Jump › Use this theme › pick the six clips you shot.
2. **Giant Jump:** the same import flow — Shot Lab › Giant Jump › Use this theme › pick your six clips.
3. The app handles the cut/composite automatically once the clips are selected; there's no manual keyframing step.

## Settings
- 4K120 if hang-time smoothness matters to you; otherwise standard resolution.

## Tips
- Kids' version: six jumps round the garden, or one jump at each stop on a family outing — the six-location structure works for any casual repeated activity.
