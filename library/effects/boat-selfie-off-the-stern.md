# Boat Selfie / Off-the-Stern Shot
Held out over the back of a boat, this reads almost like a drone shot circling round — without needing a drone.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Rest the extended stick under your arm, or hold it out sideways over the stern — turning the camera on its side helps hide the bend of the stick in the reframe.
2. Hold steady while the boat is moving — the boat's motion supplies most of the "drone" feeling on its own.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Set clip speed to around 32×.
2. Keyframe to keep the boat recentred in frame through the speed ramp.
3. Optional: end on a look-back at yourself or the group for a closing beat.

## Settings
- 5.7K30, Motion ND on for extra blur at speed.

## Tips
- Good one for a snorkel-boat or ferry ride out to a dive/snorkel spot — see [Snorkelling & water settings](../settings/snorkelling-and-water.md) for the rest of that day's setup.
- Turning the camera sideways on the stick is the detail that sells this — a straight-up stick reads obviously as a selfie stick in the reframe.
