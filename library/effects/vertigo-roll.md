# Vertigo Roll
A slow rotation of the view while you walk somewhere with real height under you — a bridge, cliff path, or viewpoint — for an unsettled, vertigo-ish feel.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## See it

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GmYovhkMmKY&t=520s) · Ben Claremont

## Shoot it
1. Hold the stick above head height, walking normally across the bridge/walkway/viewpoint — nothing special to do at shoot time, this comes from footage you'd shoot anyway.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Keyframe 1: camera at a slight angle.
2. Keyframe 2, several seconds later: rotated further still — small, precise roll values rather than a big swing.
3. Use an ease curve between them so the rotation reads as a slow drift rather than a snap.

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at.

## Tips
- Small roll values do more work than you'd expect on a 360 shot — a few degrees over several seconds is usually enough to unsettle the frame without looking like a mistake.
- Good for viewpoints and drop-offs where there isn't much else happening, but the height itself is the point.
