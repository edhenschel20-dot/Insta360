# Luau / Fire-Dancer Night Show
A seated evening show — torches, fire knives, a lit stage — filmed from your table without ever leaving the family to go find a better spot.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

**[general advice]** — no 360-specific tutorial for a fire-dancer show exists yet. This page adapts the [Lighted Parade](../shots/lighted-parade.md) plan and [Night and low light settings](../settings/night-and-low-light.md) to a seated stage show.

## Shoot it
1. Wipe both lenses before the show starts — stage lights plus a smudge make halos you can't fix later.
2. Plant the stick in the mini tripod at the table edge, or hold it at chest height, lenses facing the stage rather than side-on — that keeps the performers off the stitch seam.
3. Let it roll for the whole show. Say "mark" when the fire knives come out.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Fixed view on the stage for most of the show.
2. Switch to [Split-Screen MultiView](split-screen-multiview.md) with the kids' faces for the fire finale.
3. Only slow the fire spins down if you shot 5.7K60 — PureVideo doesn't do 60fps, so pick one: night-mode quality or slow-motion, not both.

## Settings
Full reference: [Night and low light](../settings/night-and-low-light.md).
- PureVideo 5.7K30 (or 8K30), EV −0.7 to −1 so the flames don't blow out to white blobs.
- White balance auto, then lock it once the stage lights settle.

## Tips
- Facing the lenses at the stage rather than side-on is the one deliberate choice here — it keeps the performers off the seam instead of splitting them across two lenses.
- This works for any seated fire or dance show, not just a luau.
