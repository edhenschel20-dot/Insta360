# Clone Trail

Several copies of the same person trailing behind them as they walk across a static shot.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Check app version (menu names vary by app version)

## Shoot it
1. Camera static — tripod, or held steady on a plant/table.
2. One person moves across the scene in a single direction while the camera keeps rolling.

## Edit it (phone app)
Edit > Shot Lab > **Clone Trail** (also called Shadow Clone) — the app composites the copies automatically.

## Settings
- Any resolution; a static camera is the only real requirement.

## Tips
- Static camera means whoever is shooting can step out of frame entirely, or be one of the clones themselves.
- Related templates: **Clone Loop** (walk round the camera doing an action, you're cloned as it pans) and **Shadow Clone** (different actions in different spots, 10-15 s each) — Ben Claremont [demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3-5jjbZubYo&t=472s).
