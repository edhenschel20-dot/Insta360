# Hamster Wheel
You (or the kids) appear to walk inside a giant wheel that rolls along with you — the inverse of Walking Tiny Planet, where you're on top of the world instead of inside it.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Set the camera on the ground in the centre of an open, flat circle.
2. Walk (or have the kids run) around it once, keeping roughly the same distance out.
3. Open ground with nothing else nearby works best — a lawn, a beach, a plaza.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Tilt the reframe 90°, distortion around 1, and widen the FOV until the "wheel" fills the frame.
2. Pan so you sit at the bottom of the wheel.
3. Add an end keyframe at the same tilt/distortion with a slight zoom in.

## Settings
- Any resolution already shot at.

## Tips
- The inverse trick to try alongside this one is [Walking Tiny Planet](walking-tiny-planet.md) — same laps, opposite feel.
- Works well for a beach-day loop with the kids running the circle.
