# Freeze Go

The video freezes at the peak of a jump or action while the camera keeps moving around it.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## See it
_No verified example video yet. Add one with the "Add a video" form._

## Shoot it
Normal video with a clear peak moment — a jump, a catch, a high-five. No special setup needed.

## Edit it (phone app)
Apply the Movement template **Freeze Go** (in the "Highlights" category) — the AI finds the peak moment for you (menu names vary by app version).

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at; higher fps (5.7K60/4K120) gives a cleaner freeze frame if the action is fast.

## Tips
- Works well straight off long rolling footage of kids playing or jumping — you don't need to know in advance which jump will be "the one."
