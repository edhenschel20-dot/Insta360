# Curtain Reveal / Natural Fade
Open a set of curtains onto a view for a reveal shot — or close them at the end of a clip for a fade to black that doesn't need any editing at all.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Set the X5 on a tripod inside the room, framed toward the curtains and the view beyond.
2. Set exposure manually — the bright outside view and the darker room will otherwise fight each other on auto exposure.
3. Trigger the shot with the remote (or voice/gesture start) so you can walk into frame and open the curtains yourself.
4. For the fade-to-black version, do the same in reverse: start with the view open, walk over and close the curtains at the end of the clip.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Zoom and rotate freely in post — because it's 360, you can reframe the reveal however you like after the fact rather than committing to a framing at shoot time.

## Settings
- Manual exposure is the important one here — auto exposure will hunt between the bright window and the dim room.

## Tips
- A genuinely free "natural fade to black" without any timeline editing — useful as a closer for a hotel-room or travel-day segment.
- Works well as an opener too: start on closed curtains, open them, reveal the scenic view beyond.
