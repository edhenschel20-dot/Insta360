# Low-Angle Ground Skim

A camera racing along just above the ground — dramatic, close-up texture rushing past.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Point the stick down, camera just above the surface — don't let the lens actually touch the ground.
2. Keep some distance from any subject you're skimming past.

## Edit it (phone app)
Frame on the subject with a single reframe, or track it with [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) if it's moving.

## Settings
- 5.7K30 or higher fps for a smoother skim if you're moving fast.

## Tips
- Watch for the stick's shadow crossing the stitch line in bright sun.
- "The step over" (skim low, then step over an object as the camera passes it) and Brandon Li's "Under Over" are two named variants worth a watch — no transcript exists for either, so check the video rather than this page for the exact move: [demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-64ENzj6usE&t=417s), [demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=awhRm5ruOuo&t=138s).
