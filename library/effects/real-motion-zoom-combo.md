# Real Motion + Zoom Combo
When you're already moving — on a boat, in a gondola, up a cable car — a slow zoom layered on top of the real motion makes the shot feel more deliberate. Zoom out as you pull away ("Sail Away"), zoom in as you approach ("Zoom Dolly" for tension).

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. Nothing special — just have the camera rolling while you're on the boat, gondola, or helicopter cabin. Let it roll as normal.

## Edit it (phone app)
**Sail Away (zoom out as you leave):**
1. Keyframe 1: closer/narrower FOV, near the start.
2. Keyframe 2: wider FOV, later in the clip, timed to when the boat/gondola is pulling away.

**Zoom Dolly (zoom in for tension, as you approach):**
1. Keyframe 1: zoomed in tight, at the start.
2. Keyframe 2: zoom right out, as you arrive or as the moment lands.

## Settings
- Any resolution you already shot at — this is purely a reframe/FOV move on top of real movement.

## Tips
- This is one of the easiest "make it look intentional" edits in the library — you're not adding motion, just adding a zoom to motion that already exists.
- Works especially well on a snorkel/dive boat pulling away from a dock, or a cable car climbing away from a valley floor.
