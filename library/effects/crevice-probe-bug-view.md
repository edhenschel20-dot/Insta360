# Crevice Probe / Bug View
Push the stick into a gap, under a ledge, or low through grass and flowers — the camera goes where you physically can't fit.

**Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Works on X5:** Yes

## Shoot it
1. **Underwater/rockpool:** push the stick into a crevice, under a rock ledge, or along the seabed.
2. **On land ("bug view"):** hold the stick low, threading it through grass, flowers, or a rock pool at ground level, following a path like an ant would.
3. Reframe forward at a wide field of view once you're through.

## Edit it (phone app)
1. Reframe wide, forward-facing, to sell the "probe" feeling of squeezing into a tight space.

## Settings
- No special mode — a normal 360 clip, reframed close-up afterwards.

## Tips
- Kids tend to love following a specific path (an ant's route, a crab's hole) with this one — give them the stick and let them lead.
- Pairs with [Low-Angle Ground Skim](low-angle-ground-skim.md), which is the same low-to-the-ground idea moving in a straight line rather than probing into a gap.
