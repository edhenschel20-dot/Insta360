# Slow-Motion Tracked Run

Cinematic slow motion of someone running, riding, or otherwise moving fast, with the camera keeping them in frame.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Works on X5:** Yes

## See it
<div class="video-embed"><iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/PZx1yj1pUvA?start=580" title="10 sensational Insta360 X5 tricks for 2026" loading="lazy" allow="fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZx1yj1pUvA&t=580s) · Gimbal Guru

## Shoot it
Shoot in 5.7K60 or 4K120 — these give roughly 4x slow-motion on playback. No special framing needed at shoot time.

## Edit it (phone app)
Use [Deep Track](../editing/deep-track.md) on the runner or rider, then set clip speed to 0.25–0.5x.

## Settings
Full reference: [Bullet time & slow motion settings](../settings/bullet-time-and-slow-motion.md).

## Tips
- Good default for kids running around, dogs, or anything on wheels — shoot normally, decide the slow-mo moment later.
