# Join clips and finish

Once Claude has exported the edited clips (flat MP4 files), you can
join several into one finished video.

## On the laptop: Claude Code and ffmpeg
Ask **Claude Code** (the terminal on the laptop), not the desktop app:
> "Join the clips in `Downloads\<folder>` in this order, with
> [cross-fades / fade through black / quick cuts to the beat], add
> `<music>.mp3` with a fade-out, title '<text>', export 1080p."

This uses ffmpeg (already installed on the laptop) and very little of
the Claude allowance. If the video will be posted anywhere, use
royalty-free music, for example from the YouTube Audio Library.

**Insta360 Studio v5 and later** also has multi-clip editing with music
and effects, so Claude could do the joining in Studio in the same
session.

## On the phone: the Insta360 app
Exported clips are small enough for LocalSend (turn the VPN off), a USB
cable into `DCIM` or `Movies`, or the hub's drive. In the Insta360 app,
add them from the **phone album**. That gives you transitions (tap the
square between clips), music, Auto Edit (clips over 2 seconds) and
FlashCut templates.

- **No 360 tricks** on flat clips: no reframing, Deep Track, tiny planet
  or Shot Lab. Do those in Studio first.
- **Your own music** may not be allowed in the app (one review said so;
  check it). For your own song, join the clips with ffmpeg instead.
