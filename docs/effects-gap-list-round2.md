# Effects gap list, round 2 — filling the thin groups

Researched 2026-09-26 for the Insta360 X5 + phone app. Round 1 is `effects-gap-list.md` (86 effects now exist; see `library/effects/index.md`). This round targets the thin situation groups: **Night (3), Car & road trip (4), Golf & sport (7), Bicycle (7), Beach & water (15)**, plus 360 photo tricks, editing recipes, accessories-to-shots, and a sweep of 2025–2026 creator "top 10" videos.

Every YouTube ID below was verified live with the oEmbed endpoint (HTTP 200) on 2026-09-26. Start times (`@seconds`) come from the video's own chapter markers (yt-dlp), or from the auto-transcript where a chapter had none (marked *≈*). Upload dates are from yt-dlp metadata.

**How to read the tables.** Columns follow the spec front matter: *Category* is the `category:` slug, *Good for* the `good_for:` slugs (1–4). *X5?* = works on the X5 (yes / likely / unknown). 💎 = uses limited AI generations. Where the example was shot on a bike, boat or older camera, the *How* cell says how Ed adapts it (let it roll, always full 360, he's in shot).

**Search caveat.** WebSearch quota was exhausted before this round started; DuckDuckGo and Bing blocked scripted queries, and Insta360's blog/manual/forum pages return 403 or render only via JavaScript. So this sweep is built almost entirely from **~95 YouTube tutorials (chapters + transcripts, 2022–2026)** plus Ben Claremont's blog. Written Insta360 sources are cited only where round 1 already had them. Items that could not be sourced at all are in "Not found" at the end.

**Two things to check in the app before building pages.** (1) The spec has no `photo` category: the 360-photo items below are filed under `x5-modes` or `reframe-moves` — consider adding a `photo-tricks` category. (2) Editing recipes are better as `../editing/` how-tos than as effect pages; they're listed in their own table.
