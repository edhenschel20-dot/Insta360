# Oktoberfest practice shoot: 26 Sep 2026

The goal today is practice for Maui, not a perfect film. Shoot short clips on
purpose, then practise editing them at home. Drawn from `settings-presets.md`,
`shot-recipes.md` and `creative-shots-playbook.md`.

## Before you leave
- Charge the batteries and bring a spare or a power bank. PureVideo and a long day drain them fast.
- Clear the card.
- Bring the lens cap. The X5's lenses stick out and scratch easily, so cap it between shots.
- Bring the mini tripod or the stick's own stand, if you have one.
- Wipe both lenses before every shot. A fingerprint smear is the most common reason footage looks bad.

## Let it roll (you're there to enjoy it)
1. **Press record and enjoy the day.** No need to stop and start.
2. **Say "mark"** (voice control) when something great happens: the ride drops, a great float. It's the only habit, and it makes finding moments later much faster.
3. **Try to point a lens roughly at the action.** You'll be on the other side of the camera, and that's fine; you fix the view in the edit. Keeping the lenses facing the action (rather than you side-on) just keeps the action off the stitch seam.

## Carnival (12:00–5:00, bright sun)
**Settings:** Video mode · 5.7K 60fps · EV −0.3 · Standard colour · auto everything else.
Bright sun blows out skies and white rides, and −0.3 protects them. Use −0.7 if a lot of the frame is sky.

Shots to try:
| Shot | How | Edit at home |
|---|---|---|
| **Kids on a ride** | Stand at the rail with the stick planted and still. Let the ride go past you. | **Deep Track: draw the box on your son yourself.** This is the most important skill to practise. |
| **Slow-mo ride** | 4K 120fps. Point it at a swinging or spinning ride. | Slow it down; trim to the best 3 seconds. |
| **The giant** | Camera 20–40 cm off the ground. The boys walk toward it and look down. | 2 keyframes: feet, then faces. |
| **Crane reveal** | Camera at the boys' knee height, then raise the stick overhead over 3 s. Hold before and after. | Keyframe 1 on faces, keyframe 2 looking down at the midway. Ease In/Out. |
| **Fake drone** | Stick fully extended overhead in the busy midway. | Reframe looking down at 45°. |
| **Bullet time** | Bullet Time mode, good light only. Spin on the spot with the stick out, or swing it around the boys. | Nothing; trim only. |
| **Tiny planet** | Stick planted upright on the midway or near the Ferris wheel, boys in a ring. Take a photo. | One-tap Tiny Planet. |
| **Walk-through** | TimeShift mode. Walk steadily down the midway, stick overhead. | Let it speed up; nothing else. |

Rides: follow the operator's rules. Many don't allow loose items. If one does, use a wrist strap and keep the stick short. The ground shots above are safer and usually look better.
If you'll post clips that include other people's kids, check with their parents first.

## Lighted parade (7:00 pm, dusk into dark)
**Settings:** **PureVideo · 4K or 5.7K 30fps · EV −0.7** · white balance Auto · Standard colour.
This is your tip, and the research backs it: the float lights are small bright spots on a dark background, and −0.7 stops them blowing out.

- **Plant the camera; don't walk.** Use a stick on the tripod at the curb, raised about 1.5–2 m so it clears the crowd's heads. The camera stays still and the floats move through the frame. At night, a moving camera plus a slow shutter means blur.
- **One clip per float,** starting just before it arrives and stopping after it passes.
- **Don't use Bullet Time or slow-mo at night.** They need lots of light.
- **Experiment:** shoot the same float twice, once at **−0.7** and once at **0**. Compare them at home. That's how you learn what the setting really does.
- Edit: **skip AI Edit/Auto Frame** (they choose faces over the action). Use a single fixed view pointed down the street (seconds, no keyframes), Deep Track with a box you drew on one float, or Multi-View if you want the parade plus your son's reaction.

## Editing homework (tonight or tomorrow)
Pick 5 clips and use each one to practise one technique:
1. **Deep Track with a box you drew yourself** (kids on a ride).
2. **Two keyframes + Ease In/Out** (crane reveal).
3. **Tiny Planet** (group photo).
4. **Quick Edit: steer by moving the phone** while it plays (walk-through). One pass, no keyframes.
5. **Compare the parade clips at −0.7 and 0.**

Notes on what worked and what didn't go in the practice log. That's the start
of your Maui checklist.
